# Adversarial Idea Review — EXP-022

## Prioritized actionable feedback

1. **Lookahead changes effective step size and coupled weight decay, not only variance (major).** In `proposals/idea-01.md`, every fifth fast trajectory is pulled halfway back to the slow anchor while the literal LR and `1e-4` decay stay fixed. Only half of each five-step endpoint displacement is committed to the anchor, and coupled decay is filtered by the same interpolation. This matters because EXP-008/009 found width-2 sensitive to changing its decay balance, while EXP-012/015 found strong-phase fit easy to suppress. The Lookahead paper supports the fast/slow mechanism and broad `k=5, alpha=0.5` robustness, but not equivalence to this recipe's effective LR/decay under an 80%-at-0.1, 300-second horizon. **Refinement:** add an aligned diagnostic reporting each five-step committed parameter displacement and decay-only displacement versus SGD. Register reduced progress as the main failure mechanism; do not compensate LR or decay inside this experiment, which would confound the test.

2. **Lookahead faces strong local optimizer-safety counterevidence (major, not fatal).** EXP-020 showed that finite, lower-loss Nesterov still caused a candidate-only 96.875% one-class transient at step 11. Lookahead is different and matches control through step four, but the first reset at step five creates a location/momentum mismatch in the same fragile early regime: momentum is retained after weights are copied halfway toward the slow anchor. The proposed 200-batch concentration gate is sensible but not sufficiently diagnostic. **Refinement:** for the first 50 steps, inspect every synchronization and its following update, recording class histogram, update norm, momentum norm, and fast/slow distance against exact-corpus control. That makes a veto attributable to synchronization rather than another opaque optimizer failure.

3. **Channels-last correctly targets the hot path, but both load-bearing premises lack direct support (severe).** `02-system-understanding.md` assigns 97.57% of GPU-stage time to model forward/backward, so the target is real. However, the cited PyTorch/NVIDIA guidance supports layout propagation and chiefly reduced-precision/Tensor Core benefits; its local distillation explicitly establishes no speedup for this tiny FP32 ResNet-20. Even a 3% kernel win must then make 807 extra updates improve an accuracy metric diagnosed as generalization-limited. EXP-013 already showed a plausible H20 exposure idea missing a stable paired gate. **Refinement:** retain the strict timing veto, but first run a cheap warmed FP32 timing pair before building the full semantic harness. If it does not comfortably beat 3%, retire it. If it does, describe production as a test of the unverified exposure hypothesis, not a supported accuracy mechanism.

4. **Channels-last cannot cleanly attribute an accuracy change to exposure (moderate).** The proposal allows small FP32 differences from different cuDNN algorithms, yet the goal uses one seed and a ten-image threshold. Changed reduction order can alter the trajectory independently of extra steps. A 94.25% result would therefore remain a combined layout/numerics/exposure effect even if all timing gates pass. **Refinement:** preserve numerical parity and exposure thresholds but avoid a causal “more steps won” claim. Validate the 19-look cap against the realized epoch schedule, not only simulated 69/70-epoch cases. No hard constraint is violated, but the cap itself changes `train.py` and must remain strictly measurement-only.

5. **CutMix alpha 0.5 does not preserve the effective intervention rate (major).** `proposals/idea-03.md` correctly derives lower label ambiguity and accounts for 32x32 clipping, but endpoint-heavy draws turn many selected branches into near-original or near-full-donor examples. Thus p stays 0.5 syntactically while meaningful regional mixtures become less frequent. The CutMix source attributes benefits to class-bearing regional occlusion; moving mass away from moderate rectangles may remove the signal that made EXP-010 gain 0.60 points. Same-class pairs also show that `2λ(1-λ)` measures label ambiguity, not spatial perturbation value. **Refinement:** report adjusted-lambda band rates—e.g. `(0,.1]`, `[.25,.75]`, `[.9,1)`—and same-class frequency, not only variance and mean ambiguity. Frame this honestly as weaker effective CutMix dose.

6. **CutMix alpha 0.5 runs against both the best result and recorded guidance (moderate).** EXP-010 won despite its 0.35-point lower switch checkpoint, immediately exceeded the no-CutMix first-weak result, lowered NLL, and finished at its best. That does not show the small fit deficit needs fixing. EXP-011 then advised preserving p=0.5 and preferring an orthogonal representation mechanism over more scalar tuning. Alpha differs from the failed p=0.75 point, so this is not a fatal unchanged retry, but the beneficial direction is unsupported. **Refinement:** retain the registered 90.00% switch and 93.16% first-weak diagnostics. If switch fit rises but first-weak or final accuracy falls, conclude moderate rectangles were useful and retire alpha tuning rather than trying nearby values.

7. **Scope and evaluator integrity are acceptable.** All three fit within `train.py`, preserve the evaluator and seed, count real training overhead, and forbid rerolls. Channels-last appropriately addresses extra max-selection opportunities. Lookahead and CutMix should also assert that timing did not create a candidate-specific additional evaluation look.

## Scored verdict

### Lookahead-Wrapped Momentum SGD

- **Evidence and reasoning: 3.5/5** — The NeurIPS mechanism, CIFAR momentum-SGD evidence, and distinction from EXP-018's post-hoc SWA are real; transfer is weakened by the longer paper horizon and unquantified effective-LR/decay and momentum-location effects.
- **Potential impact: 3.5/5** — It changes the noisy strong-phase trajectory throughout training and has more ceiling than a scalar tweak, though this repository's optimizer fragility creates substantial downside.

### FP32 Full-Model Channels-Last

- **Evidence and reasoning: 2.5/5** — Profiling identifies the right cost center and the proposal is rigorous, but source evidence is indirect for tiny FP32 kernels and exposure-to-accuracy is untested.
- **Potential impact: 2/5** — A passing speedup buys only about 3% more updates with no new generalization mechanism; a timing veto or accuracy-neutral exposure gain is more likely.

### CutMix Alpha 0.5 at Fixed Probability

- **Evidence and reasoning: 3/5** — The one-literal intervention, beta math, and EXP-010/011 comparison are clean, but ambiguity is only a proxy and the direction removes moderate events supported by the winning baseline.
- **Potential impact: 2.5/5** — It may recover a little strong-phase fit at no compute cost, but it is a low-ceiling scalar refinement that can dilute the larger existing CutMix gain.

## Named pick: Lookahead-Wrapped Momentum SGD

Lookahead wins because it is the only finalist with a literature-backed mechanism that directly changes optimization throughout the diagnosed short, noisy strong-view phase and offers plausible upside beyond narrow scalar interpolation. It is materially distinct from EXP-018: synchronization feeds the smoothed point into subsequent gradients rather than evaluating a stale endpoint average. Channels-last depends on two speculative links—FP32 speedup and useful extra exposure—while alpha 0.5 weakens an ingredient whose current setting supplied the largest recent gain. Advance Lookahead only with the synchronization-local safety and committed-displacement diagnostics above; a mismatch or concentration veto should retire this exact `k=5, alpha=0.5`, persistent-momentum point without rescue tuning.
