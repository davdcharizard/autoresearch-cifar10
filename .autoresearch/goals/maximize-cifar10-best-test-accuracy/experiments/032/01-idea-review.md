# Adversarial Review — EXP032 Candidate Selection

## Prioritized Feedback

**1. [Momentum reset] The mechanism's own algebra caps impact near the noise floor — this is the load-bearing weakness.** The proposal quantifies the lasting effect as cumulative displacement `≈0.09·b_s`, "comparable to one final strong update of `0.1·b_s`" (idea-01.md §Mechanism, lines 26). So the entire intervention is a *one-step-magnitude, sign-ambiguous* nudge injected at the start of a ~5,400-step weak tail. The proposal honestly concedes this ("Impact risk — high," line 136; "94.25–94.35 is formally positive but remains weak single-seed evidence," line 145). Given the history's best_test_acc spread (94.00/94.02/94.09/94.15/94.22 in `04-results.tsv`), a +0.10 pass from a one-step displacement is barely separable from single-seed variance — the run is information-poor whether it passes or fails. *Path to improve: this is a ceiling limit, not a fixable flaw within the single-seed protocol; the honest framing is that it's a high-safety, low-yield bet whose value is as much the first-weak cosine/direction diagnostics as the gate outcome.*

**2. [Tail-only EMA] Fatal mechanistic headwind — the accepted tail is monotone-improving, so any trailing average pulls backward.** The brainstorm itself notes "EXP010 ends at its best" (line 49), and EXP002 established the annealed trajectory is monotone with final == best (`03-experiment-learnings.md` low-importance pattern). EXP018's uniform tail SWA already failed for exactly this reason (94.02 < 94.15, worse NLL). EMA weights recent iterates more, but if the endpoint *is* the best iterate, every average that includes earlier points is worse. The "distinct hypothesis" claim (learnings line 145) is technically true but does not address the underlying monotonicity that sank SWA. *Path to improve: only worth running if paired with a constant-LR SWA tail (a genuinely different trajectory shape) rather than an EMA over the accepted cosine-annealed tail — otherwise it retries EXP018's mechanism.*

**3. [Tail-only EMA] BN inconsistency is unaddressed and known-harmful.** The plan evaluates shadow weights against *online* BN running stats (brainstorm line 45). Shadow params don't match those stats; EXP018 needed 1,624 BN-recalibration batches to even try. Evaluating averaged weights on mismatched BN is a concrete, predictable degradation the plan waves away as "imperfect." Combined with the EXP029 <1% exposure gate applied to a full 1.07M-param copy every 16 steps, this is high-effort, high-risk, low-evidence.

**4. [CutMix α=0.5] Pure parameter-chasing with no directional hypothesis — reward-hack/noise smell.** The brainstorm concedes "no local evidence establishes the direction" (line 62) and calls it "parameter chasing around a proven alpha-1 point" (line 68). α=0.5 gives a U-shaped Beta → box areas concentrate near 0 or full-image (more extreme label swaps), which pushes *toward* stronger effective mixing — precisely the direction EXP011 (p=0.75) and EXP027 (hard N1/M7) showed over-regularizes the short strong phase. A marginal pass here would be indistinguishable from seed noise and wouldn't survive a benchmark/seed change. *Path to improve: if pursued, pre-register a mechanistic reason to expect α<1 helps (e.g., a measured switch-fit target) rather than sweeping blind; absent that, it's a coin-flip on a proven knob.*

**5. [Momentum reset — strengths worth crediting, not flaws].** It is the only candidate immune to the goal's recurring High-importance failure ("global-LR optimizer-path changes create class-collapse," EXP020/022/028): it runs at LR~0.01, acts once, *deletes* rather than amplifies velocity, never relocates parameters, and fires strictly after the strong phase — so it cannot touch strong-phase class geometry. Placement is correct against `train.py` (eval at 288–296 precedes the transition block at 304–314; optimizer is never recreated, so all 59 buffers persist to the reset point). It is genuinely novel vs history, not a retry. Its literature support is weak (restart-momentum is NAG, not literal SGD-buffer zeroing — line 139), so it is reasoning-driven, not evidence-driven — but the reasoning is rigorous and on-bottleneck (weak-tail generalization, the exact lever EXP030 proved matters).

## Required Gates for the Pick (Momentum reset)

- **Copied-state safety comparison before production**: immutable EXP022 strong corpus (SHA `e04dc2fe…`) + EXP028 weak corpus (SHA `ffefe980…`), 200 strong / 64 weak records, digests recomputed; serialize/fsync the report *before* any veto assertion.
- **Static/algebraic**: exactly one helper + one call in the existing transition block (after eval + weak-loader build, before the first weak update); all 59 buffers zeroed, no other optimizer key/group/scalar mutated; first update matches `w − lr·(g+λw)` and later updates match installed SGD within 1e-7/1e-6; params/BN/gradients/CPU+CUDA RNG bitwise unchanged post-reset; a second call is treated as controller failure (proves exactly-once).
- **Mandatory collapse/spike gates** (per the High-importance recurring failure): no weak step where candidate max predicted-class share >95% while control ≤95% (lower loss cannot waive); no candidate update >25% of pre-update param norm; candidate/control update ratio ≤5; ≤5× own 16-step median; loss-EMA ratio ≤1.5; momentum-diff norm decays per the 0.9 recurrence.
- **Timing/exposure**: no candidate-only CUDA sync added to hide the 59 zero-ops; confirm ~300–301s counted, <600s total, transition near 80% with 8 workers stopped and exactly 59 buffers reset, 45–55% CutMix, no soft targets post-switch, first weak LR ~0.01, ≤1 eval/epoch. Step count ≥~26,629 is a consistency expectation, not a discard trigger.
- **Production**: reconfirm 94.15 baseline @ `7c1e7d8`; only `train.py` tracked-modified; one idle ~98GB H20; single seed-42 run, `timeout 595s`, exit 0, ten-field summary; **no rerun/rescue** (no partial scaling, buffer deletion, per-layer selection, LR change, or boundary shift — each is a new EXP ID).

## Verdict — Pick: **Reset SGD Momentum Once at the 80% Boundary**

It wins on merit despite a modest impact ceiling. It is the only candidate whose reasoning is both rigorous and clean of the goal's dominant failure mode: it attacks the diagnosed weak-tail generalization lever (the same lever EXP030 confirmed is live) while being structurally incapable of the class-concentration collapse that invalidated six prior optimizer/representation attempts. Its honest weakness — a one-step, sign-ambiguous effect near the noise floor — is a *ceiling* concern, not a soundness or safety concern.

The alternatives are weaker on the axes that matter: the EMA idea faces a mechanistic headwind (monotone tail → averaging pulls backward) that already sank EXP018 and is not rebutted; the α=0.5 idea has no directional hypothesis and pushes toward the over-regularization that EXP011/027 showed hurts — a marginal pass would be seed noise, not signal. Between a well-reasoned bet with a low-but-real ceiling and two poorly-supported bets, the momentum reset is the correct EXP032 selection. Proceed, but treat a bare-pass (94.25–94.35) as weak single-seed evidence and weight the first-weak direction/cosine diagnostics as part of the payoff.
