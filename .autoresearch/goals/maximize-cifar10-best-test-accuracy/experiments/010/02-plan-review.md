Below is my adversarial review of the concrete EXP-010 plan. I read the plan, the brainstorm, the idea-review, the idea-03 proposal, the goal definition, the learnings file, the EXP-003/006/007 analyses, and `train.py`/`prepare.py`. Concerns are ordered hardest/most-fundamental first. I flag only problems; sound parts are omitted.

---

### 1. The declared success margin sits at or below the single-run noise floor — a "pass" cannot be distinguished from augmentation-stream luck (reward-hacking-adjacent)
- **Where:** proposal "Testable Hypothesis and Expected Impact" (+0.10 to +0.35), Milestone 3 / Verification step 8 (`best_test_acc >= 93.65`), goal `01-definition.md` (>=0.1pp, no reroll).
- The improvement bar is exactly +0.10pp = **10 test images out of 10,000**. The predicted effect (+0.10–0.35) straddles that bar. Meanwhile the plan perturbs ~40% of plateau steps' inputs, which diverges the *entire* gradient trajectory from EXP-007 — the plan itself concedes (RNG section, risk #10) that "a marginal result cannot support a precise causal effect-size claim." So a run landing at 93.65–93.75 is indistinguishable from run-to-run trajectory noise, yet the protocol forbids replication or reroll to check. Contrast EXP-007, whose +1.25 margin was accepted precisely *because* it dwarfed the stream noise. Here the expected signal ≈ the noise. This is the core validity problem: the plan's verdict machinery will stamp "improvement" on what may be noise, and there is no in-plan safeguard (nor can there be, given one-run/no-reroll). At minimum this should be explicitly acknowledged in the verdict basis so a bare-pass isn't over-claimed as a genuine mechanism win.

### 2. The plan directly executes the action EXP-007's own next-steps recorded as a warning, and its only tripwire is diagnostic-only — a plateau underfit becomes a guaranteed full-run burn with no early abort
- **Where:** EXP-007 `04-analysis.md` line 44 ("do not stack CutMix on the already hard strong phase"); plan Abort Criteria line 42 and Milestone 3 line 20 ("Record a switch checkpoint below 87.08% as compounded-underfit evidence, never as an adaptive retry trigger").
- The learnings file shows the plateau underfits easily: EXP-006 Cutout dropped the strong checkpoint to 83.15% and lost 0.67pp; EXP-008/009 show the width-2 plateau is decay-sensitive. Stacking soft-target CutMix onto N1/M7 for ~40% of a ~240s high-LR window is exactly the compounding those results caution against. The plan's response is a tripwire at 87.08% that is *explicitly forbidden from triggering any action* — so if the strong phase craters, the plan still consumes the full run and the whole H20 budget for a preordained no-improvement. That is defensible only if you accept a wasted run; it is not a mitigation, and the plan presents it as one.

### 3. The soft-target step-loss precedent (EXP-003) is only half-covered — the retention gate bounds throughput, not convergence-within-fixed-time
- **Where:** "Probability-Target GPU Gate" / Verification step 5 (`predicted_step_ratio >= 0.97`); learnings "label smoothing reduced steps 6.7%"; EXP-003 `04-analysis.md`.
- EXP-003 failed for two reasons: (a) it cost 6.7% of steps, and (b) soft targets "lowered loss but not top-1." The plan's ≥97% retention gate addresses only (a). It has no test for (b) — the possibility that dense probability-target training simply converges more slowly per step within ~70 epochs, so 26,329 CutMix steps produce a worse solution than 27,143 hard steps regardless of throughput parity. The plan treats "steps retained" as if it were "quality retained." The gate can pass and the hypothesis still fail for the exact reason EXP-003 failed. The hard-label tail is the intended cure, but nothing in the plan verifies the tail is long enough to erase composite-distribution mismatch (risk #3 is listed but unmitigated).

### 4. The two feasibility preflights are decoupled and never run under real contention, and the GPU retention gate is arithmetically very tight
- **Where:** "Worker Collate Gate" (≥120 batches/s, fresh process) and "Probability-Target GPU Gate" (interleaved steps, separate process); Verification steps 4–5.
- The GPU gate's `ratio = 0.8·(2·t_hard/(t_hard+t_soft)) + 0.2 >= 0.97` requires `t_soft <= ~1.08·t_hard` — the soft step may be at most ~8% slower than the hard step. That is a narrow window the plan never sanity-checks against a rough prior. More importantly, the timer in `train.py:204` (`t0` after the batch is yielded) means CutMix collation cost falls *outside* `training_seconds` but *inside* the 600s wall clock: if worker collation intermittently starves prefetch during the real run, wall time inflates toward the 600s kill even while counted time stays at 300s. The worker gate (120 b/s) and GPU gate are each measured in isolated fresh processes; neither reproduces the actual run's simultaneous 8-worker CutMix collation + pinned H2D + width-2 model + evaluator contention. Passing both independently does not guarantee the joint system stays under 600s, and a decoupled pass is the plan's stated green light to launch.

### 5. The area-correctness and mixed-fraction gates encode version-specific torchvision internals as ground truth
- **Where:** proposal "Installed Torchvision Semantics" (roll-by-one pairing, `lambda_adjusted = 1 - pasted_area/1024`), Verification step 3 ("donor target mass matching pasted area within 1/1024"), Target/Loss Invariants.
- The functional gate asserts donor target mass equals clipped pasted-area fraction to within 1/1024, which hard-codes the assumption that `v2.CutMix(alpha=1.0, num_classes=10)` at torchvision `0.24.1` does per-batch single-lambda, roll-by-one pairing with post-clip area correction. If the installed implementation differs at all (e.g., per-sample lambda, different clipping, different label mixing), the assertion either spuriously fails a correct run or, worse, the "mixed fraction 45–55%" provenance count (`targets.ndim == 2`) still passes while the actual mixing semantics differ from what the hypothesis assumes. The plan leans on these internals for both correctness verification and attribution but does not pin/verify the library behavior independently of its own assertions.

### 6. Reduced plateau steps land specifically on the phase the hypothesis credits for the gain
- **Where:** proposal "Probability and Alpha Choice" (~40% of total steps mixed, all in the plateau) + Fixed-Time Feasibility (26,300–27,200 steps).
- The whole thesis is that CutMix improves the *strong-view representation* built during the plateau. But the ~3% step loss from soft-target CE is concentrated in that same plateau (the hard tail runs at full speed). So the intervention simultaneously adds regularization to the strong phase and removes optimizer exposure from it — the plan cannot separate "CutMix helped representation" from "fewer plateau steps hurt it." Given EXP-004 had far more steps at lower capacity and still lost to width-2, marginal plateau steps may be low-value, but the confound is unaddressed and the plan offers no diagnostic to disentangle it (the strong-checkpoint number is captured but, per concern #2, is action-inert).
