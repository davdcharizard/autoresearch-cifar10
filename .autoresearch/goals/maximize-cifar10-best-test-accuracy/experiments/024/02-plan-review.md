2. **[Milestones 2-3 / Verification steps 4-5] The timed 80/20 workload is not executable from the corpus definition as written.** The preflight defines only 100 “hard” and 100 CutMix batches, while timing later refers to persisted “strong/weak” tensors; a hard-target strong N1/M7 batch is not identified separately from a weak-tail crop/flip batch, and the 800 strong steps do not state the required 50/50 hard/soft mixture. 3. **[Abort Criteria / Verification step 4] The gradient/update veto is post-hoc and undefined.** “An unexplained matched gradient/update norm ratio >2x” does not name the tensor population, normalization (global norm, per-parameter RMS, or per-stage), aggregation across steps, comparison direction, or whether transient first-step values count. Define the exact serialized statistic and threshold before running; otherwise the executor can either reject a viable candidate or waive an adverse signal after seeing it.

4. **[Milestone 4 / Abort Criteria / Verification step 6] The production wall-time and GPU-idle aborts are not hard bounded.** Plain `timeout 600s` sends `TERM` but supplies no `--kill-after`, so a CUDA-hung process can survive beyond the ten-minute hard constraint; “absent GPU activity” also has no sampling interval or grace period and could fire during legitimate evaluation or the loader switch. Add a forced-kill grace and a predeclared inactivity duration that excludes known evaluation/switch windows.

5. **[Verification step 7] The counted-budget necessary condition has no numeric pass range.** “`training_seconds` near the unchanged 300-second budget” leaves a formal condition subjective after the result is known. Predeclare the accepted lower/upper tolerance (including the expected one-step overshoot and one-decimal summary rounding) and distinguish that internal counted-time check from the separate command/`total_seconds <600` wall-time checks.

6. **[Verification step 8] The mechanistic decision rule over-attributes a joint intervention to the shortcut.** A weak first-tail result is declared evidence against the “40%-identity second transition,” but the candidate simultaneously changes final-stage convolutions, BN/channel count, classifier, initialization/RNG consumption, and fixed-time exposure; the chosen proposal itself says this outcome implicates the widened transition *or classifier*. Keep the diagnosis at that joint level unless the controller records a predeclared transition-specific signal that can distinguish shortcut dilution from the other changes.
