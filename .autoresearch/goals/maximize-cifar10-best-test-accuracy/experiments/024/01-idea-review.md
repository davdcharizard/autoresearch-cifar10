# Adversarial Review — EXP-024 Finalists

## Prioritized feedback

**1. Idea 01 (ECA) — the primary systems-risk citation appears misattributed, undercutting its central feasibility argument.** The proposal and brainstorm repeatedly justify the timing gate with "EXP-012 measured 1.233x cost for nine SE gates" (`proposals/idea-01.md:16`, `01-brainstorm.md:54`). But in `04-results.tsv:16` and `03-experiment-learnings.md:94`, EXP-012 is *full-preactivation ResNet-20* (94.22%), not an SE-gate timing probe. The one number the launch-overhead case rests on is either from an unrecorded sub-probe or a wrong attribution. *Fix:* cite the actual source of the 1.233x measurement, or run the paired timing probe first and treat it as unknown until measured — do not present it as established evidence.

**2. Idea 01 (ECA) — mechanism/limiter gap: gating reallocates capacity, but the proven lever here is *adding* capacity.** The diagnosis (`02-system-understanding.md:17`) says the accuracy limiter is generalization under a short strong phase, and the single largest win in the entire history was *adding* channels (EXP-007, +1.25). ECA adds 15 params and only reweights 128 existing channels across 3 blocks — it cannot supply new representational capacity. ECA-Net's evidence is deep ImageNet backbones; transfer to a shallow 3-block CIFAR stage is speculative and the proposal admits it (`idea-01.md:15`). *Fix:* frame the hypothesis honestly as "conditional reallocation," and lower the point prediction — ECA-Net top-1 gains are ~0.3–0.5% on deep nets and will likely shrink here.

**3. Idea 01 (ECA) — the self-imposed timing gate is so tight it may block production before any accuracy signal.** Requiring aggregate ratio ≤1.035 and ≥26,000 updates (`idea-01.md:92`) with five sequential per-block kernels (pool/Conv1d/sigmoid/mul + backwards) on a backward-bound step is a real risk of a no-go verdict. That is good discipline, but it means the *expected* outcome is "invalid on timing," yielding no evidence either way. This is a low-information bet.

**4. Idea 02 (GeM) — engineered so safely that it likely cannot clear a 10-example gate; the ceiling is capped below the target by construction.** With `alpha` init 1/64, capped at 1/8, and the scale ratio *detached* (straight-through-like), the pooled feature can move at most 3.125% at init and 25% at the cap, and the gradient signal driving `alpha` is deliberately frozen (`idea-02.md:48,100`). The proposal itself concedes it "may be too small to move ten CIFAR-10 examples" (`idea-02.md:102`). Given the +0.10 gate (94.25%), this is the crux weakness: maximal safety bought at the cost of nearly all upside. *Fix:* if pursued, justify why a ≤12.5% pooled-feature blend should move ≥10 test examples, or raise the cap with a stronger (non-detached) but still-bounded gradient path — but that reopens the EXP-014 scale risk the detach was added to avoid.

**5. Idea 02 (GeM) — the detached input-dependent scale is an unvalidated gradient asymmetry the proposal cannot defend on impact.** Forward normalization is input-adaptive while its derivative is frozen (`idea-02.md:100`); this is a designed straight-through hack whose effect on representation learning is untested and admitted to possibly "harm representation learning." Combined with weak literature transfer (GeM = retrieval, mixed-pooling ≠ this normalized global endpoint), evidence for *impact* is thin even though evidence for *safety* is strong.

**6. Idea 03 (widening) — main architectural risk (transition dilution) is real and honestly flagged; the extra channels' usability is the open question, not depth.** The second Option-A transition zero-pads 96 of 160 channels, so only 40% of layer3 outputs have an identity path (`idea-03.md:86`). The residual branch *does* produce all 160 via conv1(64→160), so capacity exists — but weaker identity propagation into the widened stage is the thing to watch. This is correctly identified as the primary risk (`idea-03.md:54`), and the falsification logic distinguishes transition dilution (weak first-tail conversion) from a pure generalization miss (`idea-03.md:82`). Sound.

**7. Idea 03 (widening) — the 94.35% point estimate is optimistic; late-only widening has far lower marginal return than the full doubling that produced +1.25.** EXP-007's +1.25 came from doubling *every* stage (width 1→2); this widens one stage 128→160 (+25% in layer3 only). Extrapolating a 94.35% peak (`idea-03.md:9`) from that is a stretch, especially against EXP-012 reaching only 94.22% and the +0.10 gate being ~10 examples. *Fix:* present 94.25–94.30% as the realistic target and lean on the exposure/trajectory diagnostics, not the point estimate, to interpret a marginal result. The trade is nonetheless favorable: +40% params for only ~10% exposure loss (24–25.4k vs 26.9k updates), versus EXP-007 which won at 29% exposure loss.

**8. All three — single-seed, ~10-example resolution makes any bare pass weak causal evidence.** EXP-002 found a best-vs-final gap of 0.01 and best-over-checkpoints carries upward bias (`idea-02.md:85`). All three correctly forbid seed rerolls, but the reviewer should note none of the three can *confirm* a mechanism from one run at this margin — the trajectory/NLL/switch-fit diagnostics each proposes are what separate a real effect from noise. This is a shared limitation, not a differentiator.

**9. None retried a disallowed approach.** ECA is materially distinct from the failed EXP-012/015 residual-suppression family (identity-init, bounded gate, no branch suppression — `idea-01.md:7`); GeM is materially distinct from EXP-014's raw-max branch (bounded, norm-matched, no second classifier — `idea-02.md:40-48`); widening is distinct from EXP-023's depth-cut (preserves all nine blocks — `idea-03.md:13`). No fatal "already-failed, unchanged" flags for any candidate.

| **03 Widening to 160** | **8** — directly executes the documented learning ("preserve depth, test stage-specific capacity"); width is the strongest lever in the history (EXP-007 +1.25); EXP-023's loss cleanly attributed to depth, not width | **7** — real capacity at the cheapest (8×8) stage for only ~10% exposure loss; tempered by marginal late-only returns and transition dilution |

## Pick: **Idea 03 — Depth-Preserving Final-Stage Widening to 160**

It wins on both axes, not on caution. Its evidence is the strongest of the three: it is the exact follow-up the experiment record recommends (`03-experiment-learnings.md:106`), and it isolates the one variable — added capacity — that has produced the largest gain in this goal's history, while EXP-023's counterexample is specifically attributed to depth removal, which this design avoids. Its ceiling is also the highest: adding 40% more parameters where they are cheapest is a proven lever, and the exposure cost (~10%) is far smaller than the cost width-2 already overcame. The two exploratory bets lose on merit, not novelty: ECA only reallocates existing capacity and rests on a misattributed timing figure plus a self-gate likely to abort before yielding data; GeM is engineered so conservatively (α ≤ 1/8, detached gradient) that its own author doubts it can move 10 test examples. Refine Idea 03 by (a) dropping the 94.35% point estimate to a realistic 94.25–94.30%, (b) treating the 96-of-160 zero-padded transition as the primary diagnostic to watch in the first-weak checkpoint, and (c) keeping the trajectory/NLL evidence — not the single best-accuracy number — as the basis for any causal claim at this margin.
