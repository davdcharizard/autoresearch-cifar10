# Adversarial Review — EXP-009 Candidates

## Prioritized Critique (most important first)

**1. [Applies to both scalar ideas — near-fatal for impact] The interpolators sit on a 1-D curve whose endpoints are already measured, and there is no evidence the curve is non-monotonic.** We know 93.55 at `1e-4` (EXP-007) and 93.38 at `5e-4` (EXP-008, `04-analysis.md` §Results). An interpolated point (`2e-4`, `3e-4`) can only *beat* the higher endpoint if top-1 peaks strictly above `1e-4` — i.e., if `1e-4` under-regularizes. Nothing in the history supports that: the incumbent recipe already generalizes at 93.55 with train-loss EMA ~0.04 (not overfit to zero), and every step of added decay in EXP-008 *reduced* top-1. The most likely outcome for `2e-4`/`3e-4` is a value in the 93.4–93.55 band — a no-improvement against the +0.10 gate. *Fix:* only pursue a scalar point if you can articulate a concrete reason `1e-4` is over-fitting at width-2; otherwise treat the scalar lever as near-exhausted.

**2. [Diagnosis inversion — undercuts all three] The goal's own learnings say fit is the limiter, yet every candidate adds regularization.** `03-experiment-learnings.md` Patterns: "Width-2 capacity beats the fixed-time update loss under strong views," and EXP-008's signature was *underfit* (strong checkpoint 90.08→81.29, tail still rising at +0.02/epoch at termination). Width-2 was accepted precisely because more *fitting* capacity under strong views was valuable. Adding norm pressure pushes the same direction that already failed. The brainstorm frames this as an "operating-point tradeoff" but never reconciles it with the finding that the model is fit-limited, not norm-limited, in 300s. *Fix:* state explicitly why a small decay increment would land on the generalization side of the peak rather than deepening the already-observed underfit.

**3. [Unaddressed failure mechanism — horizon, not magnitude] None of the ideas address that EXP-008's tail "was still rising at termination."** The +0.02 pts/epoch endpoint slope (EXP-008 §Results) means the run did not converge — higher decay suppresses fit early and needs *more* epochs to cross back over. In a fixed 300s horizon, any decay > `1e-4` risks the same "peaks after the timer" failure at reduced magnitude. `3e-4` is more exposed to this than `2e-4`. *Fix:* pre-register the crossover epoch estimate; if the projected peak falls after the budget, the point is dead on arrival.

**4. [Selective 5e-4 — mechanism partially self-defeating, admitted in its own risk section] Keeping conv/linear weights at `5e-4` likely reproduces most of the EXP-008 underfit.** The bulk of decayed parameters and norm pressure is in convolution kernels, not BN affine/bias (few params). The idea's own risk note concedes "strong-view underfit may persist." So the intervention may retain the failure it's trying to cure while adding a second uncontrolled change (parameter grouping), muddying attribution. *Fix:* decouple the two levers — test selective grouping at a *moderate* weight scalar (`2e-4`/`3e-4` on weights, 0 on BN/bias) instead of the known-toxic `5e-4`. That keeps the genuinely-supported mechanism (BN/bias should not be decayed — standard practice) while dropping the magnitude that collapsed fit.

**5. [Single-seed, no-reroll near a 0.10 gate — applies to all]** All three bet on a sub-0.2-point effect resolved by one fixed-seed run with rerolls prohibited (`01-definition.md` constraints). A `2e-4` result of, say, 93.58 is inside plausible run noise and would still fail the gate. This makes low-ceiling ideas especially poor expected-value bets. *Fix:* prefer the candidate with the largest, most legible expected effect so the signal clears noise, not the one closest to the incumbent.

**6. [3e-4 specifically is dominated] EXP-008's high-confidence next step already ranks `2e-4` over `3e-4`** ("`5e-4` depressed the strong checkpoint by 8.79 points," §Next Steps). `3e-4` carries strictly more overshoot risk than `2e-4` for the same near-zero upside, and is the same on-curve gamble. It adds nothing `2e-4` doesn't, at higher downside. No refinement rescues it as a distinct bet.

## Scored Verdict

| Candidate | Evidence & Reasoning | Potential Impact |
|---|---|---|
| Coupled WD `2e-4` | **3/5** — cleanest attribution and EXP-008's explicit next step, but the mechanistic case is on a downward-trending curve with no non-monotonicity evidence. | **2/5** — bounded near/below 93.55; most likely no-improvement against the gate. |
| Coupled WD `3e-4` | **2/5** — same on-curve logic but steeper-response evidence argues against it; EXP-008 explicitly deprioritizes it below `2e-4`. | **2/5** — same ceiling as `2e-4` with more downside; overshoot likely. |
| Selective Weight-Only Decay `5e-4` | **2.5/5** — weakest *local* evidence (BN cause not isolated), but strongest *external* basis (excluding BN/bias from WD is standard, well-supported) and the only candidate off the exhausted scalar curve; partly self-defeating by keeping conv at `5e-4`. | **3.5/5** — only candidate with a credible path above 93.65: retain the NLL gain from strong conv decay while freeing BN affine to recover the collapsed strong-view fit. |

**Pick: Selective Weight-Only Decay `5e-4`.**

It wins not because its reasoning is airtight — it isn't (critique #4) — but because it is the **only candidate that leaves a lever the goal has already effectively exhausted.** The scalar interpolations (`2e-4`, `3e-4`) are interior points of a segment whose endpoints (93.55, 93.38) are both known; absent any evidence of non-monotonicity (critique #1), their expected outcome is a no-improvement, and a research pick should not spend a single-seed run confirming an interpolation that is bounded below its own baseline. The selective idea changes *where* decay acts, giving it a mechanism — BN/bias exclusion, which is textbook-standard and plausibly the actual driver of EXP-008's strong-checkpoint collapse — that could restore fit while keeping the confirmed NLL benefit, i.e., a genuine path over the +0.10 gate. Its ceiling is the highest and is backed by external practice, which the critic rubric credits equally with in-setting proof.

**Mandatory refinement before running (from critique #4):** do not run it at `5e-4`. Set weight-tensor decay to `2e-4`–`3e-4` with zero decay on BN affine and bias — this fuses the only two mechanisms in the candidate set (moderate scalar + selective targeting) and avoids re-importing the conv-weight underfit that `5e-4` alone caused. Pre-register the strong (80%) checkpoint recovering toward EXP-007's ~90% as the decisive early signal; if it re-collapses like EXP-008, kill the interpretation early. If the reviewer insists on isolating one lever, the honest fallback is `2e-4` coupled — but that is the low-ceiling safe play, and its most probable verdict is no-improvement.
