# Adversarial Review — EXP031 Candidate Selection

## Prioritized Feedback

**1. Idea-03 (channels-last) has a mechanism–limiter mismatch that is close to fatal.** The diagnosis is explicit on two separate bottlenecks: the *systems* limiter is backward (75.46%, `02-system-understanding.md:10`), but the *accuracy* limiter is "generalization under a short strong-view phase" (`02-system-understanding.md:17`), and headroom states plainly that "additional exposure has not yet been causally tested at the accepted recipe" (`02-system-understanding.md:21`). Idea-03 accelerates the systems bottleneck to buy more updates, but its accuracy hypothesis rests entirely on "extra accepted-recipe updates raise best_test_acc" (`idea-03.md:22`) — the one causal link the diagnosis says is unproven, and that EXP030 actively undercuts (more tail motion → lower train loss, worse NLL, `04-results.tsv:34`). So it is a *double* conditional: it must (a) clear a ≥3% FP32 timing gate its own risk section says "may be neutral or slower" for tiny 32×32 kernels (`idea-03.md:18,198`), and then (b) win on an exposure→generalization mechanism the local evidence contradicts. *Path to improve:* reframe as a pure infrastructure probe whose success criterion is the timing gate alone (bank the speedup for a future accuracy mechanism), not as a scored accuracy run — but as written for EXP031 it is dominated.

**2. Idea-03's positive result would be attribution-confounded even if it passes.** The proposal concedes different cuDNN FP32 reduction order changes rounding, so this is "a net layout run, not a bitwise counterfactual with only more steps" (`idea-03.md:134-136,202`). A 94.25%+ result therefore conflates layout-induced numerical drift with extra exposure — you cannot cleanly credit the stated mechanism. Combined with #1, this is the weakest candidate.

**3. Idea-01 (momentum reset) has a structurally capped ceiling and an ambiguous sign — its own algebra concedes this.** The omitted inherited term is `mu^k·b_s`, decaying below 1% in ~44 weak steps; the lasting displacement is ~`0.09·b_s`, "comparable to one final strong update" (`idea-01.md:26`). That is the entire upside: one strong-update-magnitude nudge. Worse, the direction is unjustified — the proposal itself notes `b_s` "can align with or cancel the new weak gradient" (`idea-01.md:116`), so the reset is as plausibly neutral or harmful as helpful. There is no positive argument that deleting velocity improves *generalization* specifically; the cited restart-momentum literature is flagged as non-transferable (NAG, not SGD-buffer zeroing, `idea-01.md:139`, `01-brainstorm.md:7`). The point prediction of 94.27% sits 0.02 above the 94.25% gate — a razor-thin, most-likely-null bet. *Path to improve:* pair the reset with a directional prior (e.g., only reset if the boundary buffer is anti-aligned with early weak gradients) to give it a sign — but that is a different, more complex experiment.

**4. Idea-01 is correctly distinguished from the recurring optimizer-path failures — this is NOT a disqualifying retry.** The High-importance recurring failure is "Global-LR optimizer-path interventions destabilize early class geometry" (EXP020/022/028, `03-experiment-learnings.md:89-93`). This intervention is one-time, at LR≈0.01, deletes rather than amplifies/relocates velocity, and never touches the high-LR strong trajectory (`idea-01.md:29-34`). It clears the "already-failed" bar. Its safety design (copied-boundary-state comparison, concentration/update-spike gates reusing EXP022/EXP028 corpora) is the most rigorous of the three and the runtime risk is genuinely negligible. The weakness is impact, not validity.

**5. Idea-02 (max-residual pooling) is the only candidate whose mechanism targets the diagnosed accuracy limiter — and it answers a named open question.** `02-system-understanding.md:32` explicitly asks "Which final spatial aggregation preserves localized CutMix features better than global average pooling?" and EXP010's +0.60 from class-bearing regional mixing (`03-experiment-learnings.md:192-194`) supports that final-map location carries signal. This is direct diagnosis alignment that neither other idea has.

**6. Idea-02's core confound is training-time distribution drift of the effective coefficient.** The scale `s` is calibrated once on *unaugmented, eval-mode, init-weight* activations (`idea-02.md:20-27`), but production runs train-mode BN under N1/M7+CutMix. The "≤10% aggregate perturbation" bound is guaranteed only at initialization; the residual/average RMS ratio can drift arbitrarily during training. The proposal acknowledges this ("not universal normalization," `idea-02.md:94`) but has no in-run gate on it. *Path to improve:* add descriptor-RMS-ratio logging at the switch and terminal evals (diagnostic-only, respecting the no-recalibrate rule) so a drifted coefficient is at least detectable post-hoc.

**7. Idea-02's mechanistic risk: max is area-insensitive and may fight CutMix's area-proportional targets.** A sparse argmax can latch onto the CutMix *donor* patch, misaligning with the soft label (`idea-02.md:45,92`). This is the risk to the hypothesis itself, not just to safety. The load-bearing check is the switch-fit gate against 89.73%/87.08% underfit marker (`idea-02.md:49,86`) — if switch fit drops, the mechanism backfired. This is correctly specified.

**8. Idea-02 is well-distinguished from EXP014 and outside the retired pool-first class.** EXP014 collapsed via an independent zero-init raw-max classifier with 4.10× first-gradient (`03-experiment-learnings.md:147-149`); idea-02 adds no params, uses one shared classifier on a convex descriptor with max-coefficient ≤0.10, and residual `(max−avg)` vanishes on uniform maps (`idea-02.md:33-41`). The retired "pool-first transition shortcut" failures (EXP017/021, `03-experiment-learnings.md:109-111`) are at transition *downsampling*, not final global pooling — different location. The EXP014 precedent is genuinely neutralized. Its real exposure is the representation-change base rate (EXP012/015/017/021/024/025 all failed/blocked) plus two blocking gates (numerical + 99% timing à la EXP029).

**9. Cross-cutting: all three correctly hold evaluation count fixed (≤19) — none reward-hacks the max-metric via extra looks.** Idea-03's explicit 19-cap (`idea-03.md:63-79`) is the cleanest, and idea-02/01 inherit the fixed cadence. No candidate games the measurement. Good.

## Scored Verdict

| Candidate | Evidence & Reasoning | Potential Impact |
|---|---|---|
| **Max-residual pooling (idea-02)** | **6.5** — cleanly distinguished from EXP014, grounded in local CutMix evidence + a named open question; literature is generic and it swims against a representation-change failure base rate. | **7.0** — directly attacks the diagnosed accuracy limiter; highest ceiling — a working aggregation change is reusable and could echo width/CutMix-scale gains. |
| **Momentum reset (idea-01)** | **7.0** — cleanest causal story, tightly tied to the most recent learning (EXP030), correctly separated from the EXP020/22/28 failure class; safety design is strongest. | **4.0** — self-capped to a ~one-strong-update transient displacement with an admittedly ambiguous sign; point prediction barely clears the gate → most-likely-null. |
| **Channels-last FP32 (idea-03)** | **5.0** — rigorous gating, but a mechanism–limiter gap (attacks systems, not accuracy) and generic literature that concedes FP32 uncertainty; result would be attribution-confounded. | **3.5** — double conditional: likely fails the ≥3% timing gate, and even if not, exposure→accuracy is untested and undercut by EXP030. |

**Rationale.** Idea-03 is dominated: it improves the wrong bottleneck for the scored objective and is most likely to burn the slot on an `invalid` timing failure. The decisive contest is 01 vs 02. Idea-01 wins on cleanliness of reasoning and probability-of-a-valid-run, but its upside is structurally bounded by its own algebra and its direction is unargued — its modal outcome is a rigorous null. Idea-02 is the well-reasoned exploratory bet aimed squarely at the diagnosed accuracy limiter and a stated open question, with the highest ceiling and its headline failure precedent (EXP014) genuinely neutralized. Per the mandate to judge on merit and upside rather than caution, the higher ceiling plus diagnosis-fit outweighs idea-01's safer-but-capped profile.

**Pick: Scale-Controlled 10% Max-Residual Global Pooling (idea-02).**

It dominates because it is the only candidate whose mechanism matches the objective's binding constraint — generalization/representation — rather than a secondary state-transition lever (01) or the non-binding systems bottleneck (03). It converts a standing open question into a falsifiable, bounded, no-new-parameter test, and its principal historical risk is defused by design. Idea-01 is the correct fallback if gate-blocking risk must be minimized, but its ceiling is too low to prefer on merit; idea-03 should not be run as a scored accuracy experiment.

**Required gates / confounds for the winner (must hold, no rescue):**
- **Switch-fit gate is load-bearing** — switch accuracy must stay near 89.73% and above the 87.08% underfit marker; a drop means area-insensitive max is fighting CutMix targets (feedback #7). This is the earliest real signal the mechanism helps vs harms.
- **99% exposure / paired-timing gate** — extra `adaptive_max_pool2d` backward must not cost >1% (EXP029 precedent); block, don't shrink `s`, on miss.
- **Add descriptor-RMS-ratio diagnostics at switch and terminal** to detect train-time coefficient drift beyond the init-calibrated ≤10% (feedback #6) — logging only, does not revise `s`.
- **Exact-corpus concentration/update gates on production-distribution batches** (per `idea-02.md:70-74`, and consistent with the EXP015 lesson that safety must use materialized production batches, `03-experiment-learnings.md:58-60`) — no candidate-only >95% class concentration; a low loss cannot waive it.
