# EXP-025 Identity-Gate Amendment Review

**Recommendation: approve the conjunctive gradient tolerance; do not retain bitwise shared-gradient equality as a veto.** The repeated full-controller discrepancy (`max_abs=7.4505806e-08`, `relative_norm=3.74015372e-07`) is plausibly FP32 CUDA accumulation-order noise from the candidate's extra backward nodes changing scheduling/accumulation, not a changed initial function: shared state/RNG and logits are bitwise equal, and the separately warmed deterministic diagnostic recovers exact gradients. `CUBLAS_WORKSPACE_CONFIG` alone also does not make every cuDNN convolution-backward path bitwise reproducible.

Use fixed, pre-registered per-tensor checks `max_abs <= 1e-7` **and** `||candidate-control|| / max(||control||, 1e-30) <= 1e-6` for every finite shared parameter gradient on both hard and soft cases, and report the worst tensor/value rather than stopping at the first mismatch. These bounds are tight (only about 1.34x and 2.67x the observed maxima), not too loose. Do not relax them further, and retain bitwise requirements for shared initial parameters/buffers, CPU/CUDA RNG, unit gates, and initial logits.

**BN buffers still need an explicit exact comparison.** Snapshot and compare every common `running_mean`, `running_var`, and `num_batches_tracked` after each control/candidate training forward; initial equality makes exact post-forward equality sufficient. Also require an eval forward to leave all buffers unchanged. ECA is after `bn2` and the shared forward tensors are bitwise equal, so BN-buffer tolerance is neither necessary nor justified; any mismatch remains a veto.
