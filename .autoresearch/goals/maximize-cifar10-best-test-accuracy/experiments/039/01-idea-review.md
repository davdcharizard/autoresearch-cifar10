# Adversarial Review — EXP-039 Finalists

## Prioritized Feedback (most important first)

**1. [Channels-Last — mechanism/limiter gap, near-fatal for impact] The accuracy causal chain is contradicted by the goal's own diagnosis.** The proposal is an *exposure* hypothesis (+3% speed → +807 updates → +0.10 acc). But `02-system-understanding.md:21` states "additional exposure has not yet been causally tested at the accepted recipe," and the *accuracy* bottleneck is explicitly "generalization under a short strong-view phase," not step count. EXP-007 (`03-learnings` Patterns) *won* the frontier with **70.76% of prior steps**, and EXP-023 kept 87.24% of updates yet regressed. More updates has never been shown to move this metric, and one data point shows the opposite. So even a clean timing win attacks the systems bottleneck, not the accuracy one. *Fix path:* reframe honestly as a systems/feasibility probe whose accuracy payoff is a coin-flip, or pair the speedup with a use for the freed time that targets generalization — but the current single-lever framing overclaims.

**2. [Channels-Last — most-likely concrete failure] The timing gate probably vetoes it before any scored run.** The cited source (`pytorch-channels-last.md`, and the proposal itself, idea-01:22-24) says NHWC gains are for reduced precision / large images, and warns FP32 32×32 must be measured. The most probable outcome is a stable <3% speedup → `INVALID`/no-go after an expensive 7-pair preflight, yielding **zero accuracy information**. That is a low expected contribution to the goal. *Fix path:* run a cheap 200-step layout timing spike *first* as a gate before committing the full preflight machinery.

**3. [EMA — targets a phenomenon measurement says is absent] The best–final gap is 0.01 points.** EXP-002/EXP-010 dense-tail evaluation found best 91.83 / final 91.82 and a monotonic annealed trajectory (`03-learnings` Low-Importance + Patterns). If the online tail is monotonic with final≈best, a recency-EMA is at best a slightly-lagged copy of the final weights; the lag can only hurt during the "rapid terminal refinement" the proposal itself lists as risk #2. Expected value ≤ online best. *Fix path:* none that rescues the coefficient — the prior is the problem, not the decay.

**4. [EMA — reintroduces EXP-018's unaddressed failure mode] BN recalibration.** The recency-weighting argument addresses *why the parameter average* in EXP-018 was biased, but EXP-018 also **reset+recomputed all BN stats (1,624 batches) and worsened NLL** (`03-learnings:179-181`). This proposal does exactly the same full BN reset+refresh (idea-03:29,32) and then *requires* terminal NLL < 0.1934 — with no mechanism for why recalibration helps calibration here when it hurt there. The distinction from EXP-018 is real but partial; the second failure channel is inherited unchanged. *Fix path:* justify or drop the BN reset (e.g., keep online BN buffers), but that undermines the parameter/buffer-consistency argument — a genuine tension.

**5. [EMA — cost against a weak prior] It reserves the last 1% for refresh** and floors exposure at 98% (idea-03:60), spending ~269 real updates for a mechanism with a null-to-negative prior. Highest effort of the three for the weakest expected effect.

**6. [RMS Readout — main flaw, acknowledged] The safe coefficient is plausibly sub-threshold.** With `alpha=1/64`, for a typical dense final map (rms/mu ≈ 1.5–2×) the descriptor shifts only ~1–1.6% per channel; the 10.94% figure is the pathological one-hot worst case, not the expected effect. The author admits this (idea-04:104). Given the metric gate is 10 test images, a ~1% descriptor bias may not clear +0.10. *Fix path:* the proposal correctly forbids post-hoc rescue, so this is an accepted bet — but consider a preregistered pre-scoring diagnostic (already-materialized 512-image train corpus, borrowing idea-03's trick) to check the descriptor actually shifts predictions before spending the scored run.

**7. [RMS Readout — genuinely distinct from failed history, credit due] The intrinsic per-example bounds are the strongest mechanistic argument in the set.** EXP-014 failed on a 4.10× first-gradient raw-max branch; EXP-031 failed because a calibration-only bound drifted to 4.34× per-example (`03-learnings:151-153,187-189`). This method has no argmax, no new params, no init-only calibration, and a *lifetime* algebraic bound with a dense Jacobian floor `[63/64, 71/64]` (idea-04:64-83). It is not a naive retry — it directly cures the documented reason those failed, and it preserves EXP-010's switch fit (near-identity), dodging the representation-suppression failures (preactivation/zero-gamma/pool-first). Safety gates are very likely to pass, so it very likely reaches a real scored measurement.

**8. [Channels-Last — fragility] The fixed-19-look retrofit is a fresh invalidation surface.** Because faster steps change epoch count, the proposal must re-derive 19 evenly-spaced unique evaluation epochs (idea-01:64-70) and prove it in preflight; if thresholds don't land on unique epochs the run is invalid. The brainstorm itself flagged (line 19) that this cadence change may not be comparable to EXP-010's. Reasonable but adds a non-training dependency that can sink the run for reasons unrelated to the hypothesis.

**9. [Channels-Last — not a clean counterfactual, acknowledged] Different cuDNN reductions change the seed-42 trajectory** (idea-01:222), so the result is the net implementation effect, not an exposure counterfactual — meaning even a pass can't attribute the delta to exposure. Honest, but it dissolves the mechanistic claim the whole idea rests on.

## Scored Verdict

| **Short-Timescale Late EMA** | **4/10** — clean half-life math, but targets terminal noise the 0.01 best–final gap says is absent, and reinherits EXP-018's BN-recalibration failure unaddressed. | **3/10** — a lagged copy of an already-monotonic tail; ceiling ≈ null, downside negative, and it spends ~269 updates to get there. |

### Pick: **Intrinsically Bounded Average-plus-RMS Readout**

It is the only finalist whose mechanism attacks the *stated* accuracy bottleneck — generalization / preserving localized CutMix evidence — rather than the systems bottleneck (channels-last) or a phenomenon measurement says is nearly absent (EMA). Its central innovation, an intrinsic per-example algebraic bound with a dense Jacobian floor, is a well-argued, concrete fix to the *specific* reason every prior aggregation attempt (EXP-014/031) collapsed, so it is exploratory without repeating a known failure. Practically, its safety gates are near-certain to pass and its op is near-identity, so it very likely reaches an actual scored result on the diagnosed limiter — unlike channels-last, whose most-probable path ends in a timing veto with no accuracy signal.

The honest caveat: its risk is being *too weak* (sub-threshold 1/64), and this recipe's base rate for representation changes clearing +0.10 is poor. Channels-last is the defensible runner-up **only** if the team weights "cheap pre-scoring falsifiability / don't waste the scored run" above impact — but on the stated criteria (reasoning strength + upside toward the diagnosed limiter), that safety edge doesn't outweigh a mechanism the goal's own diagnosis says won't move the metric. EMA should be dropped: weakest prior and a re-inherited failure mode.
