# Adversarial Review — EXP-030

Read: brainstorm, all three proposals, goal definition, system-understanding, experiment-learnings, results.tsv, and `train.py` (confirmed `ANNEAL_START_LR` is referenced only in the `progress > LR_HOLD_FRACTION` cosine branch at lines 236–238; strong-phase LR is the literal `LR=0.1`).

## Prioritized feedback (most important first)

**1. Idea-01 (75% boundary) fights the single strongest promoted pattern in this goal.** The Medium-importance pattern "Long high-LR exploration followed by low-LR refinement improves this fixed-budget model" (EXP-001/002) is a *monotone-in-more-strong-exploration* result: 15%→80% hold **won by adding** strong high-LR time. Idea-01 does the opposite — it *removes* ~1.3k strong updates. The historical gradient on this exact lever points the wrong way. The synchronization argument (LR drops too, unlike EXP-005) is genuinely distinct and correctly rules out the EXP-005/027 weak-at-LR-0.1 failure mode, but distinctness only means "not already disproven," not "supported." The proposal itself concedes EXP-010 "do[es] not prove the strong representation is mature by 75%." *Path to improve: none within EXP-030 that survives the headwind — this needs positive evidence that 75% suffices before it's worth a scored run.*

**2. Idea-01 has an eval-sampling confound that idea-02/03 lack.** `best_test_acc` is a max over evaluations, and moving `LR_HOLD_FRACTION` to 0.75 starts `dense_tail_due` (line 287) ~5% earlier, adding ~3–4 tail evaluations. More tail checkpoints = more chances to sample a transient high point — a mild upward bias on the metric that is *measurement, not training improvement*. EXP-002's 0.01 best-final gap bounds this small, but it is a real asymmetry: idea-02 and idea-03 hold eval cadence identical to EXP-010 and have no such bias. *Path to improve: hold the number of tail evals constant, which the coupled single-constant design explicitly forbids.*

**3. Idea-03 (p=0.40) has no evidence for its benefit direction and the highest preflight cost for the lowest ceiling.** EXP-011 (p=0.75 loses) only establishes the *upper* side is bad; it says nothing about below 0.50. The proposal admits "no local evidence that accuracy is monotone below p=0.50" and "the accepted point may already be optimal." The intervention is a 10% disagreement band — likely below single-seed resolution (10 test examples). It also directly weakens the exact mechanism responsible for the frontier's +0.60, and carries the heaviest gate burden (exact-corpus safety replay per EXP-019/021 + five paired timing pairs) for a probable null. *Path to improve: only worth it if reframed as the first point of a real sweep, which the sweep track — not a single scored run — is designed for.*

**4. Idea-02's central weakness is directional ambiguity, not attribution.** EXP-010 finishes *at* its best (EXP-002 best-final gap = 0.01). A rising-to-the-end trajectory is used to argue "under-refined, wants more amplitude," but it equally supports "already well-tuned, more amplitude overshoots." This is the genuine coin-flip. The mitigations are real, though: 0.02 is still 5× below the strong-phase 0.1 and converges to the identical `1e-4`, so it's a modest nudge, not a regime change. *Path to improve: the proposal's SGD-recurrence preflight (step 5, verify 2× first-weak displacement) and the first-weak-vs-93.16% diagnostic directly instrument the overshoot risk — keep both as hard gates.*

**5. Idea-02 has a coupled weight-decay confound (acknowledged).** Fixed `WEIGHT_DECAY=1e-4` means doubling tail LR also doubles effective tail shrinkage. A positive result conflates "more gradient motion helped" with "more decay helped." Not disqualifying — the goal is best_test_acc, not mechanistic purity — but it caps the interpretability of a win. *Path to improve: report final NLL vs 0.1934 as the proposal specifies; a gain with worse NLL flags the decay/overshoot pathway.*

**6. All three correctly avoid the recurring class-collapse trap.** None touches the optimizer path (EXP-020/022/028 High-importance recurring failure), so none needs the update-spike gate; idea-03 still (correctly) retains the exact-corpus concentration gate as a cheap insurance. This is sound scoping across the board.

## Preflight gates (winner)

For idea-02, require before the single seed-42 run: (a) schedule audit — LR bitwise-equal through progress 0.8, ≈0.02 only on the strict `>0.8` branch, monotone non-increasing tail, exact `1e-4` endpoint; (b) confirm `ANNEAL_START_LR` is unread on the strong branch (verified in source) so switch accuracy must land ≈89.73% — a material pre-switch drift means unintended scope leakage, invalidate; (c) identical param/buffer/RNG hashes, 1,073,962 params, exposure ≥26,629; (d) **no timing campaign** — both arms share the identical instruction graph (correct call; a timing apparatus would only add noise). No worktree or GPU-kernel gate needed.

## Pick: **Idea-02 (raise `ANNEAL_START_LR` to 0.02)**

It wins on both axes at once. It is the **only** candidate that leaves every historically-protected structure bitwise-intact — the 80% boundary, N1/M7, CutMix p/alpha, momentum, decay, data stream — so it neither retries a failed lever nor fights the promoted long-exploration pattern, and it is the only one whose null result is still cleanly informative. Its mechanism (rising terminal trajectory in EXP-010 + fast tail recovery in EXP-012/026) is a real, untested amplitude gap, and its attribution is the cleanest of the three (zero overhead, one scalar, no eval-cadence or exposure perturbation). Idea-01 loses because its central lever points against EXP-001/002/005 and it introduces an eval-sampling confound; idea-03 loses because it perturbs the known optimum in an unsupported direction for a likely-sub-resolution effect at the highest preflight cost. The honest caveat that keeps idea-02 at 4/3 rather than higher is the directional ambiguity of "already ends at best" plus the coupled-decay confound — but a modest 2× nudge that still converges to the same endpoint is the right-sized bet to resolve it.
