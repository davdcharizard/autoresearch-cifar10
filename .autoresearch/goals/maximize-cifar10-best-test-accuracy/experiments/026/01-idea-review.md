# Adversarial Idea Review EXP-026

## Prioritized Feedback

1. **Exact-Corpus Balanced Mixup/CutMix Retry is the strongest candidate, but its evidence must be described as unresolved rather than cleanly favorable.** EXP-019's `04-analysis.md` records one registered candidate-only greater-than-95% class-concentration veto and a lower candidate loss EMA; the later pass could not clear that veto because forkserver augmentation drift made it a different trajectory. The proposed post-N1/M7, pre-policy corpus plus serialized per-step evidence is exactly the report's prescribed remedy and is materially different from retrying a valid failure. However, `01-brainstorm.md` overstates that the old result was invalid “solely” because evidence was non-replayable: the initial adverse event is still real evidence, merely unattributable and unreproducible. Refine the rationale to say EXP-026 adjudicates that adverse signal on one immutable natural corpus; it does not erase it.

2. **The Mixup candidate's mechanism is a substitution bet, not established complementarity.** `proposals/idea-01.md` correctly keeps the accepted 50% soft-target frequency, but replaces half of the locally successful CutMix events with alpha-0.4 Mixup. The cited CutMix evidence favors CutMix over Mixup in its own setting, while the Mixup paper does not validate this 25/25 composition or alpha under the 300-second N1/M7 recipe. Thus the claimed benefit must come from diversity of interpolation geometry overcoming lost localization-oriented CutMix exposure. Preserve the proposal's honest compound attribution and the fixed alpha/split; interpret a miss as rejection of this combination, not Mixup generally. No hard-constraint violation is present.

3. **The Mixup corpus protocol is rigorous but has one provenance claim to tighten.** Recording the worker CPU RNG state immediately after `default_collate` matches the policy boundary in current `train.py`, and mirroring loader/model/optimizer construction before `iter(loader)` matches its parent-RNG order. Still, worker id/seed and a captured state do not by themselves prove that the recorded batch is the exact production ordinal under asynchronous prefetch. The controller should additionally prove, with per-record source tensor hashes and predicted policy outputs, that the source collator performs no policy draw and that replaying each stored state yields the registered categorical `u` and byte-identical shared hard/CutMix branches. The proposal already specifies these checks; treat them as essential acceptance evidence rather than metadata.

4. **Full-network Ghost BatchNorm has a weak match to the diagnosed regime and a high chance of dying at timing.** `02-system-understanding.md` identifies convolution/BN backward as 75.46% of counted step cost, while `proposals/idea-02.md` replaces each of 19 fused BN calls with two normalization calls plus a full-batch `var_mean` and buffer update. It adds no image-throughput mechanism, so its strict 1.03 timing gate is appropriate but plausibly fatal. Scientifically, batch 128 is not the large-optimizer-batch regime motivating Ghost BN, and CIFAR's spatial dimensions already give each group many statistic samples; the useful noise may be too small to clear 0.10 point even if timing passes. Before selection, a disposable layerwise reference/timing microprobe would be valuable, but do not change group size or scope after observing it.

5. **Ghost BN's custom running-buffer policy is coherent but tests more than canonical Ghost BN.** The two 64-example halves determine training activations, while one separately computed full-128 statistic updates evaluation buffers. That avoids the clear bug of two sequential momentum updates, yet it creates a deliberately hybrid train/eval estimator not directly supported by the cited Ghost-BN result. Upstream activations themselves already depend on ghost statistics, so full-batch buffers cannot remove the distribution mismatch. Keep the direct mathematical oracle, once-per-step counter checks, and weak-tail first-evaluation diagnostic; describe any failure as this group-64 plus full-batch-buffer policy, not Ghost BN broadly.

6. **Strong-phase residual dropout is the least supported candidate and points in the same direction as recurring underfit failures.** `proposals/idea-03.md` cites WRN chiefly for placement, while acknowledging its reported CIFAR-10 dropout rows slightly worsened; the strong-phase-only duration has no direct published support. Locally, EXP-011 showed more strong-phase mixing lowered the switch by 2.91 points, and EXP-012/015 showed identity/residual perturbations suppress short-horizon strong fit. Excluding transition blocks and using `p=0.05` reduces risk, but also makes the effect plausibly smaller than the ten-example gate. If retained as a future experiment, mask validation must distinguish dropout-created zeros from the many pre-existing ReLU zeros (for example by intercepting the generated mask or comparing only known nonzero inputs); a raw observed-zero-rate check would be invalid.

7. **All three candidates preserve formal scope, evaluator, seed, and one-run acceptance, but only the Mixup retry directly resolves a documented open avenue.** Ghost BN and dropout are new regularizers layered onto a recipe whose accepted switch fit has little margin. The exact-corpus retry instead follows EXP-019's explicit reconsideration condition, keeps model compute and the total mixed frequency fixed, and has the cleanest path to preserving the accepted 26,898-step exposure. Its high controller effort is justified by causal attribution; it is not an accuracy reward hack because the corpus is natural, frozen before either arm, and cannot be rematerialized after a veto.

## Winning Idea

**Select Exact-Corpus Balanced Mixup/CutMix Retry.** It wins because it attacks the diagnosed accuracy bottleneck with a distinct, literature-backed interpolation geometry while preserving the accepted architecture, optimizer, schedule, total mixed frequency, and likely exposure. More importantly, it closes a precise experimental-history gap: EXP-019 did not yield a valid result and explicitly required persisted post-transform inputs for reconsideration. The immutable pre-policy corpus, shared gate decisions, byte-identical common branches, and serialize-before-veto discipline make this a causally interpretable new experiment. Carry forward the prior concentration event as adverse evidence and make every provenance check launch-critical; do not weaken the registered intervention or gates.
