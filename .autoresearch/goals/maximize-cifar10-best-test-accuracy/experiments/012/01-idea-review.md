# Adversarial Review — EXP-012 Candidates

Three candidates, all correctly pinned to the accepted EXP-010 frontier (94.15%, need ≥94.25%, +0.10 gate, one seed, 300s budget, representation-quality frontier). None violates a hard constraint or retries a discredited approach. Critique ordered by importance, then scored verdict.

## Prioritized Critique

**1. [SE] Central risk (kernel-launch overhead) is asserted but never measured — the one thing that could kill it is unquantified.** Idea-02 specifies elaborate timing gates (95% floor = 25,553 steps) but presents *no* paired H20 measurement, whereas idea-01 ran an actual paired diagnostic (1.87%, 26,404 steps). This asymmetry matters: on this model a full step is only ~10.8ms and the convs are tiny, so 9 SE blocks adding ~9 global pools + 18 micro-linears + 9 sigmoids + 9 broadcasts is ~40+ extra kernel launches into a launch-bound step. The SE paper's *own* number is a bad omen — 190→209ms (**+10% latency for +0.26% FLOPs**) at ImageNet scale, where convs are far larger and launch overhead is *proportionally smaller* than here. Realistic chance the candidate fails its own exposure gate and never runs. *Fix:* run the paired H20 timing diagnostic **before** selection, exactly as idea-01 did, so the go/no-go is known rather than gambled. This is a feasibility (no-go) risk, not a wasted-run risk — but it should be retired up front.

**2. [Projection] The stated mechanism is largely defused by the proposal's own analysis — expected delta is near zero.** Idea-01 honestly concedes: (a) Option A does *not* delete the skipped representation — every input channel is passed at one spatial phase, and the stride-2 3×3 *residual branch already produces all output channels*, so the "half the channels get no shortcut" framing overstates the loss; (b) 1×1 stride-2 samples the same even phase, so no aliasing/spatial gain; (c) the only real new mechanism is learned channel mixing on the skip path in a **9-block** net where Han et al. (PyramidNet) actively favor zero-pad identities. The supporting evidence (ImageNet ResNet-34, 25.03→24.52 ≈ 0.5pt) is at much greater depth/width/duration with no CIFAR A/B ablation in-paper. Net: a well-measured, low-ceiling bet whose own reasoning predicts it likely lands below 94.25. *Fix:* none that preserves the idea's identity — the ceiling is intrinsic; would only be worth it as a cheap composition later.

**3. [Preactivation] Mechanism does not match the diagnosed limiter, and depth is wrong for the effect.** Idea-03's mechanism is "clean identity gradient path → easier optimization," but this network already optimizes fine (train loss reaches ~0.04; the limiter per EXP-007/010/011 is representation/generalization, not optimization difficulty). The direct CIFAR evidence cited (6.61→6.37 @110L, 5.93→5.46 @164L) is exactly where the proposal admits gains "grow with depth and may vanish at 20 layers" — at ResNet-20, pre- vs post-activation is near-parity in the literature, so this is roughly a coin-flip around baseline needing +0.10 to win. Gap between claimed mechanism and stated bottleneck is the core weakness. *Fix:* the one genuinely representation-touching sub-change is the added final BN-ReLU before pooling (feature conditioning) — if pursued, foreground that as the hypothesis rather than the identity-path story, which this depth won't cash.

**4. [Preactivation] Under-claimed strength: this is the closest to a true paired comparison.** Same param count, same conv count/order, and BN affine init is deterministic (ones/zeros, no RNG draw) → the Kaiming stream is plausibly *identical* to EXP-010, unlike idea-01 (admits RNG shift) and idea-02 (needs a `fork_rng` + `SELinear` marker to preserve it). Cleanest attribution and best exposure retention (~97%+, compute-neutral). This raises confidence in *interpreting* a null but does not raise the ceiling.

**5. [SE] r=16 leaves stage-1 hidden=2, and the composite-descriptor confound is real.** Two hidden units barely gate 32 channels; most of SE's capacity lives in stage 3 (hidden 8), which is acceptable but means the early-stage benefit is thin. More importantly, during CutMix, global average pooling blends two class regions into one descriptor — the gate may learn composite-specific channel emphasis that breaks at the 80% hard-tail switch (the same phase-distribution-shift failure family that sank EXP-011's over-regularization). *Fix (bounded, no rescue-tuning):* keep r=16 as pre-registered, but pre-declare the first-weak-checkpoint vs EXP-010's 93.16% as the specific diagnostic for descriptor-mismatch, so a null is correctly attributed rather than blamed on capacity.

**6. [All three] +0.10 on one seed = ~10 test examples; every candidate's honest lower bound sits *at* the gate.** All three predict ranges whose floor is exactly 94.25. None is disqualifying (protocol forbids rerolls and they all respect it), but it means the pick should be driven by *ceiling × mechanism-fit*, not by which floor looks safest — a bare pass is weak causal evidence for all.

## Scored Verdict

**Identity-Initialized Squeeze-Excitation (idea-02)**
- Evidence/reasoning: **4/5** — portable SE evidence across architectures + the sharpest mechanistic tie to the *actual* recipe (channel selection on a wide model under two-region CutMix); docked because the decisive timing risk is unmeasured.
- Potential impact: **5/5** — adds a qualitatively new capability (the model currently has zero channel-selection mechanism) squarely on the diagnosed representation-quality frontier; highest ceiling (~0.1–0.4).

**Option-B Projection Shortcuts (idea-01)**
- Evidence/reasoning: **4/5** — direct A/B evidence + a real paired H20 measurement (best-supported feasibility); but transfer to shallow CIFAR is weak and countered by PyramidNet.
- Potential impact: **2/5** — proposal's own analysis defuses the mechanism; expected delta near zero in a 9-block net.

**Canonical Full Preactivation (idea-03)**
- Evidence/reasoning: **3/5** — direct CIFAR evidence but at 5–50× the depth; cleanest attribution/exposure, yet mechanism targets optimization, not the actual (generalization) limiter.
- Potential impact: **2/5** — near-parity expected at 20 layers; effectively a coin flip needing +0.10.

### Pick: **Identity-Initialized Squeeze-Excitation (idea-02)**

It wins on the axis the history says matters. EXP-007–011 establish that scalar/regularization levers are exhausted and that *representation capacity* is where gains live (width = biggest jump; CutMix complemented it; p-tuning and decay-tuning failed). SE is the only candidate that installs a genuinely new representational mechanism — per-sample channel recalibration — and its plausibility is specifically amplified by the accepted CutMix (two class-bearing regions the gate can arbitrate). Its identity initialization (`2·sigmoid`, zero-expand) makes the run start *exactly* as EXP-010, so downside is bounded, and its RNG-alignment discipline keeps attribution clean.

Projection loses because its own reasoning predicts a near-null on this shallow net despite the nicest measurement; preactivation loses because its mechanism attacks optimization the network doesn't struggle with, at a depth where the literature says the effect vanishes. The one thing standing between SE and a clean win is the unmeasured launch-overhead risk (critique #1) — **selection should be conditioned on running idea-01-style paired H20 timing first**; if it clears the 25,553-step floor, it is the highest-expected-value bet on the board. If timing fails the gate outright, fall back to preactivation as the compute-neutral, cleanest-attribution probe rather than the low-ceiling projection.
