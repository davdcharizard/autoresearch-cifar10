# Report EXP-022: Budget-sized pre-activation Wide ResNet backbone (size-gated)
- **Created**: 2026-06-30

## Goal
Maximize CIFAR-10 `best_test_acc` (%) within a fixed 300s training-compute budget, editing only `train.py`. Baseline **96.38** (EXP-008, commit 07c3760); improvement bar **≥ 96.48** (+0.1pp) AND clearly above a same-session control beyond the ~0.1–0.2pp noise floor, replicated. This experiment tested the first **wholesale-different backbone** after 16 straight within-DavidNet nulls.

## Idea & Hypothesis
Chosen idea (Codex-reviewed 9/10 evidence, 8/10 impact): replace the DavidNet backbone with a **pre-activation Wide ResNet** — the canonical higher-ceiling CIFAR-10 conv net (WRN-28-10 + cutout = 97.1%) — keeping every backbone-agnostic recipe win (EMA, tail flip-TTA, one-cycle, LS 0.2, Cutout12+RandomErasing, bf16/channels_last) and re-adding the twice-validated off-budget torch.compile warmup (+12%) to fund WRN's per-step cost. Hypothesis: the largest budget WRN that anneals at `num_steps ≥ 12610` (130 ep) clears 96.48 and beats a same-session compiled DavidNet control, because WRN's documented under-cutout ceiling lies above the DavidNet family's ceiling that every within-architecture lever failed to breach.

## Approach
Added to `train.py` (sole editable file): `PreActBlock` + `WideResNet` (pre-act basic block, 3 stages of N=(d−4)/6 blocks at 32/16/8, GAP head, no scale_out, no whitening), env-gated (`MODEL=wrn`, `WRN_DEPTH/WRN_WIDTH/WRN_PEAK_LR`), defaulting to the unchanged DavidNet baseline. Re-added the banked off-budget `torch.compile` warmup (separate `train_fwd` handle, local-RNG dummies under bf16 autocast, BN snapshot/restore, no optimizer.step, `cuda.synchronize()` before the budget timer). Plan-review hardening folded in: fixed DataLoader `generator(1234)` so the data/aug stream is identical across cells; smoke measures EMA-inclusive throughput; `num_steps` anneal gate (not the partial-epoch `num_epochs`); WRN LR chosen by **train-loss stability** in the smoke (never test acc); per-epoch recompile monitor. 16/16 correctness smoke checks passed (param counts exact, TTA bit-equal, BN-restore, compile aliasing, global-RNG untouched, DavidNet invariant intact).

## Execution
Three phases, one clean GPU-1 session (no contention retry). (1) **Sizing smoke** (compiled, EMA-inclusive): WRN-16-4 → 190 ep, WRN-22-4 → ~130 ep, WRN-16-8 → 68 ep (disqualified under-anneal). (2) **Peak-LR stability smoke** (60s, past the 0.15 LR peak): both 16-4 and 22-4 stable at peak_lr 0.4 (= the linear-scaled WRN LR 0.1×512/128), no divergence → no LR adjustment needed. (3) **Official same-session triple** via a fresh orchestrator: c0 (DavidNet+compile), cA (WRN-22-4, the most capacity that anneals), cB (WRN-16-4, clean-anneal disambiguator). All trained the full 300.0s, finished < 600s wall, compiled cleanly (warmup 12–14s off-budget; recompile monitor steady 24–26ms, no mid-run recompile). The compiled DavidNet control hit 172 ep — re-validating the EXP-014/021 compile recipe a 3rd time.

## Results
- **Primary metric**: 96.34 (best WRN cell, cB) — baseline 96.38, delta **−0.04pp**. cA (WRN-22-4) 96.31, cB (WRN-16-4) 96.34, vs same-session compiled DavidNet control c0 **96.32**.
- **Observations**: Both WRN cells TIE the control — cA−c0 = **−0.01pp**, cB−c0 = **+0.02pp** (both inside the ~0.1pp noise floor). Crucially the tie is **not under-anneal**: cA reached 12880 steps (≥ the 12610/130-ep gate), recompile-clean, with a healthy early trajectory (ep1 33.8% → ep5 69.2%, no divergence); cB annealed luxuriously at 196 ep / 18988 steps. Neither WRN cell approached 96.48. VRAM remained a non-constraint.
- **Analysis**: The hypothesis is **falsified for budget-sized WRN at 300s**. A wholesale move to a structurally different conv backbone family — pre-activation ordering, multi-block-per-stage depth, GAP readout, no whitening, no scale_out — lands at the *same* ~96.3 as DavidNet, at full anneal, across two sizes spanning 2.75M–4.30M params. The two sizes bracket the anneal/capacity tradeoff (16-4 comfortable, 22-4 near-gate) and both tie, ruling out "wrong size" as the cause. The decisive nuance the reviewer flagged held: the budget-feasible WRNs (≤~4.3M, to anneal in 300s) do NOT inherit WRN-28-10's 97.1% ceiling — that number is a 36.5M net at 200 matched epochs; the higher-capacity WRN-16-8 (10.96M) that might carry more of it under-anneals at 68 ep. So the ceiling is **not specific to DavidNet's topology** — it survives a different backbone family, strongly implicating the *recipe/data/compute regime* (the aug+regularization+eval stack at 300s on CIFAR-10) as the binding limiter, not the architecture.
- **Key Learning**: A wholesale pre-activation Wide ResNet backbone ties DavidNet at full anneal across two budget sizes (96.31/96.34 vs 96.32); the ~96.4 plateau is backbone-family-independent, pointing the limiter at the recipe/data/compute regime rather than the network topology.

## Verification
- **Conditions**: NC1 (within budget, valid metric, <600s wall) PASS; NC3 (anneal gate num_steps ≥ 12610) PASS (cA 12880); NC2 (best_test_acc ≥ 96.48 AND beat same-session c0 by > noise) **FAIL** (cA 96.31 / cB 96.34, both < 96.48 and within noise of c0 96.32); NC4 (genuine method change — prepare.py byte-clean, only train.py, seed 42, LR by train-loss stability not test acc, ≤1 eval/epoch) PASS.
- **Review Notes**: Results confirmed trustworthy — clean uncontended session, fully annealed (NC3 pass), recipe re-validated by the 172-ep compiled control, integrity intact. The same-session control + identical-data-stream design removes the data-order confound; no confirmation pair was needed since neither cell cleared the bar or beat the control.
- **Verdict**: no-improvement
- **Verdict Basis**: valid, fully-annealed result; NC2 (improvement bar) failed — both backbone sizes tie the control.

## Unexplored Avenues
- **WRN + the proven whitening front-end / scale_out**: cA/cB ran clean standard WRN (no whitening). The +0.15pp whitening front-end and the scale_out output trick are backbone-agnostic riders that could be added onto WRN — but they are small and the WRN base already ties, so the expected lift is sub-bar (low confidence).
- **A compile-funded higher-capacity WRN that anneals**: WRN-16-8 (10.96M, the size likeliest to carry WRN-28-10's ceiling) under-annealed at 68 ep. A more aggressive throughput lever (channels_last is on; cudagraphs conflicts with EMA; lower-precision matmul) might fund ~100 ep — but EXP-014/021 already showed extra epochs/capacity don't move THIS recipe, so low confidence.
- **Non-conv backbone** (ConvMixer idea-03, or a conv-stem+ViT hybrid): the most mechanistically-distinct option, still untried. But from-scratch CIFAR transformers/isotropic nets typically underperform CNNs at low epoch budgets, and the present null suggests the limiter is the recipe/data regime, not conv-specific inductive bias — so a new family alone is unlikely to break it (low-medium confidence).

## Next Steps
1. **Pivot OFF the backbone axis entirely — the limiter is now most likely the recipe/data/compute regime, not topology** (high confidence). Two backbone families (DavidNet + Wide ResNet) now tie at ~96.3–96.4 at full anneal. The highest-EV remaining direction is to attack the *generalization ceiling* directly: e.g. a stronger/longer-effective augmentation or label regime that the ~150-ep budget can still absorb, or test-time augmentation richness — levers that change the data/loss the net sees rather than the net. (Note input-aug mechanisms were largely closed EXP-008/011/015, so this means a genuinely new data/loss angle, not a 4th occlusion/mixing/transform aug.)
2. **Cheap confirmation that the ceiling is data/compute-bound, not architecture-bound** (medium confidence): a short literature check on the best *published* CIFAR-10 accuracy achievable at ~150 epochs / single-pass (no ensembles), to quantify how far 96.4 is from the realistic 300s-budget ceiling and whether ANY method clears it — bounding whether the goal is near-saturated.
3. **Whitening-on-WRN rider** (low confidence): only if a quick win is wanted; expected sub-bar given the WRN base ties.
