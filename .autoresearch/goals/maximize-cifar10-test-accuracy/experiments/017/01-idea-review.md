# EXP-017 Idea Review

## Prioritized Feedback

1. **Neutral stage-3 SE has the strongest local premise, but success would not automatically prove input-conditioned feature selection.** EXP-010 and EXP-011 are the only post-baseline architecture probes with positive deltas (94.11% and 94.15%), while EXP-012 and EXP-016 show that compressed refinement and sacrificing early depth fail. That supports preserving `[2,2,2]` and modulating dense stage-3 branches. The hidden attribution risk is that zero-initialized second projections receive first-step bias gradients while the first projections are initially blocked; the gates can first behave as learned, sample-independent channel/residual scales rather than attention. Keep the proposal's identity/state/RNG checks, and preregister training-only diagnostics for per-example gate variance, feature-driven versus bias-driven gate logits, mean displacement from one, and saturation. Record these diagnostically without changing the run or verdict. This is not fatal: even learned channel scaling targets the supported late-feature neighborhood, and the exact candidate remains distinct from EXP-014 because all accepted residual branches are active at initialization. Sources: `experiments/016/proposals/idea-03.md`, `experiments/010/04-analysis.md`, `experiments/011/04-analysis.md`, `experiments/012/04-analysis.md`, and `experiments/016/04-analysis.md`.

2. **Early-window SAM has the largest exploratory ceiling but silently assumes sharpness is the remaining limiter.** The history establishes full fitting, exposure saturation, and failures of several regularizers; it does not measure parameter-space sharpness or show that accepted errors are sensitive at radius 0.05. The proposal itself acknowledges that neither rho nor the 10% window has local calibration, and `knowledge/papers/time-matters-regularization.md` supports early regularization generally, not SAM. Non-adaptive global SAM is also scale-sensitive in this BatchNorm-heavy network, so a few tensors could dominate the perturbation while roughly half the unique high-LR updates in the first 30 seconds are lost. Before scoring, extend the evaluator-free preflight with fixed training-only measurements of first-pass versus perturbed loss, per-layer perturbation-energy shares, and finite/nondegenerate cosine between ordinary and SAM gradients; abort if one layer dominates or the perturbation has negligible/explosive loss effect. Do not tune rho or the window from these measurements. Sources: `experiments/015/proposals/idea-03.md`, accepted `train.py`, EXP-009, and `04-results.tsv`.

3. **Always-on mild RandAugment conflicts with the strongest local temporal evidence.** The saved RandAugment note gives direct CIFAR precedent, and the accepted pipeline lacks its invariances, but it does not support this exact one-op/magnitude-5 policy on the current WRN/mixup recipe. Locally, stronger mixup, CutMix, dropout, and per-example mixup all regress, while EXP-002 specifically shows that removing soft-target regularization for the final 35% enables continued clean-label improvement. Keeping RandAugment active in that tail therefore attacks missing invariance at the cost of the only validated late-refinement mechanism. The proposal correctly discloses this and its worker-RNG/CPU effects, so there is no constraint violation; however, the current always-on treatment should remain behind both alternatives. A future version needs a preregistered worker-safe early-only design rather than interpreting a failure as grounds to tune magnitude or operation selection. Sources: `experiments/016/proposals/idea-02.md`, `knowledge/papers/randaugment.md`, `knowledge/papers/time-matters-regularization.md`, EXP-002/003/005/006/015, and accepted `train.py`.

4. **No finalist has a fatal hard-constraint or reward-hacking issue as written.** Each confines source changes to `train.py`, preserves the fixed seed/evaluator/cadence, uses one local H20, and has explicit throughput, wall-time, semantic, and single-run gates. The proposed training-only diagnostics must remain evaluator-free and must not become adaptive hyperparameter selection.

## Scored Verdict

| Candidate | Strength of evidence and reasoning | Potential impact |
|---|---|---|
| Neutral stage-3 squeeze-and-excitation | **8.5/10** - Two independent positive late-capacity results support the placement, failed compression/reallocation results sharpen the design, and identity initialization plus exact RNG preservation isolate the new controller well; only the leap from capacity to conditional selection remains unverified. | **8.0/10** - It can add useful example-dependent feature selection at negligible arithmetic cost and plausibly clear the 0.10-point bar, though redundancy or bias-dominated scaling may limit the gain. |
| Ten-percent early-window SAM | **6.0/10** - The geometry argument is coherent and the implementation controls BN/state carefully, but no local sharpness evidence or cited SAM evidence supports rho 0.05, the 10% window, or global non-adaptive scaling here. | **9.0/10** - If sharpness is causal, this qualitatively new objective could move more than the narrow architecture variants, but its upside is coupled to fewer critical early updates. |
| One-operation mild RandAugment | **6.5/10** - Direct CIFAR literature and an invariance mechanism support it, but the exact strength/window lacks evidence and repeated local additive-regularization failures oppose the always-on stacking choice. | **7.0/10** - Missing invariances can move CIFAR accuracy by several tenths with little GPU cost, but interference with mixup and the clean tail materially caps confidence in this exact policy. |

**Pick: Neutral stage-3 squeeze-and-excitation.** It wins because it is the only candidate whose placement and mechanism are constrained by repeated positive local evidence while preserving every representation component that later negative experiments showed to be necessary. SAM has the higher speculative ceiling, but without a sharpness diagnostic its fixed rho/window is substantially less grounded and pays a real high-LR update cost. RandAugment has broader literature support, yet its always-on form conflicts with both the accepted clean-tail result and the local regularization trajectory. Carry the SE attribution diagnostics into the plan, but keep them observational so the scored treatment remains exactly preregistered.
