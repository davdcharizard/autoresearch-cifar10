# Adversarial Review - EXP-030 Candidate Ideas

## Prioritized Feedback

1. **None of the candidates has direct positive evidence on the accepted EXP-027 learner, so mechanism discipline matters more than apparent simplicity.** The current limiter is boundary quality at near-zero training loss (`02-system-understanding.md`), but EXP-027 already improved the deeper model's loss from EXP-011's 0.2782 to 0.2523 through RandAugment. Do not present either weaker mixup or added drop-path as correction of demonstrated over-regularization; both are exploratory hypotheses and need one-run closure rules.
2. **The label-smoothed bridge consumes the most strongly protected part of the recipe.** EXP-002 gained throughout a 35% hard-label tail, EXP-004 and EXP-020 bracketed the 65% soft-target cutoff with losses at 50% and 75%, and EXP-013's review already downgraded transition smoothing for shortening clean refinement. Epsilon 0.02 and the 65%-85% window are also ungrounded. This is not a scope violation, but it is the weakest candidate; improve it only with independent evidence identifying late overconfidence, not by choosing another epsilon or endpoint from this history.
3. **Alpha 0.1 is a clean one-constant experiment, but its directional premise conflicts with the closest duration evidence.** More endpoint-heavy draws weaken interpolation, while EXP-004 showed that removing regularization during 50%-65% lost 0.16 points and worsened loss. EXP-027 supplies no clear excess-regularization symptom: RandAugment plus depth raised accuracy and reduced the deeper model's loss. If selected, justify it only as closure of the lower alpha bracket and require a valid miss to close immediate strength tuning.
4. **Changing alpha does not preserve the accepted stochastic trajectory even though it preserves seed 42 and batch-shared sampling.** EXP-005 established that alpha-dependent Beta rejection sampling changes subsequent CUDA draws, including permutations. The preflight should verify the intended Beta(0.1,0.1) distribution and unchanged code paths, but the report must describe the result as the alpha-defined fixed-seed process rather than bitwise isolation; no rerun or alternate alpha may follow.
5. **The drop-path rationale over-attributes EXP-011's whole-model loss to `layer3[2]`.** No branch-level evidence shows that this block learned a brittle shortcut, and EXP-027's improved loss indicates the existing augmentation already regularized the composition. Reframe the mechanism as a test of whether sparse early reliance on the added transformation improves robustness, and preregister lower accuracy or higher loss at >=130 passes as rejection of that exact targeted-mask hypothesis.
6. **Drop-path is materially distinct from EXP-006, but the negative prior remains strong.** Masking one complete residual contribution at p=0.05 is narrower than elementwise p=0.10 dropout in every block, yet both stack feature masking with early mixup and preserve no compute. The exact proposal's private generator, eval/p=0 identity, global-RNG invariance, one-block targeting, hard-tail bypass, and >=130-pass timing gate are necessary; any failure of those controls is fatal, and a scored miss must not trigger adjacent-probability tuning.
7. **Best-only acceptance is especially fragile at the new baseline.** EXP-027's best 94.32% exceeded its final 94.22% by exactly the required 0.10-point margin. Every candidate must require `best_test_acc >= 94.42%` and preregister a final-accuracy/loss corroboration before scoring so a maximum over sparse evaluations is not narratively promoted after the fact. This does not replace the goal metric, but it makes the mechanism interpretation auditable.

Advance only the exact `experiments/028/proposals/idea-03.md` treatment: p=0.05 on `layer3[2]` through 65%, private seed 28028 with no rerolls, exact accepted eval/hard-tail behavior, and the fixed semantic and >=130-pass gates.
