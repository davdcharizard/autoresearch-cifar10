# Idea Review EXP-034

## Prioritized Feedback

1. **Batch 512 is the stronger objective-facing bet, but its throughput gate cannot validate the optimizer hypothesis.** `02-system-understanding.md` supports the systems premise: about 98% of isolated step time is forward/backward and the H20 has 98.9% allocation headroom. The required `>=1.10x` complete-body image rate is appropriately strict because anything less would trade away roughly 45% of accepted optimizer, BN, and batch-shared-mixup decisions for limited exposure. Even a pass only proves feasibility, however; linear LR scaling preserves an approximate first-order displacement per image, not Nesterov dynamics, the example-domain momentum horizon, BN update cadence, or coefficient refresh cadence. Keep the four constants indivisible, describe the score as a test of this exact operating point, and prohibit momentum, floor, warmup, or adjacent-batch rescue after either a gate miss or valid score.

2. **The batch-512 rationale must not treat memory headroom or extra passes as accuracy evidence.** EXP009, EXP016, and EXP028 delivered 159.07-171.70 passes and still regressed, while EXP028 directly showed that extra late updates cannot replace essential high-resolution adaptation. The H20 memory figure establishes that batch 512 fits, not that convolution utilization improves or that a larger batch generalizes. The proposal already has the right remedy: balanced current-machine complete-body timing, raw-window/CV disclosure, a real-loader gate, and a sole score only above 146.308096 projected passes. Refine the narrative so the potential gain comes from testing a new large-batch optimization regime with substantially more full-model image exposure, not from exposure alone.

3. **Use a contemporaneous accepted loader control in the batch-512 wall check.** `experiments/032/04-analysis.md` shows that active worker delivery can be unstable even when median loader cost appears affordable. Candidate-only loader epochs compared with the historical 345.3-second wall result leave transient host contention harder to diagnose. Balance fresh accepted and candidate active/inactive loader epochs in the same session, print all windows before assertions, require the existing per-arm `CV <=5%`, and retain both differential and conservative absolute projections below 500 seconds. This strengthens attribution without weakening the no-rerun rule for a stable miss.

4. **Batch 512 changes the metric's sampling opportunity as well as the learner.** Its 97-batch epochs drop 336 examples per permutation instead of 80 and trigger every-fifth-epoch evaluation after 248,320 rather than 249,600 images; greater throughput should also produce more evaluated epochs. These changes are legitimate consequences of the treatment, but a best score could benefit from more evaluation opportunities. Preserve `best_test_acc >=94.42%` as the sole verdict and report unique evaluations, best-final gap, final accuracy, and final loss explicitly; do not infer improved boundary quality from a transient maximum alone.

5. **Alpha 0.1 has excellent attribution but a strongly negative prior and no diagnosed over-regularization symptom.** `experiments/033/proposals/idea-01.md` correctly isolates one constant and distinguishes the weaker side from failed alpha 0.4. Yet EXP002 made alpha 0.2 the strongest standalone gain, EXP004/020 bracketed its 65% duration negatively, EXP005 rejected stronger mixing, EXP015 rejected per-example coefficients, and EXP027 improved both accuracy and loss with the accepted composition. The interaction claim that deeper capacity plus RandAugment may need weaker interpolation is plausible but symmetric: those additions may instead make alpha 0.2 more necessary. Keep alpha 0.1 framed as a low-confidence family-closure experiment, not a likely improvement, and do not let its implementation simplicity substitute for evidence.

6. **Alpha 0.1's fixed-seed CUDA divergence limits mechanism inference.** The proposal correctly notes that concentration-dependent Beta rejection changes later coefficient and permutation draws. That is part of the treatment rather than a seed reroll, but a ten-example gain cannot separate average regularization benefit from this deterministic realization. Retain the distribution and reproducibility probes, one-run rule, final-accuracy/loss corroboration, and normal-exposure requirement; state that even a valid win advances the fixed protocol but does not establish a continuous alpha optimum or authorize an adjacent sweep.

7. **Neither candidate violates the hard constraints, and both have appropriately fail-closed scopes.** Batch 512 needs the stricter feasibility burden because it changes graph shape and cadence; alpha 0.1 needs only semantic/distribution and complete-step retention checks because the loader and tensor shapes are source-identical. Do not combine the candidates: doing so would lose attribution and make a miss uninterpretable.

## Pick

**Batch 512 With a Fully Scaled LR Curve.** It wins because it has the larger defensible upside and directly tests unused H20 utilization while retaining every model parameter and full-gradient path. The `>=1.10x` image-rate / `>=146.308096` pass gate is stringent enough that a score is earned only when the systems benefit is material relative to the loss of update cadence. Alpha 0.1 is cleaner, but it is primarily closure value in a uniformly negative local neighborhood. Advance only the exact `512, 0.4 -> 0.004, 32000-cap` treatment, strengthen loader timing with a contemporaneous accepted control, and treat any score as evidence for that indivisible fixed-seed operating point rather than for large-batch equivalence in general.
