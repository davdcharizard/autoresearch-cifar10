# Report EXP-008: Stronger augmentation — Cutout 8→12 + light RandomErasing
- **Created**: 2026-06-28

## Goal
Maximize CIFAR-10 `best_test_acc` (%, higher is better) within a fixed 300s training budget, editing only `train.py`. Baseline entering this experiment: **96.00%** (EXP-004, commit ae31206). Improvement bar: ≥96.10% (+0.1pp) AND clearly above the ~0.1pp run-to-run noise floor.

## Idea & Hypothesis
Chosen idea (cross-model reviewer's pick over decoupled-weight-decay and a milder 256→320 widen): strengthen train-time augmentation from `Cutout(8)` to `Cutout(12)` (the airbench96 value) plus a light `RandomErasing(p=0.25, scale=(0.02,0.15), value=0.0)`. The diagnosis: the net is **saturated / regularization-bound with a large epoch surplus** — it fits ~142–150 epochs in 300s vs airbench96's 37 for the same ~96%, so ~100 epochs are wasted re-fitting an already-fit train set (overfitting). The classic conversion of surplus epochs into accuracy is stronger regularization, which slows *convergence* (consuming the surplus) so the fully-annealed minimum generalizes better. Crucially, augmentation runs on the CPU DataLoader workers in parallel with the GPU, so unlike the EXP-005/007 capacity adds it does NOT cut the epoch count — sidestepping the recurring under-anneal failure. Hypothesis: best_test_acc rises to ≥96.10% with `num_epochs` held at ~142–150 (throughput preserved) and the early/mid trajectory at or modestly below EXP-004 (ep25 near ~92.6%), the annealed tail closing the gap and finishing higher.

## Approach
Two edits inside the `train_tf` Compose in `main()` (`train.py:205-213`): `Cutout(8)`→`Cutout(12)` and an appended `transforms.RandomErasing(p=0.25, scale=(0.02,0.15), ratio=(0.3,3.3), value=0.0)`. `RandomErasing` is a first-class torchvision transform (no new dependency); `value=0.0` = mean-fill in the std=1 normalized eval space, matching the existing Cutout zero-fill convention. Everything else (ResNet9 architecture, PEAK_LR=0.4, schedule, optimizer, EMA, whitening, batch size, TTA, `forward`, seeds) held byte-identical to EXP-004 for clean single-variable attribution. Milestone-1 smoke confirmed: the exact pipeline produces a finite [3,32,32] tensor; `RandomErasing(p=1.0)` does zero a rectangle (mechanism live); `Cutout(12)` zeroes ≤144 px; and `num_params == 7,784,627` UNCHANGED (augmentation-only ⇒ zero param/architecture delta). Deviation from plan: none. The plan was hardened by a cross-model (Codex) review that fixed 7 concerns (genuineness awk parsing `test_acc:` not the circular `best:`; `num_params` exact `$2` compare; untracked-scope via `ls *.py` since `run.log` is gitignored; and — most substantively — recognizing that the 300s budget EXCLUDES DataLoader-wait, so a worker bottleneck would inflate WALL `total_seconds`, not cut `num_epochs`, making `total_seconds` the primary throughput-free check).

## Execution
One clean run on GPU 1, `timeout 600`, exit 0, wall 447.6s. No retries, no divergence, no adjustments. Training used the full 300.0s and fit **150 epochs / 14480 steps** — in the EXP-004/006 142–150 band. img/s steady ~25.3–25.6k (≈ EXP-004's ~26k); `total_seconds` 447.6 ≈ EXP-004's ~440s.

## Results
- **Primary metric**: **96.38%** (baseline: 96.00%, delta: **+0.38pp, +0.40%**) — peak == final at ep150. This is ~4× the ~0.1pp noise floor — a decisive, clearly-above-noise gain (the largest single-experiment lift since EXP-001's recipe install).
- **Observations**:
  1. **Throughput FULLY preserved — the hypothesis's key prediction confirmed.** num_epochs 150 (vs EXP-004's 142), total_seconds 447.6 (≈440s), img/s ~25.4k — all in their normal bands. The stronger CPU-side augmentation did NOT slow the GPU step OR inflate off-budget wall (no worker saturation). This is the property that distinguishes it from the EXP-005 (131 epochs) and EXP-007 (94 epochs) capacity adds that lost purely to lost epochs.
  2. **Trajectory confirms the mechanism (slower convergence → higher annealed ceiling), NOT under-fit.** ep25 92.31% (vs EXP-004's ~92.6% — modestly below, the predicted "harder-but-not-broken aug" signature), ep50 93.75%, ep100 95.13%, then the annealed tail closes and overtakes: ep147 96.32 → ep150 96.38. The early dip is mild (~0.3pp at ep25), far from the collapse that would signal too-strong augmentation.
  3. **Mild still-rising tail at the end** (ep147 96.32 → ep150 96.38, best==final) — but at a level +0.38pp above baseline. This hints at *additional* headroom (the anneal may not be fully complete for the harder-augmented net), not a failure.
- **Analysis**: The hypothesis is validated end-to-end. The "saturated / regularization-bound with epoch surplus" diagnosis was correct — the net WAS overfitting in its surplus epochs, and converting those epochs into harder training via input-space augmentation lifted the annealed generalization ceiling by a large, clearly-above-noise margin. The two pre-registered risks (sub-noise like EXP-006's TTA; under-fit from too-strong aug) both failed to materialize: the combination cleared the noise floor by ~4×, and the trajectory showed controlled (not broken) convergence. Throughput-free regularization is now the most productive lever class identified for this saturated operating point — it directly exploits the structural fact (4× epoch surplus) that the failed capacity experiments kept colliding with.
- **Key Learning**: On this throughput-bound, regularization-bound net with a ~4× epoch surplus, stronger CPU-side augmentation (cutout 8→12 + light RandomErasing) is a +0.38pp lever that converts wasted epochs into a higher annealed ceiling WITHOUT cutting the epoch count — the first decisive win since the capacity plateau, and a template for further regularization-side gains.

## Unexplored Avenues
- **Push the regularization lever further**: the still-rising tail + a *larger* epoch surplus now (the harder-augmented net used the surplus but may want even more) suggests room to (a) bump cutout to 14 or RandomErasing `p`/area up a notch, or (b) add a SECOND complementary augmentation (e.g. TrivialAugmentWide / RandAugment on the PIL image) — previously deferred as "too aggressive for short budgets," but the +0.38pp headroom and 150-epoch budget make a *moderate* RandAugment now worth testing. Risk: tipping into under-fit (watch ep25/tail).
- **Compose throughput-free levers**: stack the EXP-008 augmentation with the untried **decoupled weight decay** (idea-01) and/or a label-smoothing retune — all zero-epoch-cost, orthogonal regularization-regime changes; the noise floor makes bundling small wins attractive now that we have a strong base.
- **Re-test capacity ON TOP of the new ceiling**: with augmentation now lifting the ceiling, a milder capacity add (256→320, idea-03) might clear the bar where it wouldn't have at 96.00 — but it still carries the under-anneal risk; lower priority than the throughput-free levers.
- **Mild tail-anneal tweak**: the still-rising tail hints the one-cycle may slightly under-anneal the harder-augmented net; a small `PCT_START` or end-LR-floor adjustment is a cheap probe (sweep territory).
