# Adversarial Review - EXP-031 Candidate Ideas

## Prioritized Feedback

1. **The channels-last timing formula is wrong for this time-defined schedule and must be corrected before planning.** `MIXUP_END_FRACTION=0.65` allocates 65% of counted *time*, not 65% of steps. Therefore `0.65 * early_ms + 0.35 * hard_ms` does not predict fixed-time throughput. Use `(0.65 / candidate_early_ms + 0.35 / candidate_hard_ms) / (0.65 / accepted_early_ms + 0.35 / accepted_hard_ms)`, as `idea-03.md` does, then recompute the 1.02/135.67 gates. The current arithmetic-mean gate can advance a candidate that does not actually deliver the preregistered exposure.
2. **Neither systems candidate directly attacks the diagnosed accuracy limiter.** `02-system-understanding.md` identifies near-zero tail training loss and 0.2523 test loss as a generalization/boundary problem. EXP-009, EXP-016, and EXP-028 delivered 159.07, 171.70, and 159.10 passes yet all regressed, so more images are not itself evidence of higher accuracy. Channels-last remains defensible because it preserves the logical FP32 learner more faithfully than those treatments, but its claim should be limited to a cleaner opportunity for extra full-model updates, not an expectation that exposure is sufficient.
3. **Batch 512 is not an optimization-equivalent exposure treatment under the accepted Nesterov dynamics.** Doubling LR while halving steps per image approximately preserves first-order mean-gradient displacement and coupled decay only in a simplified no-momentum view. It does not preserve the momentum horizon in examples, Nesterov's update, BatchNorm updates, or the batch-shared mixup refresh rate; at the 1.10 gate it projects only about 14.3k updates versus 25,978 accepted. `idea-01.md` acknowledges most of these differences, but “exact linear-scaling counterpart” and preservation language should be weakened. Treat this as an indivisible large-batch optimizer operating point with no local positive evidence, not the symmetric resolution of EXP-029.
4. **The batch-512 throughput gate protects images but not the mechanism most likely to fail.** Its 10% image-rate floor still permits roughly 45% fewer optimizer/BN/coefficient decisions, while EXP-028 already showed that many extra images with altered adaptation can lose 0.33 points. No preflight can prove the doubled-LR trajectory is useful; retain the one-run closure and do not repair LR, momentum, floor, warmup, or adjacent batch size after a valid miss. The existing finite-step, worker, wall, and exact four-line scope gates are otherwise sound.
5. **Channels-last must be described as stochastic-stream preservation, not accepted-trajectory preservation.** The proposal correctly notes that deterministic NHWC cuDNN kernels may diverge in low FP32 bits from step one. Reword the summary's “same deterministic training stream” to mean the same sampler/augmentation/Beta/permutation decisions for an equal step prefix. The independent `git show` oracle, logical-state equality, bounded cross-layout error, exact candidate self-replay, FP32 audit, evaluator-entry check, and complete pinned-copy-through-update timing are the right controls.
6. **Alpha 0.1 is exceptionally clean, but the claimed over-regularization direction has no observed symptom.** EXP-027 improved both the extra-block accuracy and loss, while EXP-004/020 bracketed the 65% duration, EXP-005 rejected stronger alpha, and EXP-015 rejected changed coefficient correlation. The new deeper-plus-RandAugment composition makes the weaker-side test distinct, but only as low-confidence family closure. Keep the exact one-line diff, distribution/RNG checks, normal-exposure gate, and prohibition on adjacent alpha/cutoff/seed retries.
7. **Use endpoint and loss consistently as preregistered corroboration, not an unstated replacement objective.** The goal defines `best_test_acc` as primary and requires `>=94.42%`; faster candidates legally create more epochs and sparse evaluations. Projected evaluation count and `final_test_acc` should be reported to expose maximum-over-opportunities effects. If final accuracy is made a hard veto for channels-last or batch 512, state that as an intentionally stricter project rule and apply it consistently; otherwise follow `idea-03.md` and keep final/loss interpretive rather than allowing them to override the primary verdict.

## Pick

**End-to-End FP32 Channels-Last Training.** It wins narrowly because it attacks the measured 98% forward/backward cost without removing gradients, changing capacity, reducing storage precision, or intentionally changing stochastic decisions. That makes any verified speedup a cleaner extension of the accepted learner than batch 512's coarser optimization regime, while alpha 0.1 remains a closure experiment in a uniformly negative neighborhood. Advance only after replacing the weighted-millisecond formula with reciprocal time-phase throughput, recomputing the gates, and preserving the independent-oracle, evaluator-compatibility, exact self-replay, FP32, one-score, and no-rescue rules. If corrected timing misses 1.02x, close exact channels-last without scoring.
