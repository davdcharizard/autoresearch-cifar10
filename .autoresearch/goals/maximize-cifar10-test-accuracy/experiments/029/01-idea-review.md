# Adversarial Review - EXP-029 Candidate Ideas

## Prioritized Feedback

1. **Batch 128 is the strongest untested lever, but its claimed mechanism is broader than "finer updates."** `experiments/028/proposals/idea-02.md` correctly admits that halving batch and the full LR curve also halves the momentum horizon in examples, doubles BatchNorm and batch-shared mixup refreshes, doubles decay applications, and halves the terminal LR. Any score therefore tests one indivisible operating point, not update granularity alone. Preserve the exact four-constant specification and report those competing mechanisms; do not rescue a miss by restoring the accepted LR floor or changing momentum.
2. **The batch-128 lineage is weaker evidence than the brainstorm implies.** EXP-001 introduced batch 256 / LR 0.2 within a large architecture-and-schedule bundle, so it does not establish that reversing the pair helps the accepted `(2,2,3)` plus RandAugment learner. `02-system-understanding.md` does, however, identify boundary quality per backward pass as the limiter and lists this operating point as open. Keep the proposal's direct H20 exposure/update gates and interpret the run as exploratory rather than a locally validated refinement.
3. **The 0.001 terminal floor is the batch proposal's clearest concrete failure mechanism.** EXP-008 found that a zero floor worsened final loss and attributed the miss to inadequate hard-tail update amplitude; halving the accepted 0.002 floor moves in that same direction while twice as many updates only approximately preserves accumulated motion under momentum and decay. This is not fatal because the floor stays nonzero and the full linear-scaled treatment is coherent, but final loss and best-final gap should be preregistered corroboration and a miss must close this exact operating point.
4. **Alpha 0.1 has a clean bracket rationale but does not directly address the diagnosed compute/boundary limiter.** `03-experiment-learnings.md` shows alpha 0.4, altered duration, and per-example coefficients all failed, while alpha 0.2 remains part of the accepted early-invariance interaction. An endpoint-heavy Beta draw may reduce over-regularization, but no accepted-run evidence indicates over-regularization: EXP-027 improved both best accuracy and the deeper model's loss. If advanced later, keep it a strict one-constant test and avoid claiming that symmetric mean preservation implies comparable optimization, since Beta(0.1,0.1) materially changes effective mixing frequency.
5. **The alpha-0.1 upside is probably below the required +0.10 margin.** It changes no exposure and only weakens an already successful early regularizer; every nearby mixup intervention in EXP-004/005/015/020 regressed. The only defensible reason to run it is inexpensive completion of the strength bracket, not a strong expectation of improvement. A valid miss should close immediate mixup-strength tuning rather than invite an intermediate alpha.
6. **Targeted drop-path still attacks a speculative failure mode and faces the closest direct negative prior.** `experiments/028/proposals/idea-03.md` infers a brittle shortcut from EXP-011's 0.2782 loss, but EXP-027 already reduced that loss to 0.2523 when RandAugment was composed with the block. EXP-006 then shows that adding early residual masking to mixup can lose 0.55 points. Restricting `p=0.05` to `layer3[2]` is materially distinct, but there is no evidence the accepted block remains under-regularized.
7. **Drop-path offers neither compute relief nor a likely margin-sized dose.** The residual branch remains fully computed, so the mask only adds overhead, and it affects one of seven blocks on 5% of early examples. That combination can be too weak to clear +0.10 if harmless yet still disturb the precise depth/RandAugment synergy if harmful. If ever advanced, retain the private RNG, exact hard-tail identity, >=130-pass gate, and predetermined closure rule; do not tune adjacent probabilities from the result.

## Pick

**Batch 128 With Fully Scaled LR.** It wins because it is the only candidate that directly tests the system understanding's open question about boundary quality per backward pass while preserving gradients in every stage, the accepted architecture, and the successful depth-plus-RandAugment interaction. Alpha 0.1 is cleaner but has a low ceiling and a uniformly negative local neighborhood; drop-path has a more speculative mechanism and the strongest contrary prior. Advance batch 128 only as the exact four-constant operating point in `experiments/028/proposals/idea-02.md`, with its fixed exposure/update gates and no post-result LR or momentum repair.
