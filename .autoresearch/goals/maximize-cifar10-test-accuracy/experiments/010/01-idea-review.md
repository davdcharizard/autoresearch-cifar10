# EXP-010 Blind Idea Review

## Prioritized Feedback

1. **Advance the selective 160-channel final stage, but treat its throughput estimate as a prior, not evidence.** The candidate has the strongest cumulative case: `experiments/009/proposals/idea-02.md` gives exact topology, parameter, and MAC accounting; the EXP-009 critic assigned it the largest impact score; and `experiments/009/04-analysis.md` explicitly names it the high-confidence next step after BF16 failed despite 12.1% more exposure. It adds 39.0% parameters for 17.0% forward MACs by spending compute at 8x8 instead of widening the expensive 32x32 and 16x16 stages. However, the claimed 90.3% throughput / 128.1-pass point is an affine interpolation from accepted WRN-16-2 and structurally different uniform WRN-16-3 kernels. Keep the preregistered 85% throughput and 120-pass gates, but make the fresh matched H20 preflight authoritative and report realized scored-run exposure even if the projection passes.

2. **The selective-width preflight should cover the production timed region.** The existing proposal's synthetic CUDA-resident inputs omit the pinned-host copies included inside `train.py:211-249`; EXP-009 already identified this gap, then fixed it for BF16. Benchmark accepted `[32,64,128]` and candidate `[32,64,160]` with the same pinned-host-to-device copy, mixup, forward/loss/backward, and SGD path, in balanced order with warmup and window CV reporting. This matters because the fixed 300-second objective rewards the complete copy-plus-step throughput, not convolution MACs. A passed preflight establishes feasibility only; it cannot establish that 120-128 passes are enough to optimize the wider model.

3. **The architecture hypothesis remains exploratory and needs narrow interpretation.** Wide-ResNet evidence supports width generally, not an asymmetric final-stage expansion, and no local result proves that 128-channel 8x8 features are the accuracy bottleneck. The unchanged 64-channel stage may constrain useful features before the 64-to-160 projection, while fewer optimizer steps can leave the added weights less trained. Preserve every accepted FP32 data and optimization choice, reject any result below 94.17%, and interpret a stable negative result with at least 120 passes as rejection of this allocation only, not of capacity or width generally. Do not rescue it with LR, width, or regularization changes after seeing the score.

4. **FP32 fused SGD is currently a mechanism without measured opportunity.** It is attractive because it retains FP32 model arithmetic and attacks EXP-009's unresolved exposure question more cleanly than BF16, but there is no local fused-versus-foreach/default timing evidence. This WRN's roughly 10.8 ms production step is convolution-heavy, so launch savings from fusing updates over only 691,674 parameters may be negligible. Before considering a scored run, require a balanced production-path preflight with identical initialized model/RNG batches and a preregistered material throughput gate; verify finite updates and FP32 parameters, gradients, and momentum buffers. Also describe it as numerically equivalent at the algorithm level, not bitwise identical, because fused operation ordering can differ. Without a clear measured gain, its expected ceiling is too low for the 94.17% gate.

5. **RandAugment has the strongest contrary local evidence and should remain last.** `experiments/009/proposals/idea-03.md` is specific and materially different from CutMix or block dropout, but it still adds regularization to a recipe for which CutMix, stronger mixup, and residual-branch dropout all worsened accuracy at normal exposure. Applying RandAugment throughout the 35% hard-label tail also conflicts with the evidence that this tail is useful for fitting the accepted augmented distribution. Moreover, torchvision `N=1, M=5` is not uniformly mild because AutoContrast, Equalize, and other magnitude-independent choices are not weakened by `M`. The loader preflight can establish wall-time feasibility but cannot repair the weak local accuracy prior; a negative run must end this policy rather than trigger stronger `N/M` variants.

6. **No candidate has a fatal scope or protocol violation.** All can be implemented in `train.py` with existing local dependencies, one H20, seed 42, the frozen evaluator, and at most one evaluation per epoch. Operational preflights are legitimate because they do not inspect test accuracy. For whichever candidate advances, retain the single fixed-seed scored run, the 600-second timeout, and the formal `best_test_acc >= 94.17%` acceptance threshold; lower loss, more parameters, or more exposure cannot rescue a lower score.

## Pick

**Advance the selective 160-channel final stage.** It wins because it is the only candidate with both meaningful accuracy upside and a compute-aware design grounded in the measured failure of uniform widening. FP32 fused SGD should not consume a scored run until it demonstrates material production-path speedup, while RandAugment is opposed by the strongest repeated local pattern. Harden the width preflight to time the complete production copy-plus-training body, keep the 85%/120-pass gates, and preserve the accepted FP32 recipe so the single scored result cleanly tests selective capacity.
