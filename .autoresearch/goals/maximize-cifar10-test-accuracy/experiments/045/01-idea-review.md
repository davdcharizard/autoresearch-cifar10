# Adversarial Idea Review EXP-045

## Prioritized Feedback

1. **Advance the two-transition projection-shortcut average; its implementation contract is internally correct and it is the only finalist with a distinct mechanism.** Accepted `train.py` applies `Conv2d(1x1,stride=2)` to `ReLU(BN1(x))` at `layer2[0]` and `layer3[0]`, so the shortcut really selects phase `(0,0)` from each `2x2` cell. Pooling first produces `[N,32,16,16]` and `[N,64,8,8]`; the unchanged projections at stride 1 then produce `[N,64,16,16]` and `[N,128,8,8]`, exactly matching the unchanged main branches. `layer1[0]` remains the accepted stride-1 `16 -> 32` projection, and identity blocks still return raw `x`, not `preactivated`. The two parameterless pools add no state, initialization draw, optimizer member, or forward/backward RNG use; registering each after its projection preserves all 52 trainable tensor names, bytes, and order. Keep the proposed four-phase impulse oracle, shape hooks, cloned construction-state check, and RNG replay.

2. **The shortcut hypothesis is credible, not diagnosed, and must be interpreted narrowly.** EXP026's `+0.05` invariance near-signal and EXP027's successful invariance-plus-depth composition support phase stability as useful context, but neither measured transition aliasing. The stride-2 `3x3` main branches remain unfiltered, so this does not remove network-wide aliasing; it replaces only the bypass's single-phase sample with a box-averaged sample. The most likely failure is that averaging suppresses fine CIFAR evidence and lowers shortcut variance relative to the learned branch where there is no post-addition BN. Report candidate/accepted shortcut RMS and main/shortcut RMS ratios on fixed fixtures as non-tuning diagnostics, but do not add gain compensation or choose one transition from those measurements. A miss should close only the exact complete two-transition rule as proposed.

3. **The shortcut cost arithmetic is sound as a prior, while the measured exposure gate remains decisive.** The pools emit `32*16*16 + 64*8*8 = 12,288` values/image; roughly 49,152 add/scale operations are about 0.04% of the accepted approximately 120M MACs/image. That comparison understates two extra forward kernels, saved activations, memory traffic, and pool backward, and the accepted 130.304-pass run has only 2.536% retention headroom before 127 passes. The counterbalanced complete-step H20 gate in both early and hard regimes correctly closes this uncertainty. No arithmetic estimate may override a stable timing miss.

4. **Early-only label smoothing has exact dense-target semantics but repeats a finalist already deprioritized before EXP044 and remains redundant with accepted mixup.** For PyTorch's all-class convention, the two integer-label smoothed CEs equal `CE(logits, q)` with `q=0.95*y_mix+0.05*u`: different-class masses are `0.95*lambda+0.005`, `0.95*(1-lambda)+0.005`, and `0.005` elsewhere; same-class mass is `0.955` plus nine masses of `0.005`. Linearity also gives the stated mean-reduced gradient `(p-q)/B`, with no RNG or hard-tail change. The weakness is causal, not semantic: mixup already supplies example-aware soft targets, EXP004/020 and EXP005/035 protect its duration and strength, no calibration diagnosis supports uniform mass, and EXP041 shows an added CE-derived constraint can weaken the pooled-head frontier. `epsilon=0.05` is conventional rather than locally identified. Keep it closed as a valid low-priority one-shot idea; do not advance it over a new architectural mechanism.

5. **SiLU's formula is correct, but this candidate is disqualified by head adjacency and bundles more than “retained negative evidence.”** `z` is nonnegative while signed `W1` can create negative preactivations, so SiLU can transmit moderate negative values that ReLU deletes. Yet signed `W2` already maps positive hidden features to corrections of either sign, and the direct GAP path preserves `z`. SiLU also attenuates every positive activation, changes the derivative at zero from 0 to 0.5, and has a non-monotone negative tail whose derivative becomes negative for sufficiently negative inputs; any result would belong to this whole amplitude/gating/gradient change. More importantly, EXP036 explicitly says activations were not justified by its one successful operating point, calls immediate activation changes an adjacent post-success sweep, and directs later work to preserve the exact ReLU head and seek orthogonal mechanisms. EXP041/042/044 then provide further adverse head-adjacent evidence. Training-only hidden-sign diagnostics could support a future independently diagnosed activation experiment, but they do not justify scoring SiLU now.

6. **All three proposals satisfy the literal hard constraints, and the shortcut proposal has the strongest fail-closed decision contract.** Current `train.py` is byte-identical to accepted `a7c42dc`; each treatment is `train.py`-only, dependency-free, single-H20, fixed-seed, once-per-epoch at most, bounded by 600 seconds, and retains the frozen 300-second counted budget. Interpret “reconfirm baseline” as a source/provenance audit, not another scored baseline run. For the selected shortcut, require one valid candidate score, `best_test_acc >=94.58%` and realized exposure `>=127` conjunctively, with final accuracy/loss descriptive only. Semantic or stable timing failure closes before scoring; a valid score is never rerun; a normal-exposure miss closes the exact two-pool treatment and every listed immediate pooling rescue without claiming all anti-aliasing methods fail.

## Scored Verdict

- **Strength of evidence and reasoning: 4/5.** The code-level phase-selection diagnosis, exact shapes, state/RNG preservation, cost accounting, and local invariance interaction form a coherent case; only direct evidence that shortcut aliasing limits current errors is absent.

### One-Shot Early-Only Epsilon-0.05 Label Smoothing

- **Strength of evidence and reasoning: 2/5.** Its target algebra and temporal isolation are rigorous, but accepted mixup already softens targets, epsilon lacks a local basis, and neither training loss nor prior results diagnose early overconfidence.
- **Potential impact: 2/5.** It is nearly free and could modestly regularize confidence, but adds no information and mostly perturbs an already bracketed early objective.

### Signed Smooth SiLU in the Accepted Pooled MLP

- **Strength of evidence and reasoning: 2/5.** The signed-preactivation mechanism is mathematically plausible, but it is undiagnosed, confounded by positive attenuation and altered derivatives, and directly contrary to EXP036's preserve-the-head guidance.
- **Potential impact: 3/5.** Changing the nonlinear bottleneck can materially reshape the successful residual correction at little cost, but its ceiling comes from disturbing the accepted head rather than an independently supported missing signal.
