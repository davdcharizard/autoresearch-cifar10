### High Importance
- **Budget is TRAINING-time (300s), not steps; eval & startup are excluded; size LR schedule to time** (EXP-001)
  Evidence: experiments/001/04-analysis.md § Execution — training_seconds=300.0, total wall 447.4s (192 evals + 1.2s startup off-budget).
  Implication: throughput (bf16/channels_last/batch) buys more in-budget epochs; key the LR schedule on elapsed training time, not a fixed step count. 10-min wall cap still bounds eval cadence.
- **~0.1pp run-to-run NOISE FLOOR: the time-budgeted loop fits a host-throughput-dependent number of steps in 300s, so the trained model (hence best_test_acc) varies even with the seed fixed** (EXP-006, EXP-004, EXP-005)
  Evidence: identical-training-code runs fit 142 (EXP-004, 96.00), 131 (EXP-005), 150 (EXP-006, 95.93) epochs as shared-host load varied; EXP-006's eval-only change peaked −0.07pp below the EXP-004 baseline despite strictly more eval views. (experiments/006/04-analysis.md § Results)
  Implication: the +0.1pp single-run bar sits AT the noise floor — sub-0.1pp eval-side or marginal training tweaks cannot be distinguished from epoch-count jitter. Favor changes with clearly >0.1pp expected headroom; treat any sub-0.1pp single-run "win" as unproven. Seed is fixed (no-seed-hacking), so this noise cannot be averaged away by re-rolling.

> **Recurring failure:** epoch-costing changes at the fixed 300s TIME budget under-anneal — the #1 failure mode on this goal (capacity EXP-005/007, compute-per-step EXP-013, custom-op fused-kernel cost EXP-016). Pre-measure num_epochs/throughput before committing ANY per-step change.

### High Importance
- **A WHOLESALE-DIFFERENT backbone family (pre-activation Wide ResNet) TIES DavidNet at full anneal — the ~96.4 ceiling is backbone-family-INDEPENDENT, not specific to the DavidNet topology** (count: 1, EXP-022)
  Mechanism: replaced DavidNet wholesale with a standard pre-activation Wide ResNet (pre-act basic blocks, 3 multi-block stages at 32/16/8, GAP head, no whitening, no scale_out), compile-funded, same recipe (EMA/TTA/aug/one-cycle/LS) and identical data stream. Same-session triple: cA (WRN-22-4, 4.30M, 133 ep / 12880 steps ≥ the 12610 anneal gate) 96.31 and cB (WRN-16-4, 2.75M, 196 ep) 96.34 BOTH tie the compiled DavidNet control c0 96.32 (−0.01 / +0.02pp, within noise). The two sizes bracket the anneal/capacity tradeoff (22-4 near-gate, 16-4 luxurious) and both tie → NOT under-anneal, NOT wrong-size. The genuinely higher-ceiling WRN-16-8 (10.96M, the size likeliest to carry WRN-28-10's 97.1%) under-annealed at 68 ep and was disqualified — i.e. the budget-feasible WRNs (≤~4.3M) do NOT inherit the big-WRN ceiling.
  Insight: do NOT keep swapping conv-backbone families hoping for a higher ceiling at 300s — DavidNet and Wide ResNet, the two best-evidenced fast-CIFAR families, both top out ~96.3–96.4 when annealed in budget. The binding limiter is the RECIPE/DATA/COMPUTE regime (aug+regularization+eval stack at ~150 ep on CIFAR-10), not the network topology. Pivot OFF architecture entirely: attack the generalization ceiling via a genuinely-new data/loss/eval lever, or first bound how far 96.4 is from the realistic single-pass 150-epoch ceiling. (WRN + whitening/scale_out riders are sub-bar; a non-conv backbone is unlikely to break a recipe/data-bound ceiling.) See experiments/022/04-analysis.md, knowledge/references/wide-resnet.md.
- **Adding net capacity OR per-step compute/op cost that buys too few epochs UNDER-ANNEALS at the 300s budget — the low-LR tail is truncated before the change pays off** (count: 3, EXP-005, EXP-007, EXP-016)
  Mechanism: the 300s budget is throughput-bound; any capacity add (deepen 4×4 in EXP-005: 142→131 epochs; widen layer2 256→384 in EXP-007: 150→94 epochs) cuts the step count, and since most accuracy lands in the low-LR tail, the net finishes still-climbing below baseline (EXP-007 best==final, monotone rise to ep94). Not capacity-saturation — the capacity is useful but unannealed.
  Insight: capacity placement must respect the capacity-vs-epochs tradeoff. Prefer SMALL capacity steps (256→320, not →384) that lose few epochs; or capacity at the proven full-speed 8×8 stage; or throughput-free levers entirely (eval-side, recipe alignment). Read num_epochs as the first-class diagnostic on every capacity experiment (EXP-004 ref: 142–150; ≤110 = under-anneal). GENERALIZED (EXP-013): the root cause is ANY epoch-costing change at the fixed 300s TIME budget, not just params — tail-only SAM's 2× compute-per-step removed ~26 epochs (150→124) and lost identically, even when the lighter variant fully annealed. Treat compute-per-step cost (SAM, multi-pass losses) under the same num_epochs-first gate as capacity. **RESOLVED (EXP-014): the 256→320 step is NOT under-anneal — it is genuine capacity-saturation.** With torch.compile buying back throughput, 320 annealed at a HEALTHY 143 epochs (vs EXP-007's 94) yet STILL lost −0.08pp vs the same-session control, and the matched compile-only cell proved epochs near 150 are worth ≈0 (+19 ep → +0.03pp). The layer2/8×8 WIDTH axis is exhausted; do NOT re-test 256→320/384 widen. Adding a block (EXP-004) helped once; widening the existing stage does not. EXTENDED (EXP-016): a custom per-step BN op (GhostBN) breaks the fused channels_last BN kernel → ~50% slower all-site → ~75ep (all-site) / 111ep (layer2+3) under-anneal; only layer3-only (3 small-spatial sites, +12%, 133ep) stayed in band. Custom ops that break fused kernels fall under this same num_epochs-first gate. **EXTENDED (EXP-021): capacity-saturation now covers DEPTH too, not just width.** A 2nd ReZero GatedResidual(256) at the proven 8×8/layer2 stage (+1.18M, the airbench 95→96 "extra conv per block" + EXP-004 the only architectural win) was torch.compile-funded to a HEALTHY 152 ep (num_steps 14671 ≫ the 13095/135-ep floor; best≈final, annealed) yet TIED the same-session compiled control: cA 96.26 vs c0 (173 ep) 96.29 = −0.03pp. Both WIDTH (EXP-014, 256→320) and DEPTH (EXP-021, +1 block) at the proven stage, properly annealed via compile, fail to move the metric → the within-DavidNet capacity surface is mapped and FLAT; do NOT add more depth or width anywhere in this backbone. The only remaining direction is a wholesale DIFFERENT backbone.

- **LR-schedule SHAPE (cosine one-cycle decay vs the incumbent linear triangular) is a non-lever — the throughput-free swap ties the same-session control; the strongest-evidence schedule variant moves nothing** (count: 1, EXP-020)
  Mechanism: replaced the linear triangular post-warmup decay with cosine (`lr=PEAK·0.5·(1+cos(πq))`) over the same warmup→0 envelope, single-file env-gated (`SCHEDULE=cos`, `tri` byte-identical baseline), throughput-free (all cells 150 epochs, peak_vram unchanged). LR-trace smoke confirmed the intended shape (cosine spends ~2.8× more of the post-warmup window below 0.05·PEAK). Two same-session pairs: session-1 cA(cos) 96.36 vs c0(tri) 96.04 = +0.32pp did NOT replicate — confirmation cAb 96.39 vs c0b 96.35 = **+0.04pp** (tie); cos absolute (96.36/96.39) never cleared 96.48. The cos cells were stable across sessions (96.36/96.39); the apparent +0.32 lead came entirely from a low session-1 control draw (c0 96.04 vs c0b 96.35) — identical low-control-draw artifact to EXP-016/017/019. Diagnostic cB (cos, PCT_START=0.10) 96.13 < cA → shorter cosine warmup underperforms; 0.15 is the better cosine operating point.
  Insight: do NOT re-test schedule shape (cosine/cyclic/linear permutations) — cosine carried both the internal flag (EXP-012) AND external evidence (MosaicML ~0.5% cosine>cyclic) and still ties, so the schedule-shape axis is closed. The only untried within-shape tweak is cosine + a higher PEAK_LR (cosine holds LR higher mid-run so may tolerate >0.4) — low confidence, almost certainly still ceiling-bound. See experiments/020/04-analysis.md, knowledge/references/lr-schedule-shape.md.
- **Squeeze-Excitation channel attention — the FIRST channel-attention lever — ties the same-session control; the new functional form is redundant on this saturated net** (count: 1, EXP-019)
  Mechanism: SE (GAP→1×1 bottleneck→`2*sigmoid` gate, identity-init via zero-init fc2) in the residual branches, throughput-neutral (cA ~0.97×, 144ep ≥135 gate; the per-block GAP is an async reduction, no CUDA-sync stall). Two same-session pairs: cA(layer2+3) 96.39 vs c0 96.11 = +0.28pp (session 1) did NOT replicate — confirmation cAb 96.31 vs c0b 96.29 = +0.02pp; cA absolute (96.39/96.31) never cleared 96.48. The session-1 c0 (96.11) was a low host draw (c0b drew 96.29), so the apparent lead was a low-c0-draw artifact (same as EXP-016/017). cB (all-3 SE) 96.21 < cA → adding layer1 SE is NET-NEGATIVE (early 16×16/128-ch attention hurts + costs ~5 epochs). Identity-init worked exactly (ep25 ≥ c0, not depressed).
  Insight: do NOT re-run SE at other ratios/placements (layer2+3 r=16 was the best of the swept placements; ratio is negligible-param, breadth hurts). Channel-recalibration cousins (CBAM, ECA) are the same family → very likely also sub-noise here; deprioritize. The ceiling now resists architectural channel-attention as well — confirming the within-DavidNet structural axis is exhausted. SE composes cleanly (throughput-neutral, identity-init) so re-test only as a free rider on a materially DIFFERENT backbone. See experiments/019/04-analysis.md.
- **Anti-aliased downsampling (BlurPool, Zhang 2019) — the FIRST architectural-inductive-bias lever — does NOT beat MaxPool; it loses monotonically with blur strength** (count: 1, EXP-018)
  Mechanism: replaced naive `nn.MaxPool2d(2)` at layer1/2/3 with anti-aliased pooling (fixed binomial depthwise blur, stride 2). The faithful MaxBlurPool (dense max + blur) was THROUGHPUT-DISQUALIFIED (the dense `MaxPool2d(stride=1)` at full res + the original `F.pad(reflect)` cost; zero-pad fixed reflect but max-mode still only 132ep < the 135 under-anneal gate). The throughput-free BlurPool-only form (blur replaces max; 153/146ep, AT/above c0's 150) was tested instead: cA(ks3) 96.23 = −0.08pp vs same-session c0 96.31 (within noise), cB(ks5) 96.16 = −0.15pp — MONOTONICALLY WORSE with stronger blur, fully annealed (best≈final), ep25 healthy (cA 92.41 ≥ c0 92.18, not under-fit). The low-pass discards high-freq detail; no shift-equivariance headroom because RandomCrop(pad4)+flip already train translation-robustness and 32×32 has little aliasing (vs Zhang's 224² ImageNet gains).
  Insight: do NOT re-run BlurPool variants. The downsampling-OPERATOR axis is closed. Minor confound (blur-only also changes max→avg) but the monotonic blur-strength degradation makes a masked anti-aliasing benefit implausible → faithful compile-funded MaxBlurPool is LOW priority (at best a tie). Remaining untried architectural-inductive-bias levers (EXP-018 brainstorm finalists): Squeeze-Excitation channel attention (different functional form), AdaptiveConcatPool head. See experiments/018/04-analysis.md, knowledge/references/blurpool-antialiasing.md.

  Mechanism: EXP-016 layer3-only GhostBN read +0.24pp (96.38 @133ep vs same-session standard-BN 96.14 @149ep) but was throughput-disqualified (custom op breaks fused channels_last BN kernel, ~50% slower all-site). EXP-017 reproduced the mechanism throughput-free via NoisyBN (fused full-batch BN + per-(sample,channel) post-norm jitter, β-untouched), calibrated to the layer3 ghost-equivalent σ=0.033: cA (σ=0.033) tied control EXACTLY 96.14=96.14 @140ep; cB (σ=0.083, 2.5×) only +0.09pp (96.23, within noise, < 96.48 bar). Crucially this session's c0=96.14 == EXP-016's c0=96.14 → the same-host control reproducibly draws ~96.14, so EXP-016's "+0.24" was a weak-c0 draw, not a real benefit.

- **Transform-based policy augmentation (RandAugment) ties baseline as BOTH replacement and addition — input-space augmentation is now saturated across all THREE mechanisms (occlusion / mixing / transform)** (count: 1, EXP-015)
  Mechanism: mild RandAugment(N=1,M=6) added geometric+photometric diversity (the one aug class never tried) in a same-session 3-cell run. cA (replace RandomErasing) 96.34, cB (add on top) 96.36 — both = same-session c0 96.36, none ≥ 96.48, all @ a matched 149 epochs. - **Buying epochs via torch.compile does NOT lift accuracy — the net is anneal- AND capacity-saturated at ~150 ep, NOT epoch-bound** (count: 1, EXP-014)
  Mechanism: torch.compile (off-budget warmup) bought a clean +12% throughput (cell-0 154 ep → cell-A 173 ep, math-equivalent, no recompile leak). Yet cell-A (compile/256, +19 anneal epochs) gained only +0.03pp over the same-session control, and cell-B (compile-funded 320, annealed at a HEALTHY 143 ep) LOST −0.08pp. Two arms, one conclusion: neither extra optimizer-update epochs nor more annealed capacity moves the metric → the ~96.3–96.5 plateau is a genuine GENERALIZATION CEILING for this whitened ResNet-9 at 300s, not an epoch/throughput limit. This DISCONFIRMS the "epoch budget is the binding constraint" framing that motivated the experiment.
  Insight: do NOT spend future loops buying epochs/throughput for THIS architecture (compile, sync-reduction, faster kernels) expecting accuracy — the surplus has no accuracy value here. torch.compile is still worth BANKING as a free +12% headroom (see Patterns) to fund a DIFFERENT base architecture's per-step cost, but it is not a standalone lever. After 8 straight no-improvements (EXP-006→014) the strongest remaining move is a different backbone, not another lever within this one. See `experiments/014/04-analysis.md`.
- **Tail-only SAM (sharpness-aware minimization, loss-geometry / flat-minima) ties-to-LOSES at 300s — its 2× fwd-bwd cost removes ~26 anneal epochs and the flat-minima gain doesn't offset that** (count: 1, EXP-013)
  Mechanism: hand-implemented SAM (ρ=0.05, fp32 ascent, BN-frozen perturbed pass via track_running_stats=False), applied only in the low-LR tail (`progress>=SAM_START_FRAC`) to bound cost. Ran STABLE (no NaN — fp32 perturbation works under bf16) and fired exactly as gated, but both cells lost to the same-session baseline (96.47): cA (final 35%, start 0.65) 96.29 @124ep (best==final, still-climbing → under-anneal); cB (final 25%, start 0.75) 96.18 @132ep (ANNEALED, peaked-then-dipped → under-anneal controlled, still −0.29pp). SAM's 2×-per-step compute converts directly into ~18–26 fewer anneal epochs (150→124/132); on a net where accuracy concentrates in the low-LR tail, that epoch loss dominates any basin-selection benefit. SAME root cause as the capacity under-anneal entry below — epoch-costing change at a fixed TIME budget — arriving via compute-per-step instead of params-per-step.
  Insight: loss-geometry is now exhausted alongside optimizer / eval-TTA / input-aug / regularization-scalars. Do NOT re-test plain/tail SAM at 300s. The published +0.3–1.0pp SAM gains are at MATCHED epochs (and heavier backbones) — irrelevant under a time budget that charges 2× compute as halved epochs. A near-free SAM variant (ESAM sparse-perturb, LookSAM reuse) is the only re-entry, but EXP-013 showed zero positive SAM signal even where it ran AND periodic/sparse SAM degrades accuracy in the literature → low confidence. See `experiments/013/04-analysis.md`.
- **Regularization-SCALAR refresh (weight-decay SHAPING + label-smoothing retune) does not move the saturated net — WD-shaping ties baseline, clean LS 0.1 degrades** (count: 1, EXP-012)
  Mechanism: 4-cell same-session design. Removing wd from BN γ/β + ReZero α (cell-A, LS 0.2) → 96.29 vs same-session cell-0 96.32 (−0.03pp, noise), throughput-free (150 epochs). Mechanism instrumentation FALSIFIED the directional hypothesis: decoupling α made |α| SHRINK (−0.73 vs −1.25), i.e. uniform wd was *inflating* the steady-state |α|, not suppressing the gate — and the halved |α| had zero accuracy cost, so the ReZero gate is not accuracy-limiting at any magnitude. Clean (CutMix-free) LS 0.1 underperformed: cell-C 96.09 (−0.23pp), cell-B 96.16 (−0.16pp) — LS 0.2 is the better operating point; EXP-011's "LS-insensitive" read was masked by CutMix.
  Insight: the regularization-ALLOCATION axis (weight-space wd shaping + target-space LS) is exhausted alongside input-aug, optimizer, eval-TTA, and capacity. Do NOT re-test wd-shaping or LS<0.2. Untested cousin: the conv/fc wd *level* itself (not allocation) was never swept post-EXP-008-aug — low confidence. The only untried levers with ceiling clearly above noise are schedule-shape (throughput-free) and a MILD capacity step (256→320, under-anneal risk). See `experiments/012/04-analysis.md`.
- **CutMix (region-mixing aug) ADDED on top of the existing occlusion aug does NOT stack — only matches baseline (96.40 vs 96.38, +0.02pp within noise)** (count: 1, EXP-011)
  Mechanism: CutMix (α=1, p=0.5 realized ~42%, tail-off 0.85) ran throughput-free (~26k img/s, 142 epochs) and fully annealed, so not under-anneal — a genuine ceiling. It DEPRESSED early convergence (ep25 91.21 vs EXP-008's 92.31, the harder mixed task) but left the annealed optimum unchanged at ~baseline. The over-softening sub-hypothesis (CutMix soft labels + LS 0.2) was tested and rejected: LS 0.2→0.1 lifted ep25 only +0.44pp and LOWERED the ceiling (96.32 < 96.40), so the optimum is LS-insensitive here. CutMix re-regularizes a net already near its regularization-limited generalization ceiling — redundant with Cutout12+RandomErasing.
  Insight: input-space augmentation is approaching saturation on this net at 300s — the FIRST strong aug won big (EXP-008 +0.38) but a SECOND, mechanistically-distinct one ties. Do NOT add a third input-space aug; if pursuing CutMix, test it as a REPLACEMENT for Cutout/RE (not addition) — low-medium confidence. Future regularization should target a DIFFERENT mechanism (weight-space WD-shaping, target-space LS) or loss-geometry (SAM). See `experiments/011/04-analysis.md`, `knowledge/references/mixing-augmentation.md`.

  Mechanism: EXP-009 — airbench's peak LR 0.24 DIVERGES under our long one-cycle (weight-renorm pins ‖conv‖=√out → ~24%/step rotation; airbench tolerates 0.24 only over its 8-epoch no-plateau schedule; our 150-epoch triangular plateau destabilizes BN → collapse to ~random ep25-100, recovery only as LR→0 → 94.11). Faint residual ideas (PEAK_LR_MUON≈0.02+longer warmup; update-scale+decoupled-WD Muon variant) are low-confidence and deprioritized. See `experiments/010/04-analysis.md`.

  Mechanism: the 1.5× widen (+2.21M params, layer2[0] conv at 16×16 + two 8×8 GatedResidual convs 2.25× heavier + layer3 stem 1.5×) plus shared-host contention cut epochs 150→94 (−33%); net still climbing at budget end (ep92→94: 95.79→95.81→95.85, best==final). - **Second ReZero block at layer3/4×4 (deepening the coarsest stage) → −0.10pp (96.00→95.90)** (count: 1, EXP-005)
  Mechanism: two compounding failures — (a) coarse 4×4 capacity went unused (12-conv net never out-accuracied EXP-004's 10-conv net at matched epochs; ep10 81.16 vs 85.19, 4pp BEHIND), and (b) a 4×4/512 conv is wall-clock ~10% SLOWER than a FLOP-equal 8×8/256 conv (cuDNN kernel selection at small spatial size), costing 11 epochs (131 vs 142) → under-anneal.
  Insight: ReZero capacity itself is not discredited (EXP-004 @8×8 still wins) — only the *location*. Add capacity at the proven layer2/8×8 stage (full ~26k img/s, no 4×4 penalty) or widen rather than deepen the coarsest stage. Prefer non-architecture levers (multi-crop TTA) now that layer3-depth is exhausted.

### High Importance
- **torch.compile (default mode) + OFF-BUDGET warmup = a banked, math-equivalent +12% throughput lever for THIS net** (EXP-014)
  Evidence: experiments/014/04-analysis.md § Results — cell-0 154 ep → cell-A (compile/256) 173 ep, same config; warmup_seconds 11.8s (off the 300s budget, before t_start_training), first compiled step 38ms then steady 17–22ms (~27–30k img/s), no in-loop recompile. Smoke verified param-aliasing, BN-restore, eval-boundary, RNG isolation.
  Implication: REUSABLE RECIPE — compile a SEPARATE `train_fwd = torch.compile(model)` handle (leave `model`/EMA/eval uncompiled → zero eval recompile); warm up 3 fwd+bwd at the exact static (512,3,32,32) channels_last/bf16 shape with local-RNG dummies + BN snapshot/restore BEFORE t_start_training so the compile cost is off-budget. It did NOT help accuracy alone (net is saturated, see Failed/Low EXP-014), but it's free headroom to fund a DIFFERENT architecture's per-step cost in a future loop. Use `mode="default"` (cudagraphs/reduce-overhead conflict with fresh per-step dataloader tensors + EMA).
- **Throughput-FREE stronger augmentation exploits the epoch surplus: Cutout 8→12 + light RandomErasing(p=0.25, scale 0.02–0.15, value=0) → +0.38pp (96.00→96.38)** (EXP-008)
  Evidence: experiments/008/04-analysis.md § Results — num_epochs 150 & total_seconds 447.6 UNCHANGED (CPU-worker aug, not GPU), trajectory ep25 92.31 (slightly below EXP-004's ~92.6, harder-but-not-broken) → tail overtakes to 96.38. ~4× the noise floor.
  Implication: this is the LARGEST lever since EXP-001 and is now part of the base recipe (96.38). The net is regularization-bound with a ~4× epoch surplus; convert surplus epochs into accuracy via stronger CPU-side augmentation — it cannot under-anneal (epochs are budget-protected; a worker bottleneck would inflate WALL `total_seconds`, not cut epochs). Stack further throughput-free regularization (more aug, decoupled WD, LS retune) on top; watch ep25/tail for under-fit if pushed harder.
- **DavidNet/ResNet-9 + time-based triangular one-cycle (peak 0.4, mean-loss, wd 5e-4, LS 0.2, Cutout 8, bf16/channels_last) → 95.22%** (EXP-001)
  Evidence: experiments/001/04-analysis.md § Results — 91.57→95.22 (+3.65pp), 192 epochs in 300s, peak VRAM 1.6GB.
  Implication: this is the validated base recipe; compose new ideas (whitening front-end, TTA, wider/deeper) on top of it rather than re-deriving.
- **Weight EMA (AveragedModel, decay 0.998, use_buffers=True) evaluated in place of the raw iterate + flip-TTA gated to final 20% → +0.50pp, ~free** (EXP-002)
  Evidence: experiments/002/04-analysis.md § Results — 95.22→95.72, 183 epochs/300s, wall 442.7s, VRAM 1.6GB; tail step-up 95.28→95.55 at the progress≥0.8 TTA gate.

- **Frozen 3×3/pad-1 ZCA patch-whitening first conv (eigendecomp off-budget, requires_grad=False, excluded from optimizer) → +0.15pp** (EXP-003)
  Evidence: experiments/003/04-analysis.md § Results — 95.72→95.87; early-convergence jump (ep10 85.5% vs EXP-002 81.6%) that survives ~9 fewer epochs (174 vs 183).
  Implication: validated additive front-end, now part of the base recipe; pad=1 preserves 32×32 (pool chain untouched); compute patches in EXACT eval space (mean-subtract std=1) via a LOCAL rng. Stack more depth/width on top.
