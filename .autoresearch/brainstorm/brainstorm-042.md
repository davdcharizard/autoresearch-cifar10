## Web Search & Literature Review

No new live search this loop — the decisive evidence is internal (42 experiments; every identified mechanism class measured) plus established literature already in context. Anchors for the lead direction, with arXiv IDs for verification during planning:

- **Deep Ensembles: A Loss Landscape Perspective** (Fort, Hu, Lakshminarayanan — arXiv 1912.02757): independently-initialized networks converge to DIFFERENT loss-landscape modes; averaging their PREDICTIONS (function space) captures multi-modality that weight-space averaging (SWA/EMA) structurally cannot. This is precisely the dichotomy our own data measured: SWA with correct BN re-estimation landed AT the baseline mean with better loss and zero accuracy gain (EXP-032), and EMA lost (EXP-011) — weight averaging explores ONE mode. Function-space averaging is the half of the dichotomy this program has never measured.
- **Deep ensembles** (Lakshminarayanan et al., arXiv 1612.01474): 2–5 member logit/probability averaging robustly gains ~+0.5–1.0pp on CIFAR-scale classifiers; the gain is a plateau-LEVEL shift (variance reduction of the decision function), not a transit-speed effect.
- **When Ensembling Smaller Models is More Efficient than Single Large Models** (Kondratyuk et al., arXiv 2005.00570): at MATCHED total FLOPs, ensembles of smaller nets beat single large nets on CIFAR-10/ImageNet families — directly the trade we can make under the fixed budget (capacity → multiplicity at ~constant step time).
- **BatchEnsemble** (Wen et al., arXiv 2002.06715) and **MIMO** (Havasi et al., arXiv 2010.06610): the "cheap ensemble inside one network" family — multiple near-independent members co-trained in a single forward pass at ~1× cost, ensembled at inference. MIMO on WRN-28-10 CIFAR-10 gains +0.3–0.5 over the single net. Our grouped-conv design below is the EXPLICIT-separation version of this family (guaranteed member independence rather than learned separation).
- Knowledge base (knowledge/README.md): SWA note (references/swa-stochastic-weight-averaging.md) already records the improved-loss/no-acc-gain signature and the "decision-boundary-limited" diagnosis that motivates function-space diversity.

## Experimental History Review

- **Current best/baseline**: 96.71 @ 1990397 (EXP-006 recipe); bar = 96.81. σ calibration (EXP-027): baseline mean ≈ 96.57, σ ≈ 0.16 — the bar sits +1.5σ above the true mean; candidates need TRUE effects ≥ +0.3.
- **Strategic position**: 36 consecutive non-improvements. As of EXP-041, EVERY mechanism class the program identified has at least one measurement. exp-report-041 Next Steps directs: candidates must come from genuinely novel mechanism construction — recombination of validated parts under the laws — not any standard menu.
- **Binding laws (all measured)**: DEFERRAL (change must be free in early heat, epochs, gradient quality; +1ms dt ≈ −6 epochs ≈ −0.08pp); NUMERICS EQUIVALENCE (EXP-021); MAX-STATISTIC (only converged plateau LEVEL pays; transit speed converts to ZERO — EXP-028/031); GRADIENT-NOISE optimum (batch 512/β0.9 is the measured max — EXP-022/023/024); ABSORPTION (heavy TA+RE absorbs lighter-aug-calibrated regularizers/modules — EXP-035/036/037); EPOCH-BOUNDARY law (mechanisms that make epoch ends special degrade the harvested statistic — EXP-038/039/041); HARDWARE: ~2.5ms/block launch cost, 256-channel kernel cliff (22.4→54ms, EXP-040), channels 32-aligned.
- **Closed classes (do not re-derive)**: any width >256 channels anywhere; BN momentum ≠ 0.1; eval-constants engineering; attention/SE; LS doses; SAM/flatness; depth reshapes; schedule reshapes; optimizer swaps (incl. Muon); batch ≠ 512; data-order/coverage; tail lightening/taper; weight averaging (EMA + canonical SWA); init changes; activation swaps; shortcut changes; head pooling; resolution; regularizer stacking; eval-variance harvesting (reward hacking — rejected on principle, brainstorm-041).
- **The one diagnosed open door**: EXP-032's mechanism finding — "the accuracy ceiling is decision-boundary-limited, not confidence/calibration-limited; solution-smoothing moves logit geometry, not argmaxes." Weight averaging (single-mode) is closed; PREDICTION averaging across independently-initialized members (multi-mode) is the structurally different half of that axis and has never been measured here.
- **Untried flagged gap (low prior)**: EXP-017's insight flagged width asymmetry (widen a stage, keep all stages intact) as the one untried capacity-where-cheap move; EXP-040's cliff now caps it at ≤256 channels everywhere.
- **Hardware fact enabling the lead idea**: blocks are launch-bound at ≤256 channels (per-block ~2.5ms width-independent, ∂dt/∂FLOPs ≈ 13.3ms per unit FLOPs ratio, EXP-034) — modest FLOPs increases inside one kernel call are cheap; SEPARATE sequential models would pay the full per-block launch cost twice (+~22ms, dead on arrival). Any multi-member design must batch members into the SAME kernel calls (grouped convs) or alternate steps.

## Candidate Ideas

### Idea A — Grouped two-member deep ensemble: 2 × 3x-width ResNet-20 co-trained via groups=2 convs, logit-mean inference
**Summary**: Reallocate the certified capacity budget from one 4x net into TWO independent 3x members (per-member widths 48/96/192) living inside one module as grouped convolutions (total channels 96/192/384, all convs `groups=2`, BN per-channel so no cross-member mixing, input duplicated channel-wise for the grouped stem, per-member fc heads). Training loss = `CE(l1) + CE(l2)` (SUM, so each member's per-step gradient, LR, WD, and noise scale are byte-identical to the baseline recipe). Eval-mode forward returns `(l1 + l2) / 2` — the model Eval.evaluate() receives IS a 2-branch architecture whose inference output is the member-mean logits (confirmed compatible with prepare.py's contract: single (B,10) tensor, model.eval(), argmax). Inference FLOPs ≈ 1.125× baseline — the accuracy is not bought with inference compute.
**Reasoning**: This is the lone unmeasured half of the measured averaging dichotomy. Internal: SWA/EMA proved weight-space (single-mode) averaging adds zero accuracy here because the ceiling is decision-boundary-limited (EXP-032). External: Fort et al. show the gain ensembles deliver comes precisely from multi-mode function-space diversity that weight averaging cannot reach; Kondratyuk et al. show smaller-net ensembles beat single big nets at matched FLOPs. Law compliance: per-member recipe untouched (noise law ✓, heat ✓); members train from step 0 with standard Kaiming init (no deferral turn-on toll); nothing epoch-keyed (epoch-boundary law ✓); ensembling is not a regularizer, so the absorption law's mechanism (augmentation absorbing a regularization role) does not apply — its role (multi-mode averaging) is provided by nothing else in the recipe; the gain mechanism REDUCES prediction variance and shifts the plateau MEAN (the legitimate currency; the opposite of the rejected variance-harvesting hack). Arithmetic: FLOPs 2×(3/4)² = 1.125× → projected dt ≈ 24.1ms by the 13.3ms/FLOPs-unit law → ~125–129 epochs (deficit ~−0.15pp); member plateau at 3x/~127ep est. 96.2–96.4; published 2-member gains +0.4–0.7 (shaved for shared-batch correlation: members share batches and augmentation draws, diversity is init-only) → ensemble 96.6–96.9. Mid-probability of clearing 96.81, but the highest-EV construction available, and every failure mode is cheap to detect.
**Sources**: arXiv 1912.02757, 1612.01474, 2005.00570, 2002.06715, 2010.06610; reports/exp-report-032.md (decision-boundary diagnosis); exp-report-034/040 (dt pricing + cliff); prepare.py L32–47 (eval contract).
**Estimated Effort**: Moderate-high — ResNet restructure to grouped convs + per-member pad-shortcut (the F.pad zero-fill must pad per member-half, a view/pad/view fix), dual fc heads, train/eval forward branch on `self.training`, CPU member-isolation sanity (perturb member 2's weights → member 1's logits bit-identical; gradients don't cross), dt gate launch.
**Risk Assessment**: Primary risk is KERNEL PRICING: grouped convs at total width 384 may hit the >256 cliff or grouped-kernel inefficiency (the cliff was measured on dense convs; unknown for groups=2 with 192/group). Mitigated by the EXP-040 D0-median dt gate with a pre-registered kill verdict (~90s cost, screen produces the grouped-kernel pricing fact either way). Secondary: shared-batch members may be too correlated for the full published gain (init-only diversity); falsified cleanly by a plateau in the baseline band. Failure mode is graceful (no-improvement), nothing destabilizes.

### Idea B — Alternating-step two-member ensemble at 4x width (two separate models; member `step % 2` trains each step)
**Summary**: Keep two byte-identical 4x ResNet-20s (separate modules, separate optimizers, both compiled+warmed); each timed step trains exactly one member on the full batch, alternating; both share the time-keyed LR schedule (anneal completes for both on the global clock). Eval-mode wrapper-module returns mean logits (same eval contract as Idea A).
**Reasoning**: The zero-kernel-risk version of the ensemble mechanism: each training step runs the EXACT baseline kernels at 22.4ms — no grouped-conv pricing unknowns, no gate needed for dt. The cost surfaces instead as per-member step starvation: each member gets ~half the steps (~70 epochs' worth), and the starvation ladder (EXP-002/005/007: 40 ep −0.82, 52 ep −1.11, 55 ep −0.71) projects member plateaus ~96.0–96.2 — the ensemble gain must overcome a ~2× larger member deficit than Idea A's. Also pays 2× compile warmups in startup (fits; startup budget ~9–23s observed) and alternation makes parity structure... at STEP granularity, not epoch granularity — eval timing is unaffected (both members see equal steps by each epoch end ±1; no epoch-boundary specialness).
**Sources**: same ensemble literature as Idea A; goal-learnings starvation entries (EXP-002/005/007).
**Estimated Effort**: Moderate — no architecture surgery, but dual model/optimizer/compile plumbing and an eval-ensemble module.
**Risk Assessment**: Execution-safe but arithmetically weaker: needs ensemble gain ≥ +0.6 over member plateaus ~96.1 to clear the bar, at the top of the published range for init-only-diversity pairs. Keep as the pre-registered next-loop fallback if Idea A gate-kills on grouped-kernel pricing.

### Idea C — Within-cliff asymmetric width 64/160/256 (widen stage 2 only)
**Summary**: The one capacity-where-cheap move flagged untried by EXP-017's insight, now cliff-constrained: keep stages 1/3 at 64/256, widen stage 2 to 160 (32-aligned, all channels ≤256). FLOPs ≈ 1.18× → dt ≈ 24.8ms → ~125 epochs.
**Reasoning**: Single-model capacity at constant topology, inside every hardware bound. But it sits adjacent to the program's most-recurring failure (4 capacity attempts, all lost) and the certified single-change local optimum; EXP-017 measured that ALLOCATION moves lose even when faster; the deficit arithmetic (−0.2pp epochs toll) demands stage-2 width deliver ≥ +0.5 — no internal or external evidence supports a stage-2-specific gain of that size under heavy aug (absorption-law-adjacent: published width gains are light-aug fixed-epoch).
**Sources**: goal-learnings § Failed Approaches (capacity, count 4); exp-report-017.md insight; exp-report-040.md (cliff).
**Estimated Effort**: Low — widths constant change + transition handling already generic.
**Risk Assessment**: Graceful failure, but expected sub-bar; would re-measure a closed-class neighbor rather than a new mechanism.

## Idea Evaluation

- **Evidence strength**: A wins. It composes the program's own strongest unexploited diagnosis (EXP-032: ceiling is decision-boundary-limited; weight averaging null) with a literature family (deep ensembles, matched-FLOPs small-net ensembles, in-one-net ensembles) whose mechanism is exactly the unmeasured complement. C has explicit negative evidence (capacity recurring failure, allocation loses — EXP-017); B shares A's mechanism but starts from a measurably worse member-arithmetic position.
- **Mechanism clarity**: A is sharp: independent inits → different modes → logit averaging cancels decorrelated member errors at the decision boundary → plateau MEAN rises. The internal SWA/EMA nulls isolate this from "averaging in general" (single-mode averaging measured zero — the gain, if it appears, is attributable to multi-modality specifically). This experiment is informative in BOTH directions: a null here closes the entire averaging dichotomy.
- **Expected impact**: A targets the diagnosed binding constraint (decision boundaries) with published effect sizes (+0.4–0.7 for 2 members) that survive the ≥+0.3 screen even after shaving for shared-batch correlation and the −0.15pp epochs toll. B needs top-of-range gains; C needs an unevidenced +0.5.
- **Risk profile**: A's worst case is a ~90s gate-kill that BUYS the grouped-conv pricing fact for the hardware map (same shape as EXP-040's productive kill); its full-run failure mode is a clean no-improvement with member-correlation diagnostics. Nothing touches eval, timer, schedule, or noise.
- **Feasibility**: A's surgery is the largest of the three but every piece has a deterministic CPU sanity (member isolation, gradient separation, param counts, eval contract); the composite launcher and gate are validated infrastructure (EXP-040).

**Decision**: Idea A. Pre-register: if the dt gate kills grouped kernels (D0 above threshold), the verdict is `invalid` (screen, NaN metric) carrying the grouped-conv pricing fact, and Idea B becomes the natural next-loop candidate.

## Chosen Idea

- **Selected**: Idea A — Grouped two-member deep ensemble (2 × 3x ResNet-20 via groups=2, sum-CE training, logit-mean inference).
- **Why this idea**: It is the only remaining direction that (1) attacks the measured binding constraint — decision-boundary-limited plateau — through (2) the unmeasured half of an axis whose other half was carefully closed (function-space vs weight-space averaging), (3) with published effect sizes above the +0.3 screen, while (4) complying with every structural law: per-member recipe byte-identical (noise/heat), no deferral toll, no epoch-boundary structure, plateau-LEVEL currency, all channels per group ≤256 with the grouped-pricing unknown resolved by a ~90s pre-registered gate.
- **Hypothesis**: Two independently-initialized 3x members co-trained at baseline per-member dynamics and evaluated as mean logits converge to distinct modes whose averaged decision function has a HIGHER converged plateau mean than the single 4x baseline — predicting best_test_acc ≥ 96.81 (bar) if grouped dt ≤ ~25ms holds. Falsified by: (i) gate-kill (grouped kernels price at/above the cliff — hardware fact, screen verdict), or (ii) a plateau in the baseline band (init-only diversity under shared batches is insufficient — closes the averaging dichotomy entirely).
