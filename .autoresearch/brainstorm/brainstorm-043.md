## Web Search & Literature Review

No new live search — this loop executes the pre-registered retry path from EXP-042, whose literature base is already distilled in `knowledge/papers/deep-ensembles-function-space.md` (Lakshminarayanan 1612.01474; Fort et al. 1912.02757; Kondratyuk 2005.00570; BatchEnsemble 2002.06715; MIMO 2010.06610). Additional anchors relevant to THIS loop's design choices, from the same family:

- **Fort et al. (1912.02757) fork-time result**: members forked from a shared trunk EARLY in training retain most of the function-space diversity of independent inits; LATE forks (tail-only divergence) retain little — this brackets our design space between "full alternation" (fork at t=0, max diversity, max per-member starvation) and the measured-dead late end (SWA/EMA, EXP-032/011 = fork at t≈1, zero gain).
- **Deep-ensemble gains presuppose reasonably converged members** (1612.01474 experiments use fully-trained members): on starved members, errors are more bias-dominated (shared) and the decorrelation gain shrinks — the key uncertainty in any budget-split design and the reason member quality must be maximized.
- Hardware constraint from EXP-042 (exp-report-042.md): grouped convs cost 2.8× dense dt — any multi-member design MUST run each member through the certified dense kernels (22.4ms at 4x).

The mechanism — function-space (multi-mode) prediction averaging — remains the program's last unmeasured averaging mechanism, motivated by EXP-032's diagnosis (ceiling is decision-boundary-limited; weight-space averaging measured zero).
- **Laws that bind the design** (all measured): deferral (free in early heat/epochs/gradient quality); max-statistic (plateau LEVEL only); gradient-noise optimum (per-member dynamics must stay batch 512/β0.9/peak 0.4); epoch-boundary law (EXP-038/039/041 — nothing may make epoch ends structurally special → members must alternate per STEP, not per epoch, so both are equally fresh at every eval); absorption law (not applicable to multiplicity mechanisms, argued in brainstorm-042); kernel pricing (dense only; grouped 2.8×, >256-channel 2.4×).
- **Wall-clock budget law** (EXP-000/031, goal-learnings High): evals are uncharged but cost wall — budget 300 + startup + evals×cost ≤ 600; eval thinning in non-plateau phases (once/epoch is a CEILING) and 16 loader workers are validated levers. CRITICAL here: a 2-member ensemble doubles eval forward cost (~2.6s/eval) → un-thinned 139 evals ≈ 360s busts the cap; thinning is mandatory, not optional.
- **Starvation ladder** (EXP-002/005/007): 40 ep −2.16, 52 ep −1.55(5x), 55 ep −0.71(6x) vs mean — per-member step-splitting must be priced against this curve; 4x at ~70 epochs-equivalent projects roughly −0.3 to −0.5 from the 96.57 mean.
- **Untried gaps**: function-space averaging (this loop); within-cliff width asymmetry (flagged EXP-017, low prior, capacity-failure-adjacent).

## Candidate Ideas

### Idea A — Full-alternation two-member ensemble: 2 × 4x ResNet-20, one member trains per step, logit-mean inference
**Summary**: Instantiate TWO byte-identical 4x ResNet-20s (independent Kaiming draws from the seeded stream), each with its own SGD optimizer (baseline constants); the timed loop trains member `step % 2` on the full batch — dense kernels, dt ≈ baseline 22.4ms, ~139 loader epochs, ~6,700 steps/member (~70 epoch-equivalents each). Both share the time-keyed LR (progress = elapsed/budget) so each member's one-cycle anneal completes. Eval model is a `MeanEnsemble(nn.Module)` holding both eager members, forward = mean logits (same eval-contract pattern validated in EXP-042's sanity C). Eval thinning (every 3rd epoch while progress < 0.6, every epoch after) keeps wall ≈ 560–580s.
**Reasoning**: The purest test of the open mechanism: FULL diversity (independent inits AND disjoint batch streams — members see alternating batches, strictly more diversity than EXP-042's shared-batch design), certified per-member recipe (the exact baseline net and constants; the ONLY changes are half steps + ensembling — clean attribution), dense kernels only (EXP-042's closure respected), per-step alternation keeps both members equally fresh at every epoch end (epoch-boundary law satisfied; ±1 step). Arithmetic: members ~96.1–96.3 (starvation ladder at ~70 ep with completed anneal); independent-2-member literature gain +0.5–0.8, shaved for member starvation → predicted ensemble 96.55–96.95, median ≈96.7. Honest screen: the median sits below the bar — but the upside branch is real, and EITHER outcome finally measures the mechanism: a null (ensemble ≈ single-model band) closes the entire averaging dichotomy; a gain-but-miss (e.g., 96.7 with test_loss well below family ~0.185) quantifies the diversity gain and prices the mid-fork follow-up (Idea B) precisely.
**Sources**: knowledge/papers/deep-ensembles-function-space.md; reports/exp-report-042.md (closure + Next Steps); exp-report-032.md (mechanism motivation); goal-learnings (starvation ladder, wall levers, epoch-boundary law).
**Estimated Effort**: Moderate — dual model/optimizer/compile plumbing, alternation in the loop, MeanEnsemble eval module, eval-thinning logic, CPU sanities (independent inits, optimizer separation, eval contract, alternation correctness), composite launch (baseline thresholds — dt unchanged).
**Risk Assessment**: Wall cap is the top infra risk (2× eval cost) — mitigated by validated thinning + the watchdog's wall tick; VRAM ~3GB fine. Mechanism risk: starved members' errors may be bias-dominated (correlated) → gain below literature; falsified cleanly by plateau-in-band with family-equal test_loss. Compile: two instances of the same graph — second compile should hit the inductor cache; STARTUP_KILL at tick 12 covers a cold double-compile. Failure mode graceful (no-improvement).

### Idea B — Mid-fork ensemble: one 4x trunk for the first 50% of budget, then fork into two members alternating steps, logit-mean inference
**Summary**: Train a single 4x net normally to progress 0.5 (~73 full-rate epochs), then deep-copy weights+optimizer state into member 2 and alternate steps for the remainder (~36 epoch-equivalents each on top of the shared 73). Eval phase 1 = single net (cheap), phase 2 = mean logits.
**Reasoning**: Trades diversity for member quality: members reach ~109 effective epochs (deficit only ~−0.2 vs −0.4) while Fort et al.'s fork-time curves say mid-forks retain a substantial fraction of function diversity (the forked half spans LR 0.27→0, the entire descent into separate basins). Better member floor, smaller expected gain. CHOSEN-AGAINST for this loop because: (i) a null is ambiguous (fork too late? diversity insufficient?) where Idea A's null is decisive; (ii) the fork point is an untuned knob — Idea A's diagnostics (member starvation read via test_loss + ensemble gain size) are exactly what's needed to set it; (iii) extra mid-run state-copy machinery (weights + momentum buffers + BN stats) adds failure surface.
**Sources**: Fort et al. 1912.02757 (fork-time diversity); same project arithmetic as Idea A.
**Estimated Effort**: Moderate-high (mid-run fork of model+optimizer state under compile; phase-aware eval and watchdog).
**Risk Assessment**: Graceful failure but ambiguous attribution; best deployed AFTER Idea A's read prices the diversity-starvation trade.

### Idea C — Within-cliff asymmetric width 64/160/256 (widen stage 2 only) — the non-ensemble alternative
**Summary**: The one capacity-where-cheap move still flagged untried (EXP-017 insight), cliff-compliant (all ≤256, 32-aligned): FLOPs ≈1.18× → dt ≈24.8ms → ~125 epochs.
**Reasoning**: Carried from brainstorm-042 unchanged: single-model capacity inside every hardware bound, but adjacent to the most-recurring failure class (capacity, count 4) and the certified local optimum; needs an unevidenced stage-2-specific +0.5 to clear the deficit-adjusted bar. Kept on the list as the strongest non-ensemble candidate, not competitive with A on evidence.
**Sources**: exp-report-017.md insight; exp-report-040.md (cliff); goal-learnings § Failed Approaches (capacity).
**Estimated Effort**: Low.
**Risk Assessment**: Graceful but expected sub-bar; re-measures a closed-class neighbor.

## Idea Evaluation

- **Evidence strength**: A executes the only pre-registered, mechanism-open path (exp-report-042 Next Steps #1) with the literature family already vetted in brainstorm-042; its design directly incorporates this program's own laws (per-step alternation ← epoch-boundary law; dense kernels ← EXP-042; thinning ← EXP-031). B is the same mechanism with an extra untuned knob and ambiguous nulls. C has standing negative evidence.
- **Mechanism clarity**: A is the cleanest attribution available — the exact baseline network and recipe per member, changed only by (half steps) + (mean-logit inference). Any plateau shift above the starvation-priced member level is the function-space-averaging effect, separable via test_loss (diversity shows as loss reduction even when accuracy misses).
- **Expected impact**: A's median (~96.7) is honestly below the bar, but it carries the largest upside branch of any remaining candidate (full-diversity gain at the literature's top end clears 96.9), and its read de-risks/prices B for a follow-up loop. After 37 misses with every standard class measured, "measure the open mechanism at maximum cleanliness" is the highest-value move even at mid P(clear).
- **Risk profile**: A's infra risks (wall cap, double compile) all have validated mitigations and watchdog guards; mechanism failure is a clean no-improvement that CLOSES the averaging dichotomy (high information).
- **Feasibility**: A reuses the existing loop structure (loader, schedule, watchdog thresholds — dt unchanged at 22.4ms) with no kernel unknowns; no dt gate needed.

**Decision**: Idea A. Pre-register the interpretive branches: (i) best ≥ 96.81 → improvement, function-space averaging beats the bar; (ii) gain-visible-but-miss (best 96.6–96.8 AND final_test_loss ≤ ~0.165, i.e., clearly below family ~0.185) → mechanism REAL but starvation-limited → Idea B (mid-fork) is next loop's lead with the fork point priced by this read; (iii) plateau in baseline band with family-equal test_loss → diversity gain ≈ 0 on budget-split members → the averaging dichotomy closes entirely (weight-space dead by EXP-011/032, function-space dead by this).

## Chosen Idea

- **Selected**: Idea A — Full-alternation two-member ensemble (2 × 4x ResNet-20, per-step alternation, logit-mean inference, eval thinning).
- **Why this idea**: It is the pre-registered dense-kernel implementation of the program's last open mechanism, designed for maximum attribution cleanliness (certified per-member recipe; full init+data diversity; per-step alternation satisfying the epoch-boundary law) with every infra risk covered by validated levers, and it is informative under every outcome — including pricing the mid-fork follow-up.
- **Hypothesis**: Two fully-independent 4x members at ~70 epoch-equivalents each, ensembled by logit mean, produce a converged plateau whose LEVEL exceeds the single-model bar — predicting best_test_acc ≥ 96.81 if the 2-member decorrelation gain (+0.5–0.8 in literature) survives member starvation (−0.3 to −0.5). Falsified by: plateau in the baseline band with family-equal test_loss (no usable diversity on budget-split members → averaging axis closed both halves); diagnosed-but-insufficient by: sub-bar best with clearly-below-family test_loss (mechanism real, starvation-limited → route to mid-fork).
