# Experiment Report EXP-069: AugMix mixing-concentration alpha 1.0 → 2.0

## Goal
Maximize CIFAR-10 `best_test_acc` (%), higher-is-better, editing only `train.py` within Σdt=300s GPU-compute + total wall ≤ 600s (single H20). Baseline at experiment time: **96.45** (EXP-054, commit 86161d9). Bar: **96.55**.

## Idea & Hypothesis
**Chosen idea**: On the EXP-054 best recipe (`RandomApply([AugMix()], p=0.5)` + GPU Cutout16), change the single AugMix constructor arg `alpha` 1.0→2.0. `alpha` is the concentration of BOTH distributions AugMix samples: the Dirichlet(alpha,…) weights mixing the 3 augmentation chains, AND the Beta(alpha,alpha) weight `m` mixing the chain-combination back with the clean image. It is a **mean-preserving variance knob** — raising it concentrates both toward their means (no change to average augmentation strength or 50% coverage; only lower per-image variance). **Mechanism**: higher alpha makes the Dirichlet less likely to collapse to one dominant chain → every augmented image becomes a faithful ~50/50 blend of all three chains, amplifying the "multi-chain Dirichlet mixing + clean convex-mix" STRUCTURE that project-insights line 68 credits for AugMix's win, at zero op-count cost (wall-neutral). It is the one genuinely-untested, wall-safe sub-knob of augmentation diversity — the only lever (3× confirmed) ever to lift top-1 here; the documented closures map chain-COUNT/width (EXP-055) and per-op MAGNITUDE/severity (EXP-053) but never the mixing-weight distribution.

**Hypothesis**: A more faithful, consistent per-image 3-chain blend raises best_test_acc to ≥ 96.55. Stated most-likely alternative: a within-noise null (96.2–96.45), since the augmentation peak is well-characterized and alpha is mean-preserving.

## Approach
Single-kwarg change at train.py L171: `transforms.AugMix()` → `transforms.AugMix(alpha=2.0)` (+ explanatory comment). All else byte-identical to EXP-054 (w3/severity3/chain_depth-1, RandomApply p=0.5, GPU Cutout16, cosine peak0.2/warmup0.05/Nesterov m0.9/WD1e-4/LS0.1, batch128, seed42, compile reduce-overhead). torchvision 0.24.1 `AugMix.__init__` signature pre-verified to accept `alpha` (default 1.0); `AugMix()` in EXP-054 is exactly alpha=1.0, so this is a clean single-variable isolation. Direction chosen UP (not down to 0.5) because higher alpha keeps Beta near 0.5 → preserves full 50% effective coverage (avoids the EXP-055/057 coverage-drop failure mode that lower alpha would risk). No new deps, params unchanged (4,299,866). No deviations from plan.

## Execution
Single clean run on idle GPU 1 (GPU 0 lightly used 8%/1GB — used GPU 1, uncontended), exit 0, no retries, 0 NaN/error. 91 epochs (= baseline → alpha is compute/wall-neutral exactly as predicted: no op-count change means no AugMix CPU cost change). dt steady 8ms (615 of 706 sampled steps at 8ms, 90 at 9ms, 1 at 10ms — single-graph, throughput-neutral; the CPU-side aug change does not touch the GPU step). total_seconds 595.9 < 600 (CLEAN, no wall breach). Early gate (ep1) was healthy — loss decreasing 2.21→1.68, img/s ~15,000, no dataloader starvation.

## Results
**best_test_acc 96.25% (−0.20pp vs baseline 96.45, < 96.55 bar)** — a mild regression within the documented ±0.25pp scalar-knob noise band. final_test_loss 0.1998 (> EXP-054's 0.1968) — BOTH top-1 AND eval loss were slightly worse. The hypothesis was **falsified**: concentrating the mixing distributions did not amplify useful diversity; it mildly *under*-augmented. Root cause (matches the plan's stated risk): with lower mix variance, every augmented image is a consistent average of all three chains + clean — and averaging three independent augmentations partially **cancels** their distortions, yielding a softer net image. So higher alpha behaves like a mild reduction in effective augmentation strength, the opposite of adding diversity. This is consistent with the AugMix-severity result (EXP-053: magnitude interior-optimal) and the broader finding that the augmentation recipe is at its tuned peak.

**Trajectory fit**: closes the AugMix mixing-weight-distribution (alpha) sub-axis on the UP side. Combined with the width (EXP-055), severity (EXP-053), coverage (EXP-055/057), policy (EXP-060), and delivery-path (EXP-056/057/059) closures, the augmentation-diversity lever is now confirmed null on its **internal-distribution** dimension too. This is the 15th consecutive no-improvement since EXP-054 and reinforces the project-insights High verdict that 96.45 is at/near the k=4/300s ceiling. The −0.20pp is the gentle-null end of the band (vs Lookahead/progressive-resolution's −0.6pp active harms) — a benign mean-preserving knob landing just below baseline.

## Unexplored Avenues
- **AugMix alpha DOWN (1.0→0.5)**: the opposite direction — higher mix variance, more bimodal (images either near-clean or strongly-single-chain). Could increase the *spread* of augmented appearances (more diversity across images) BUT risks the EXP-055/057 coverage-drop failure mode (Beta(0.5,0.5) puts many images near-clean → effective coverage <50%) AND collapses the Dirichlet toward single-chain (away from the credited multi-chain structure). Two-sided risk, low expected value; the UP direction already shows the mean-preserving alpha knob is null-to-negative. Low confidence — likely not worth a loop.
- **chain_depth fix to 3**: compositional-depth diversity (distinct from width/severity), but wall-infeasible (+50% AugMix ops → est. >630s breach) — see brainstorm-069 Idea 3.
- **The augmentation lever is now exhausted on count (width), magnitude (severity), coverage (p), delivery (CPU/GPU), policy (AugMix/TA/AA/RA), mixing (Mixup/CutMix), AND internal mixing-distribution (alpha)** — every CPU-side sub-axis is mapped. Do not revisit augmentation without a genuinely new mechanism (e.g., a learned/GPU per-sample policy that adds diversity AT NO epoch cost, which EXP-056/057/059 showed is infeasible at this budget).
