### High Importance
- **CPU-heavy augmentation: budget gates on Σdt (GPU time) not wall, so a dataloader-bound aug keeps full epochs but balloons WALL toward the 600s hard limit — strongest AugMix variants are wall-INFEASIBLE at NUM_WORKERS=8** (EXP-052)
  Evidence: exp-report-052 — default AugMix(w3,d-1) ran dt 8ms (GPU step unchanged) but real intra-epoch wall 46.3ms/step (5.8× starvation) → projected ~1500–1850s ≫ 600s, ABORTED. Dataloader-throughput probe: w3=21.1ms/batch (~792s), w2,d2=17.9ms (~670s), w2,d1=12.6ms (~572s, shipped), TA-baseline=5.3ms. Isolated probes UNDERESTIMATE real-load throughput (~1.3–2×; GPU process contends with the 8 workers for CPU).
  Implication: for CPU-side augmentation (AugMix/AutoAugment/heavy PIL chains), the binding constraint is the 600s wall, not Σdt — epochs are preserved but wall blows up. Probe dataloader ms/batch first (must be ≲13ms/batch for the full budget to fit under 600s on 8 workers); do an early REAL-load wall measurement (eval-inclusive ms/step × est-total-steps + ~100s startup/eval < 600) before committing a long run. Can't raise NUM_WORKERS (frozen in prepare.py). The genuinely-strong AugMix would need a GPU-side per-sample implementation to be testable.
- **RandomApply(heavy CPU aug) has HIGH run-to-run WALL variance — budget the wall conservatively** (EXP-054)
  Evidence: exp-report-054 — `RandomApply([AugMix() w3], p=0.5)` early-window real-load projected ~535s but the run finished at 593.0s (58s spread), because per-batch CPU cost varies with the random # of augmented samples. Passed <600s but only 7s margin. Isolated probe 12.9ms/batch.
  Implication: for stochastic-application augmentations, the early-window wall projection UNDER-estimates; add a safety margin (target projected ≤ ~540s, not ~585s) or use a lower p (EXP-054 p=0.4 probed ~517s). Do NOT trust a single tight (<600s) wall as reproducible — a replication may exceed it.
- **Epoch count varies run-to-run (~65–77) from per-step throughput jitter (~10–17ms); sub-~0.2pp deltas are noise** (EXP-003, EXP-005)
  Evidence: EXP-003 fit 77 epochs @ ~10ms; EXP-005 (compute-neutral WD change) fit only 65 @ ~17ms — same hardware, GPUs idle at check.
  Implication: treat improvements < ~0.2 pp as within noise at this budget; target ideas with ≥~0.3 pp expected gain, and don't over-read a single run's epoch count.
### Medium Importance
- **Input std-normalization is INFEASIBLE — the frozen eval pins `std=(1,1,1)`** (EXP-037 feasibility kill)
  Evidence: prepare.py L13 `mean,std=(0.4914,0.4822,0.4465),(1,1,1)` + L15-19 `test_tf=Compose([ToTensor(),Normalize(mean,std)])` — protected/uneditable. brainstorm-037 § Candidate Ideas (DISCARDED).

Do NOT propose changing train normalization mean/std — it is pinned by the frozen eval. (Train-only geometric augs that eval doesn't apply, e.g. crop padding-mode, are fine — eval doesn't crop.)

### High Importance
- **Clean-data BN recalibration (Precise-BN on un-augmented images before eval) HURTS −1.6pp — augmented BN running stats are the trained-in operating point, NOT a bug to fix** (count: 1, EXP-061)
  Mechanism: hypothesis was that Cutout(zeros ~25%)+AugMix(distorts ~50%) bias the BN running stats (used only at eval) vs the clean test distribution, so recomputing them on clean training images would help. FALSIFIED, backwards: clean-BN tail epochs CRATERED to ~94.7 (loss 0.225) vs the augmented-BN peak 96.28 (−1.6pp), flat across all 4 recalibrated epochs (not a sample-size artifact; 2048 imgs ample). Root cause: BN uses BATCH stats in training, RUNNING stats at eval; the conv weights + BN affine (γ,β) were optimized against the AUGMENTED batch distribution, so the augmented running stats ARE the operating point the network expects. Recomputing on clean data shifts the normalization away from what γ,β were trained for → mis-scaled downstream → collapse. reports/exp-report-061.md.
  Insight: under heavy augmentation the augmented BN running stats are load-bearing — the eval-time "mismatch" is the trained-in design point, not an error. **The eval-time BN-statistics axis is CLOSED — do NOT retry Precise-BN / clean-BN-recompute / partial-recalibration variants.** (Explains why aug cooldown EXP-034 gives only a tiny tail-climb: it lets weights AND stats re-adapt to clean data JOINTLY; recalibrating stats alone with fixed weights breaks the pairing.) Also: the recalibration overhead overran the wall (604.6>600s) on the 593s-tight AugMix recipe — see infra-errors.
- **Auto-aug POLICY-FAMILY axis fully mapped — AutoAugment(CIFAR10), TrivialAugment, RandAugment all cluster at ~96.2 < AugMix-p0.5 96.45; the winner is AugMix's STRUCTURE, not the policy** (count: 1, EXP-060; closes the policy axis opened EXP-012/014)
  Mechanism: AutoAugment(CIFAR10 learned policy) full-coverage replacing AugMix-p0.5 (one-line swap, all else byte-identical, CPU epoch-free) → **96.22 = TrivialAugment 96.22 (EXP-012)** to the hundredth; RandAugment was 96.19 (EXP-014). Fair converged full-budget run (91 ep, dt 8ms, wall 402.5s ≪ 600 — wall NEVER the constraint; img/s 15,300, no dataloader starvation), loss 0.194 (slightly < EXP-054's 0.197, lower top-1 = loss-not-top1). reports/exp-report-060.md.
  Insight: the hypothesis that a CIFAR-LEARNED policy beats dataset-agnostic AugMix was FALSIFIED — the TrivialAugment-paper thesis (AA≈TA on CIFAR-10) obtained instead. All single-policy auto-augs (AA/TA/RA, 1–2 ops, full coverage, no clean-mix) cluster ~96.2; AugMix wins (96.45) via its STRUCTURE — multi-chain Dirichlet mixing + clean-image convex mix applied to a ~50% SUBSET — not policy identity or CIFAR-specificity. **The auto-aug policy-family axis is now CLOSED (AA was the last untried major policy). Do NOT try AutoAugment(IMAGENET/SVHN), or any further single-policy auto-aug.** Combined with the sub-lever closures below, the ENTIRE augmentation lever is exhausted: AugMix-w3-p0.5 = 96.45 is the ceiling.
- **Ghost BatchNorm (noisier sub-batch normalization stats) — the NORMALIZATION axis, the last untouched accuracy axis: REGRESSES at batch 128** (count: 1, EXP-047)
  Mechanism: replaced all BN with GhostBatchNorm2d (num_splits=4, ghost size 32; per-ghost stats inject regularizing noise, Hoffer 2017). Implemented dt-SAFE (static outer-dim reshape valid on channels_last; in-place running-stat update) — the reduce-overhead CUDA graph stayed INTACT (dt 9ms steady, NOT the ~14-16ms of a graph break, cf. EXP-042). But it FAILED two ways: (a) the noisier ghost-32 stats slowed convergence (ep1 19.98% vs ~45.7% baseline) and produced a WORSE final loss (0.2197 > 0.195) → net-negative regularization on an already regularization-saturated net; (b) the manual per-ghost+full-batch reductions cost ~1ms/step (8→9ms) → epochs 91→78. Result 95.16 (−1.06pp). reports/exp-report-047.md.
  Insight: GhostBN's benefit is LARGE-batch-specific (closing the large-batch generalization gap); at batch 128 that gap is already narrow, so the added normalization noise is gratuitous and hurts (fits the regularization-saturation + epoch-wall patterns). **The NORMALIZATION axis is now CLOSED — the 96.22 k=4/300s ceiling is fully mapped across every accuracy axis.** Do NOT retry GhostBN variants (ghost-64/num_splits=2 less noise → smaller-still null; a zero-premium impl would only isolate the epoch effect, but the WORSE loss shows the pure GhostBN effect is also ≤ baseline). Precise-BN (a different normalization sub-lever) remains formally untried but near-certain negligible (~35k converged BN updates).
- **Weight averaging (EMA, SWA, AND Lookahead) — net-negative on top-1 in EVERY form: eval-time averaging approaches the cosine-to-0 baseline from below; trajectory-level averaging actively regresses** (count: 4, EXP-006, EXP-019, EXP-020, EXP-068)
  Mechanism: EMA on cosine-to-0 = no-op (95.97, EXP-006). Proper SWA with a constant-LR floor engages (project-lowest
  loss ~0.18 << 0.195) and improves monotonically as the floor drops: 0.05→95.97 (EXP-019), 0.02→96.13 (EXP-020) —
  but as SWA_LR→0 the constant tail degenerates into the cosine-to-0 baseline itself, so SWA's supremum over the
  floor IS 96.22, approached from below. **EXP-068 Lookahead (k=5, α=0.5, eval slow iterate) — the trajectory-level variant predicted-and-now-confirmed: −0.61pp to 95.84 (loss 0.2097 > 0.1968), compute-neutral (91 ep, clean 589s). Unlike eval-time EMA/SWA (which only smooth the eval point), Lookahead's slow weights re-enter TRAINING (fast←slow reset every k) — and pulling the fast weights back toward a lagging average on an already-well-tuned cosine-to-0 trajectory DISCARDS useful late-trajectory progress → actively harmful, not merely null.** reports/exp-report-006.md, -019.md, -020.md, -068.md.
  Insight: weight-space averaging cannot EXCEED a well-tuned cosine-to-0 schedule on TOP-1 at this budget, in ANY form — eval-time (EMA/SWA, maxes on loss/flatness not top-1) OR trajectory-level (Lookahead, actively regresses). Axis FULLY CLOSED — do NOT pursue SWA/EMA/Lookahead/lower-floor/cyclic-SWA or any iterate-averaging variant; the cosine-to-0 endpoint is already the top-1 ceiling for this recipe.
### Medium Importance
- **Label-mixing augmentation (Mixup, CutMix) on the TA+Cutout recipe — underfits at the 300s budget, regresses** (count: 2, EXP-011, EXP-018)
  Mechanism: soft-target mixing augs warm up slowly (CIFAR papers use 200–300 ep); at our ~71–88 ep budget on the
  already regularization-saturated net they don't pay off. Weak Mixup (α=0.2) nulled (95.86, EXP-011); strong CutMix
  (p=0.5, α=1.0) REGRESSED −1.08pp (95.14, EXP-018, loss 0.277 soft-target artifact). reports/exp-report-011.md, -018.md.
  Insight: the label-mixing sub-axis is CLOSED for this budget — do NOT try Mixup/CutMix variants (lower p, schedules)
  unless the epoch budget grows massively. Distinct from INPUT-SPACE aug (TrivialAugment), which DID gain (EXP-012);
  TA is the augmentation ceiling for this recipe. Judge any future mixing aug on ACC, not loss (soft-target inflates loss).

  Insight: the EXP-000 heuristic peak (0.2) was already well-tuned; the LR-PEAK axis is CLOSED — do not sweep it
  further. (UPDATE EXP-029: the schedule SHAPE was also tested and closed — see below. The ENTIRE LR-schedule axis

- **LR-schedule SHAPE / SGDR warm restarts — single full-budget cosine-to-0 is optimal; restarts under-converge at 300s** (count: 1, EXP-029)
  Mechanism: 2-cycle SGDR (cosine PEAK→0 each half-budget, warm restart to PEAK at 50%). PERFECTLY throughput-neutral schedule-only test (91 ep, dt 8ms, params unchanged = the cleanest fair test yet). Restart verified firing at 50.1% (lr 0→0.2, loss bump 0.84→1.16). Result: 95.55 (−0.67pp) AND final_test_loss WORSE 0.195→0.208. reports/exp-report-029.md.
  Insight: at a SHORT fixed budget, splitting ~91 ep into 2×~45 ep just gives two under-resolved anneals — the restart destroys cycle-1's converged minimum and cycle-2 can't recover past the single long cosine. SGDR's re-exploration benefit needs many epochs; its snapshot-ensemble half overlaps the CLOSED weight-averaging axis (EXP-006/019/020). Monotone toward FEWER cycles → 1 cycle (baseline) is optimal. Do NOT try more cycles, T_mult>1, or warmup-frac tweaks. **The LR-SCHEDULE AXIS IS FULLY CLOSED: peak (0.2, EXP-016/017) + floor (cosine-to-0, EXP-019/020) + shape (single cosine, EXP-029).**
- **Cutout occlusion-STRENGTH (hole size) — 16px is a clean interior optimum; both directions away regress** (count: 2, EXP-013, EXP-021)
  Mechanism: on the TA+Cutout(16) winner, 16→8px under-regularized (95.92, −0.30pp, loss↑ 0.195→0.202, EXP-013) AND
  16→20px (≈39% area) over-occluded (95.96, −0.26pp, loss↑ 0.195→0.197, EXP-021). Both fair same-budget runs (~92 ep,
  params/throughput unchanged). reports/exp-report-013.md, -021.md.
  Insight: 16px (DeVries & Taylor's tuned CIFAR value) is a clean interior optimum — the occlusion-strength axis is
  CLOSED (mirrors the LR-peak 0.2 interior optimum, EXP-016/017). Combined with policy-saturation (EXP-014) and
  label-mixing failure (EXP-011/018), the WHOLE augmentation family is mapped: TA+Cutout(16) is the aug ceiling.
- **Cutout occlusion-PATTERN (single hole vs distributed grid) — Cutout's single contiguous hole BEATS GridMask at matched strength** (count: 1, EXP-048)
  Mechanism: swapped Cutout-16 for a GPU-vectorized GridMask (distributed grid of squares, removed-area matched to ~25% to isolate pattern from strength, self-test-verified 0.254). Perfectly throughput-neutral (dt 8ms, 90 ep — a CLEAN matched-strength/matched-epoch fair test, no confound) → 95.60 (−0.62pp) AND final_test_loss 0.2100 > 0.195, slower early convergence. reports/exp-report-048.md.
  Insight: distributed occlusion (scattered small holes everywhere) regularizes WORSE than one contiguous hole on this small-image net — Cutout leaves the rest of the object intact (forcing whole-context classification), while GridMask degrades fine 32×32 features everywhere (a softer but pervasive corruption this net tolerates worse). Fits/extends the EXP-037 pattern (aug PATTERN/QUALITY tweaks are null-to-negative on this saturated recipe). **Occlusion-PATTERN sub-lever CLOSED — do NOT try GridMask variants (stronger, +rotation, or ADDED-on-top-of-Cutout, all worse-leaning). With this, the AUGMENTATION AXIS IS FULLY MAPPED: strength (EXP-013/021), policy (EXP-014), mixing (EXP-011/018), cooldown (EXP-033/034/035), border-quality (EXP-037), occlusion-pattern (EXP-048). TA+Cutout(16) single-hole is the aug ceiling.**
- **Anti-aliased downsampling / BlurPool (Zhang 2019) — too compute-expensive at the 300s budget (epoch wall)** (count: 1, EXP-024)
  Mechanism: param-free anti-aliasing (stride-1 conv → binomial blur → stride-2 subsample) at the 2 stride-2 sites.
  Correct & params-unchanged, BUT moving conv1 to stride-1 ~4×'d FLOPs at the two heaviest convs → dt 8→~9.5ms →
  epochs 91→77 → under-trained to 95.66 (−0.56pp, loss 0.195→0.209). reports/exp-report-024.md.
  Insight: COMPUTE-CONFOUNDED — the regression is the epoch wall (cf. EXP-004/009/015), not a clean test of anti-
  aliasing. Any structural change that adds non-trivial FLOPs hits the same wall regardless of merit; anti-aliasing
  can't be fairly tested at 300s/k=4. Do NOT retry BlurPool variants here unless the budget grows. UPDATE (EXP-027):
  the masked anti-aliasing hypothesis was finally tested COMPUTE-NEUTRALLY via ResNet-D (below) and ALSO regressed —
  so anti-aliased/information-preserving downsampling is unhelpful here on its own merit, not just the FLOPs confound.
- **Information-preserving downsample / ResNet-D (avgpool-2 → stride-1 1×1 shortcut, He 2019) — FAIR compute-neutral test, regresses top-1 AND loss** (count: 1, EXP-027)
  Mechanism: replaced the lossy stride-2 1×1 shortcut (samples every other pixel, drops 3/4) with avgpool-2 → stride-1
  1×1 at the 2 downsample blocks so all pixels contribute. Touches only the cheap 1×1 shortcut → throughput-NEUTRAL
  (89 ep ≫ 85, dt ~8.7ms, params 4,299,866 unchanged) — the fair re-test EXP-024 couldn't do. Result: 95.75 (−0.47pp)
  and final_test_loss WORSE 0.195→0.210. reports/exp-report-027.md.
  Insight: on this shallow 32×32 net with only 2 downsample points the lossy stride-2 1×1 is BETTER — the avgpool
  low-pass blurs discriminative high-frequency signal, whereas the strided 1×1 is a learned subsample the net adapts to.
  ResNet-D's ImageNet gain (deep, large images, many stages) does NOT transfer to shallow CIFAR. Uniquely among the
  recent nulls this hurt BOTH top-1 and loss (not a polish-moves-loss case). The DOWNSAMPLING/anti-aliasing axis is now
  CLOSED from both the expensive (BlurPool/EXP-024) and cheap (ResNet-D/EXP-027) sides — do NOT revisit it.
- **Reallocating capacity from WIDTH to DEPTH (deeper-narrower iso-param ResNet-32, k=3 {48,96,192}, 15 blocks) — NOT dt-neutral even at iso-FLOP; epoch wall → −3.64pp** (count: 1, EXP-044)
  Mechanism: the premise was that depth is more parameter-efficient for generalization than width on CIFAR (He 2016) and that a deeper-NARROWER net sized ≈iso-FLOP (97.8%) could dodge the epoch wall that killed the wider nets. FALSIFIED at the wall-clock level: 15 sequential conv+BN blocks (vs baseline 9) at lower per-layer arithmetic intensity → memory-bandwidth/launch-bound → clean uncontended dt rose 8→12ms (+50%, WORSE than EXP-038 fat-head's +31%) despite ≈iso-FLOP → only 60 ep (≪77 saturation) → severe underfit (final_test_loss 0.2905 ≫ 0.195, also slower per-epoch convergence ep1 20.7% vs ~55%) → 92.58 (−3.64pp). dt verified architectural (flat 12ms across all 23k steps, GPU idle before/after). reports/exp-report-044.md.
  Insight: depth, like width, is gated by the 300s epoch wall — and more steeply (more sequential layers cost more dt per added FLOP than wider layers). **The CAPACITY axis is now CLOSED from ALL THREE directions: uniform widening (k=5/6, EXP-004/009), FLOP-neutral width reallocation (EXP-038), AND depth↔width reallocation (EXP-044).** Do NOT retry depth variants (ResNet-26/44, asymmetric block counts, depth-at-k=4) — the dt-vs-depth curve is unfavorable at this hardware floor. The "capacity closed (width/depth)" claim (EXP-038) is now EMPIRICALLY confirmed for depth, not just asserted.
- **Widening past k=4 (k=5, k=6) under the 300s budget — even with the torch.compile enabler** (count: 2, EXP-004, EXP-009)
  Mechanism: wider nets turn compute-bound → too few epochs to converge. k=6 eager 22ms→35 ep→95.26 (EXP-004);

  compile CANNOT lift it for k≥5. Do not pursue more width/depth/capacity at this budget. (NOTE: the orthogonal axes

### Low Importance
- **AugMix op-MAGNITUDE (severity 3→6) on the w2,d1 winner — magnitude is interior-optimal; lowers loss but top-1 flat/−0.05pp** (count: 1, EXP-053)
  Mechanism: on the EXP-052 AugMix(w2,d1) winner (96.34), raised severity 3→6 (CPU-neutral, all-coverage, single-variable). Clean 91-ep run (dt 8ms, 580.4s) → 96.29 (−0.05pp, within noise) with final_test_loss 0.1961 < EXP-052's 0.2010 (loss↓, top-1 flat — polish-vs-top1 signature). reports/exp-report-053.md.
  Insight: AugMix default severity=3 is already near-optimal — op MAGNITUDE does not add useful diversity here, mirroring Cutout occlusion-STRENGTH (EXP-013/021 interior-optimal). Sharpens the EXP-052 insight: the diversity lever is chain COUNT/distinctness (mixing), NOT per-op magnitude. **Do NOT sweep AugMix severity (1/2/9) — magnitude axis closed.** The live (unexhausted) dimension is chain count (w2,d2/w3), which is wall-INFEASIBLE at 8 workers — the lever and the 600s wall are in tension; pushing it needs throughput recovery (GPU-side or stochastic-subset AugMix).
- **AugMix mixing-DISTRIBUTION alpha 1.0→2.0 (concentrate the Dirichlet chain-weights + Beta clean-mix toward their means) — mean-preserving, wall-neutral, but mildly UNDER-augments → −0.20pp (96.25)** (count: 1, EXP-069)
  Mechanism: on the EXP-054 winner, raised AugMix `alpha` 1.0→2.0 — a single kwarg, the one untested *wall-safe* augmentation sub-knob (orthogonal to width/count and severity/magnitude). alpha concentrates BOTH the Dirichlet(alpha) chain-weights AND the Beta(alpha,alpha) clean-mix toward their means: every augmented image becomes a consistent ~50/50 blend of all 3 chains (vs alpha=1 where one chain often dominates and the clean-mix weight is often extreme). It is MEAN-PRESERVING (avg strength/coverage unchanged) → cannot over/under-regularize on average, only lowers per-image mix VARIANCE; and WALL-NEUTRAL (no op-count change → 91 ep, dt 8ms, 595.9s clean, no breach). Result 96.25 (−0.20pp, within the ±0.25pp band) with final_test_loss 0.1998 > EXP-054's 0.1968 (BOTH top-1 and loss mildly worse). reports/exp-report-069.md.
  Insight: the hypothesis (more faithful multi-chain blending = more useful diversity) was FALSIFIED in the benign direction — concentrating the mix does NOT add diversity, it mildly REDUCES effective augmentation: **averaging 3 independent augmentation chains per image partially CANCELS their distortions → a softer net image**, behaving like a small severity reduction (mirrors EXP-053 magnitude interior-optimal). **The AugMix mixing-weight-DISTRIBUTION (alpha) sub-axis is CLOSED on the UP side — do NOT retry alpha>1; alpha<1 (more bimodal/variance) is contraindicated too: it pushes the Dirichlet toward single-chain (away from the credited multi-chain structure) AND drops effective coverage <50% (the EXP-055/057 failure mode).** With this, ALL CPU-side AugMix sub-axes are mapped: count/width (EXP-055 ✗), magnitude/severity (EXP-053 ✗), coverage (EXP-055/057 ✗), mixing-distribution/alpha (EXP-069 ✗) — w3/p0.5/sev3/alpha1 (EXP-054 defaults) is the tuned optimum.
- **AugMix op-SET composition `all_ops=False` (drop the 4 photometric ops Brightness/Color/Contrast/Sharpness; keep the 9 geometric/lossless ops) — NEAR-NEUTRAL for top-1 (96.43, −0.02pp, the SMALLEST post-EXP-054 delta) but LOWERS loss 0.1968→0.1911** (count: 1, EXP-072; closes the last untested AugMix sub-axis — op-menu composition)
  Mechanism: torchvision AugMix defaults `all_ops=True` (13 ops), so EXP-054's `transforms.AugMix()` silently ran the 4 magnitude-photometric ops. `all_ops=False` restricts the per-chain pool to the original-paper 9 (ShearX/Y, TranslateX/Y, Rotate, Posterize, Solarize, AutoContrast, Equalize). Single kwarg, CPU-side (Σdt-free, throughput-neutral: 92 ep, dt 8ms, 570.3s). Result 96.43 (−0.02pp, a virtual TIE — far inside the ±0.25pp band and the smallest delta of any post-054 run) with final_test_loss 0.1911 < EXP-054's 0.1968 (loss↓, top-1 flat — polish-vs-top1). reports/exp-report-072.md.

- **Widening (WRN-style) is the dominant accuracy lever — but only up to k=4 at the 300s budget** (EXP-001, EXP-004)
  Evidence: exp-report-001 k=4 lifted 92.06→94.90 (+2.84) at ~10ms/step; exp-report-004 k=6 turned compute-bound

### Medium Importance
- **`torch.compile(mode="reduce-overhead")` buys ~30% throughput on this launch-bound k=4 net** (EXP-007)
  Evidence: exp-report-007 — dt 10–11ms→8ms, ~11.6k→15.4k img/s, 77→89 epochs; DEFAULT mode was net-negative
  (1.03× for ~13.6s compile cost). Eval kept on the eager handle to avoid recompiles. No new dep (core torch).
  Implication: a working efficiency lever — but epochs are saturated, so SPEND the freed throughput on capacity
  (compiled k=5/k=6, which EXP-004 found compute-bound at eager 22ms/step), not on more epochs.
- **Validated recipe baseline: time-fraction cosine schedule + bf16 + channels_last + Nesterov + label-smoothing(0.1)** (EXP-000)
  Evidence: reports/exp-report-000.md — clean 92.06% in 300s; new current baseline (commit de14baa).
  Implication: build future experiments on top of this recipe; the baseline's step-space MultiStepLR never annealed (mistuned for the 300s budget).
- **Cutout(16) regularizes the wide model: +0.52 pp and overfit loss 0.25→0.22** (EXP-002, EXP-003)
  Evidence: reports/exp-report-002.md (94.90→95.42); reports/exp-report-003.md (efficient GPU Cutout → 96.00, loss 0.20).
  Implication: regularization is a live lever now that capacity is high; stacking more (WD 5e-4, mixup, tuned Cutout) is promising.
