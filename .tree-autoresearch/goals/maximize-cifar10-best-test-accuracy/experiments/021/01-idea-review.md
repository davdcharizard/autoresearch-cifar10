# Adversarial Review — EXP-021 Finalists

## Prioritized Feedback

**1. [Cross-cutting] All three ideas under-shoot the diagnosed effect-size bar, and none can touch the global best in one run.** `02-system-understanding.md` says a credible child needs a **stable** ~0.25–0.30-point effect above a plateau; the parent here is EXP-004 (95.40), so even a full mechanism-sized success (~95.70) stays below the 95.71 resolution bar vs EXP-011's 95.61. The staged prove-then-compose-with-EMA strategy is defensible (EXP-011's plateau has four failed children), but it means the *real* payoff of this experiment is composability. The selection should therefore weight (a) clean attribution and (b) zero interference with a later EMA composition — which favors near-free objective changes over throughput-costly architecture changes. All three proposals should state their EMA-composition prognosis explicitly; only idea-01's closing section gestures at it.

**2. [Companion classifier] The tapped stage-2 tensor is un-normalized in a pre-activation network — the head's input conditioning drifts, and the proposal's justification is slightly wrong.** The proposal claims ReLU-then-pool "is consistent with the pre-activation network's use of activated features at the next block boundary" (idea-01.md §Exact Architecture), but block 4's own path applies **BN then ReLU**; the companion skips BN, so it consumes a raw residual stream whose magnitude grows over training. DSN-style heads operated on differently conditioned features. Consequence: companion loss scale (and hence the effective 0.15 dose and the joint SAM perturbation direction) drifts in an uncontrolled way across the run. Not fatal — a BN-free head is the right *controlled* choice — but the production diagnostics should add the pooled stage-2 feature-norm trajectory (cheap, reusable from values already computed on SAM pulses) so a null or negative result can be attributed to conditioning vs mechanism. Currently only weight-displacement is audited.

**3. [Companion classifier] The literature prior is weaker than presented for this network class.** DSN (AISTATS 2015) predates residual+BN architectures; its motivation — gradient starvation in deep, poorly conditioned nets — is explicitly disclaimed by the proposal itself for a 16-layer PreAct WRN ("may not be gradient-starved"). The auxiliary-training note (papers/auxiliary-training.md) concedes headline gains come from corruption/distillation/selective-BN machinery that is absent here. So the honest evidence base is "plausible regularization bias," not "reported CIFAR gains transfer." The proposal handles this candidly in its risk section, but the expected-effect range (+0.15 to +0.35) is asserted, not derived — the low end of that range is below the useful stable-effect bar. Improvement: pre-commit that a 95.50–95.59 result (best-only, no lifted tail) does **not** trigger EMA composition; the proposal partially says this ("weak evidence and should not immediately be composed") — make it a hard rule.

**4. [SupCon] The two cited papers, read closely, argue *against* this exact adaptation more than for it.** (a) Khosla et al.'s own ablations show the projection head matters — applying the contrastive loss directly at the encoder output (exactly what idea-02 does) was the *worse* configuration in the source method, and the paper note itself flags the adaptation gap. (b) Graf et al. is cited as reassurance ("shared simplex optimum") but its actual implication, stated in the note, is **redundancy**: CE already targets the same geometry, so the auxiliary term may add only duplicate pressure on a run already regularized by CutMix + drop-path + SAM. The proposal acknowledges both but does not resolve either; that makes this the weakest-evidenced finalist. Improvement: none available within the frozen no-projection design — which is itself the finding: the compute-driven simplifications strip out the components the evidence says carry the effect.

**5. [SupCon] Concrete unaddressed failure mechanism: relative dose growth exactly where SAM operates.** With CE falling toward ~0.16 in the clean tail while SupCon's floor is bounded away from zero, the fixed 0.05 weight makes the auxiliary term a growing fraction of the joint gradient precisely in the phase whose SAM mechanism produced EXP-004's validated +0.17. The proposal names this ("the main dose risk") but offers no mitigation and no pre-registered diagnostic threshold for detecting it post-hoc. Improvement: the audit already logs primary vs SAM-replay SupCon/CE separately — add a pre-committed interpretation rule (e.g., "if tail weighted-SupCon exceeds X% of joint loss, a null is attributed to dose interference, informing a lower-weight successor") so the single run yields more than a binary verdict.

**6. [Width-288] Gap between claimed mechanism and diagnosed limiter, with local counter-evidence.** The diagnosis says the limiter is stable generalization with per-step compute tight and exposure precious (EXP-005: −0.12 from halved identity exposure at equal steps). Width-288 pays a ~7–10% exposure tax (~1,600–2,300 updates) for capacity, when nothing in the record indicates capacity binds: EXP-004's final loss is 0.1654 with final=best (well-fitted, not underfitted), and EXP-010 — invoked as leaving widening "open" — is actually mild evidence *against* late capacity being the constraint (equal-MAC late reallocation, 9.3% more updates, still 95.04). The proposal's reframing of EXP-010 is legitimate (depth-reallocation ≠ widening) but it should be stated as "untested" rather than implicitly supported.

**7. [Width-288] The attribution confound plus the +0.10 threshold makes a narrow pass nearly uninterpretable.** Shape-dependent construction shifts every downstream RNG stream (init realization, sampler/worker seeds, data order) — the proposal admits a 95.50–95.59 result is "package-level evidence," but prior goal history shows 0.14–0.29-point single-run/tail variability, which fully spans that band. To its credit the proposal sets a ≥95.60 mechanism bar; the improvement is to go further and pre-declare that a 95.50–95.59 result should **not** be used as a base for EMA composition without the stable-tail criterion (final within 0.15 of best, final-16 mean reported) being met.

**8. [Width-288, minor] The 1.076x latency prior is a one-point linear interpolation, but the margin is real.** EXP-014's measured 16.1% cost slightly undercut its 17.6% MAC delta, so sublinear-ish scaling supports 288 clearing the 1.10 gate — the systems risk is genuinely low, and the preflight design (alternating rounds, drift/MAD gates) is the best-engineered of the three. No change needed; this is the idea's strongest section.

**No fatal flaws found.** No hard-constraint violations (all modify only `train.py`, preserve budget/GPU/eval cadence), no reward-hacking (all keep the evaluator frozen and companion/aux paths out of inference), and none is an unchanged retry — width-288 is materially distinct from EXP-014 (different width, pre-committed before any accuracy signal, explicitly left open by EXP-014's terminal report).

## Scored Verdict

| Idea | Evidence & reasoning | Potential impact |
|---|---|---|
| **Stage-2 companion classifier** | **7/10** — two independent (if dated) literature priors, exact-attribution design with bitwise parent RNG parity, honest about the gradient-starvation disclaimer; docked for the un-normalized-tap conditioning gap and asserted effect range. | **7/10** — plausible +0.15–0.35 on the diagnosed representation limiter at near-zero compute, preserving full ~25.5k-step exposure; cost asymmetry means downside is almost purely the experiment slot, and it composes trivially with EMA. |
| Hard-batch single-view SupCon | **4/10** — its own citations undercut the no-projection, single-view, joint-CE form (Khosla ablation; Graf redundancy), and the 62.5% dose plus tail-growth risk are named but unmitigated. | **6/10** — a genuinely orthogonal relational signal at the exact deployed feature could complement SAM for +0.1–0.3, but the probability mass on "redundant or SAM-interfering" is high. |
| Fixed 288-channel taper | **6/10** — excellent systems evidence and the most rigorous preflight, but the capacity-limiter premise is unsupported locally (EXP-010 mildly against, parent well-fitted) and the RNG package confound weakens any narrow win. | **5/10** — claimed +0.10–0.35 must be net of a ~7–10% exposure tax the lineage has shown is costly (EXP-005), and a sub-0.20 result is inside documented noise. |

**Pick: Stage-2 training-only companion classifier (idea-01).**

It is the only finalist whose mechanism, cost profile, and attribution design all line up with the diagnosis. It attacks the stated limiter (representation quality / stable generalization) head-on rather than adjacently; it is the only one that pays **no exposure tax** — the resource the lineage has repeatedly shown is precious — so any measured gain is pure mechanism, not a capacity-vs-exposure trade; and its RNG-isolated construction gives the cleanest causal read of the three, which matters most given that this experiment's real value is qualifying a mechanism for later EMA composition. SupCon loses because its evidence base, on close reading, disfavors precisely the simplifications the budget forces; width-288 loses because it trades a validated resource for an unvalidated premise with a confounded readout. The companion's honest weaknesses — uncertain effect size on a shallow well-conditioned net, and the un-normalized tap — should be absorbed before planning: add the pooled stage-2 feature-norm diagnostic (feedback #2) and harden the no-EMA-composition rule for a best-only sub-0.60 result (feedback #3). With those two refinements it is a sound, falsifiable, well-bounded single-run bet.
