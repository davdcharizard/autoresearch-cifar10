### What the result does and does not establish

- **Established:** under this frozen protocol, the exact 1-2-3 package (allocation + redistributed drop-path dose + its RNG realization) delivers no improvement over EXP-002 despite +9.3% steps, +40% parameters, and 13 extra evaluation checkpoints. The formal threshold (95.33) was missed by 0.29 — beyond documented tail noise — so "no improvement" is solid, not marginal.

### Final verdict

**The recorded result is trustworthy and the experiment was executed to protocol; the correct tree verdict is `no-improvement` (metric 95.04, parent 95.23, threshold 95.33).** The preregistered hypothesis (≥95.53) is falsified.
