# Venues

Default venues are limited to the top three ML conferences for high signal.
Expand deliberately by uncommenting domain-specific venues below.

> **Note:** When searching ICLR, always include "ICLR" explicitly in your query
> (e.g. `site:openreview.net ICLR quantization`) to reduce noise from other
> venues hosted on OpenReview.

## Active Venues

| Venue | Proceedings URL | Domain | Notes |
|-------|----------------|--------|-------|
| NeurIPS | https://papers.nips.cc | papers.nips.cc | |
| ICML | https://proceedings.mlr.press | proceedings.mlr.press | Also includes AISTATS, COLT, UAI |
| ICLR | https://openreview.net | openreview.net | Always add "ICLR" to queries to reduce noise |
| CVPR/ICCV/ECCV | https://openaccess.thecvf.com | openaccess.thecvf.com | Vision architecture and augmentation papers |
